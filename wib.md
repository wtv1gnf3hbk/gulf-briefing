1. **US-Iran ceasefire extension.** US Vice President JD Vance said Washington and Tehran are "very close" to a preliminary agreement on a 60-day ceasefire extension, though talks continue over Iran's enriched uranium stockpile. Trump must still [approve the](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/us-iran-mou-60-day-ceasefire-extension-reached-trump-must-approve-source-says-2026-05-28/) final deal.

2. **Former Yemen president dies.** Former Yemeni president Abd Rabbuh Mansour Hadi died at age 80, according to AP News. The IRG-aligned General People's Congress mourned the former leader, who served during the country's transition period before being forced from power by [Houthi forces](https://apnews.com/article/yemen-expresident-abdrabbuh-mansour-hadi-died-b3afd7b3679af14d335578e9560c7cd8) in 2014.

3. **US warns Oman over Hormuz tolls.** The US Treasury warned Oman against facilitating tolls for Strait of Hormuz passage, according to Reuters. The move comes as regional tensions remain high over maritime [security in](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/us-warns-oman-not-engage-facilitating-tolls-strait-hormuz-2026-05-28/) the waterway.

4. **Netanyahu expands Gaza control.** Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu directed forces to expand control of Gaza to 70 percent, Reuters reported. The directive marks a significant escalation in Israel's military [operations in](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/netanyahu-directs-israeli-forces-expand-gaza-control-70-percent-2026-05-28/) the territory.

5. **Israel strikes Beirut suburbs.** Israel carried out its first airstrike on Beirut suburbs in weeks following large-scale evacuation orders for southern Lebanon. The BBC reported the strike represented an escalation after a [period of](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cj3pgrpmlklo?at_medium=RSS&amp;at_campaign=rss) relative calm.

6. **Iran attacks Kuwait.** Iran launched drone and missile attacks targeting Kuwait, prompting strong condemnation from Arab and Islamic states. The Organisation of Islamic Cooperation and Iraq both condemned the attacks, while Kuwait News Agency [reported the](https://ow.ly/4GQy30sW6ty) strikes violated sovereignty.

7. **Saudi Crown Prince meets Chad president.** Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman received Chad's President Mahamat Idriss Déby at the Royal Court in Mina Palace during Hajj season. They reviewed bilateral relations and discussed regional [issues of](https://spa.gov.sa/N2602420) common interest.

8. **Temperatures hit 47.5°C in Oman.** Oman recorded temperatures exceeding 47.5 degrees Celsius, according to Times of Oman. The extreme heat comes as the Gulf region experiences [seasonal temperature](https://timesofoman.com//article/172348-temperature-crosses-475-degrees-in-oman) peaks.

9. **UAE tourism achievements reviewed.** Sheikh Mohammed bin Rashid reviewed the UAE's tourism sector achievements for 2025, according to UAE media office. The review highlighted the country's continued focus on [economic diversification](https://twitter.com/UAEmediaoffice) through tourism.

10. **Hajj operations continue smoothly.** Saudi authorities reported efficient Hajj operations on the second day of Tashreeq, with pilgrims completing rituals at the Grand Mosque and Jamarat facility. Al Ekhbariya TV noted average circumambulation times of [45 minutes](https://twitter.com/alekhbariyatv) demonstrating organizational efficiency.
