1. **Iran attacks Kuwait.** [Iran targeted Kuwait](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/kuwaiti-army-says-air-defences-intercepting-hostile-missile-drone-attacks-2026-05-28/) with missile and drone strikes, prompting Kuwait to activate its air defenses and drawing widespread Arab condemnation including from Egypt and the Arab League. [Saudi Foreign Minister Prince Faisal](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/iran-us-trade-air-strikes-after-trump-dismisses-report-hormuz-deal-2026-05-28/) praised US President Trump's decision to give diplomacy a chance to end the conflict and restore Strait of Hormuz security.

2. **US-Iran ceasefire talks advance.** [Reports emerged](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/iran-us-trade-air-strikes-after-trump-dismisses-report-hormuz-deal-2026-05-28/) that Iran and the United States reached an outline ceasefire deal following recent air strikes, though Trump said the US was "not satisfied" with the agreement yet. The BBC reported that [US forces conducted new strikes](https://apnews.com/article/trump-iran-nuclear-cabinet-meeting-af77d581873bfeec32d7342b56841244) against Iranian military sites following Trump's comments that Iran was "negotiating on fumes."

3. **Former Yemen President Hadi dies.** [Field Marshal Abdrabbuh Mansour Hadi](https://apnews.com/article/yemen-expresident-abdrabbuh-mansour-hadi-died-b3afd7b3679af14d335578e9560c7cd8) died at age 80, according to AP reports. Hadi, who led the internationally recognized Yemeni government during much of the civil war, received tributes from IRG officials and Yemen's General People's Congress party for his "great fighter" status and patriotic stances.

4. **Saudi Crown Prince meets Chad President.** Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman [received Chad's President](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/iran-us-trade-air-strikes-after-trump-dismisses-report-hormuz-deal-2026-05-28/) at Mona Palace, discussing bilateral relations and cooperation prospects across various fields, according to Saudi Press Agency reports. The meeting occurred as part of Hajj season diplomatic activities, with the Crown Prince also hosting the annual reception for state dignitaries and pilgrims' affairs officials.

5. **Maritime security disrupted.** [Three oil and LNG tankers](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/three-oil-lng-tankers-exit-hormuz-with-transponders-off-2026-05-28/) exited the Strait of Hormuz with their transponders turned off, according to Reuters, indicating continued maritime tensions. This followed reports of Iranian naval forces conducting reconnaissance operations in the Red Sea, with US warships responding with "hysterical" fire that exploded near a Gabon-flagged vessel.

6. **Qatar mediates ceasefire efforts.** [Majed Al Ansari](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/iran-us-trade-air-strikes-after-trump-dismisses-report-hormuz-deal-2026-05-28/) dismissed reports that Qatar offered $12 billion to Iran to secure a deal, calling such claims false and designed to sabotage diplomatic efforts toward regional de-escalation. Trump separately spoke with Qatar's Emir, praising Doha's role in advancing calm according to Al Jazeera reports.

7. **Oman maintains diplomatic dialogue.** [Foreign Minister Badr Albusaidi](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/iran-us-trade-air-strikes-after-trump-dismisses-report-hormuz-deal-2026-05-28/) met with Iran's Deputy Foreign Minister, describing "good discussions" that reflected the constructive nature of Oman-Iran dialogue. He reaffirmed Oman's commitment to supporting efforts to reduce regional tensions and advance peaceful coexistence.

8. **Saudi Yemen envoy announces progress.** [Ambassador Mohammed AlJabir](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/iran-us-trade-air-strikes-after-trump-dismisses-report-hormuz-deal-2026-05-28/) stated he would visit Sanaa with an Omani delegation to consolidate the truce and ceasefire, support prisoner exchanges, and discuss dialogue between Yemeni components for a comprehensive political solution. He emphasized that nearly 2 million Yemenis work in Saudi Arabia, transferring over $4 billion annually to benefit their families.

9. **UAE advances AI initiatives.** [Sheikh Abdulla bin Zayed](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/iran-us-trade-air-strikes-after-trump-dismisses-report-hormuz-deal-2026-05-28/) announced that the UAE ranked first globally in artificial intelligence talent concentration, growing 121% between 2019 and 2025. [Sheikh Mohammed bin Rashid](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/iran-us-trade-air-strikes-after-trump-dismisses-report-hormuz-deal-2026-05-28/) launched new AI assistants for procurement, taxes, and customer services as part of the federal AI program.

10. **Hajj season continues smoothly.** [Saudi authorities](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/iran-us-trade-air-strikes-after-trump-dismisses-report-hormuz-deal-2026-05-28/) reported smooth movement and careful crowd organization as pilgrims continued arriving at Jamarat facility to perform rituals. Interior Minister Prince Abdulaziz bin Saud received counterparts from Jordan and Malaysia, while the Grand Mosque prepared to receive pilgrims for farewell circumambulation on the 12th and 13th days.
