**Gulf Bureau Brief - April 18, 2026**

1. **US-Iran talks.** The next round of US-Iran negotiations will likely be held in Pakistan on Monday, according to Iranian sources cited by [Times of Oman](https://timesofoman.com//article/170748-next-round-of-us-iran-talks-likely-to-be-held-in-pakistan-on-monday-iranian-sources). Saudi Foreign Minister Prince Faisal bin Farhan participated in a quadripartite ministerial meeting in Antalya with counterparts from Turkey, Pakistan, and Egypt, discussing support for Pakistani mediation efforts to reach a permanent ceasefire between Iran and the United States.

2. **Trump Gulf support.** President Donald Trump thanked Gulf states for their "tremendous support" during recent regional developments, according to [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/video/newsfeed/2026/4/18/trump-thanks-gulf-states-for-their-tremendous-support?traffic_source=rss). Trump separately announced Israel is barred from bombing Lebanon, saying "enough is enough," Reuters reported. The Lebanon ceasefire has been welcomed by Saudi Arabia and Bahrain as a positive step toward regional stability.

3. **Hormuz strait tensions.** Empty LNG tankers attempted crossings into the Persian Gulf through the Strait of Hormuz as Iran declared the waterway remains open, [Bloomberg reported](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-04-17/empty-lng-tankers-attempt-hormuz-crossing-into-persian-gulf). A Pakistani oil tanker made a rare entry and exit through Hormuz during the ongoing Iran war. The strait's security remains critical for global energy markets following 50 days of conflict.

4. **Middle East energy losses.** The loss of energy output in the Middle East will take approximately two years to recover, the International Energy Agency said, according to [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/loss-energy-output-mideast-will-take-about-two-years-recover-iea-says-2026-04-17/). The Iran war has led to $50 billion worth of oil losses over 50 days of conflict. Traffic and concerns in the Persian Gulf could prevent gasoline prices from dropping quickly, AP News reported.

5. **Saudi defense diplomacy.** Prince Khalid bin Salman met with defense ministers from Greece, Britain, and Hungary to discuss regional developments and condemn Iran's continued attacks on the Kingdom. The Saudi defense minister reviewed strategic partnerships and discussed the impact of Iranian aggression on global peace and security. Saudi Arabia continues to face what officials describe as "flagrant attacks" from Iran and regional proxies.

6. **Yemen ceasefire developments.** Yemen's internationally recognized government welcomed the Lebanon ceasefire announcement, with Foreign Minister Al-Eryani stating support for regional de-escalation. The Houthi negotiator Mohammed Abdulsalam announced an agreement to cancel recent banking decisions and resume Yemenia Airlines flights between Sanaa and Jordan, with increased frequency to three daily flights. Saudi ambassador Mohammed Al-Jabir indicated continued efforts to consolidate truces and support prisoner exchanges.

7. **UAE economic achievements.** The UAE entered the World Trade Organization's list of top ten merchandise exporters for the first time, advancing from 17th to 9th place globally in five years, Sheikh Mohammed bin Rashid announced on Twitter. Total foreign trade reached six trillion dirhams ($1.6 trillion) last year. The UAE also won the bid to host World Bank Group and International Monetary Fund meetings in Abu Dhabi for 2029.

8. **Russian oil waiver extension.** The US extended a waiver allowing the sale of sanctioned Russian oil following energy market turmoil, [Times of Oman reported](https://timesofoman.com//article/170745-us-extends-waiver-allowing-sale-of-sanctioned-russian-oil-amid-energy-market-turmoil). Senate Democrats criticized the Trump administration for extending the waiver. The decision comes as global energy markets face disruption from the ongoing Iran war and Middle East tensions.

9. **Hajj preparations.** Saudi Arabia's Ministry of Interior continues implementing the Makkah Road Initiative for the eighth consecutive year across 10 countries through 17 entry points. The first Pakistani pilgrims arrived in Medina under the program, with 155 pilgrims on the inaugural flight. The Ministry of Transport announced maximum readiness levels for the transportation system to serve pilgrims during the Hajj season.

10. **Qatar diplomatic activities.** Qatari Prime Minister Sheikh Mohammed bin Abdulrahman Al Thani participated in the Antalya Diplomatic Forum, emphasizing Qatar's mediation experience and conviction that crises are resolved through dialogue rather than battlefield confrontation. He met with Turkish President Erdogan and discussed regional challenges during a visit to Oman with Sultan Haitham bin Tariq, focusing on diplomatic solutions for regional stability.
