1. **Former Yemeni President dies.** Abd Rabbuh Mansour Hadi died, according to condolence messages from Saudi leadership. Prince Khalid bin Salman conveyed condolences from King Salman and Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman to Hadi's family, calling him "a wise leader who was keen on the security, stability, development and prosperity of his country." The IRG's Mohammed Al-Eryani also confirmed the death of the former president.

2. **Iran tightens Strait control.** Iran has imposed new transit restrictions through the Strait of Hormuz, requiring ships to use designated routes, [Times of Oman](https://timesofoman.com//article/172394-travel-through-designated-routes-iran-tightens-grip-on-crucial-strait-of-hormuz-transit) reported. The Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps also claimed to have downed a US MQ-1 drone in Iranian airspace.

3. **US-Iran talks continue.** Vice President JD Vance said the US and Iran are "very close" to a deal but "not there yet," according to the [BBC](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c87qng40wz9o). Foreign Minister Prince Faisal bin Farhan praised President Trump's decision to give diplomacy another chance to reach an agreement that would end the war and restore freedom of navigation in the Strait of Hormuz.

4. **Saudi Hajj season concludes.** Saudi officials celebrated the successful completion of the 1447 AH Hajj season, with pilgrims performing farewell circumambulation around the Kaaba. Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman received congratulations from regional leaders including Qatar's Emir, Palestine's President, and Kuwait's Emir on the smooth conduct of the pilgrimage.

5. **UAE economy grows strongly.** The UAE economy expanded 6.2% with GDP rising to AED 1.9 trillion in 2025, the government media office announced. Sheikh Mohammed bin Rashid reviewed tourism sector achievements for 2025 and launched new artificial intelligence assistants for government procurement, taxes, and customer services.

6. **Yemen's Al-Hudaydah liberated.** Saudi Yemen envoy Mohammed Al-Jabir declared "Al-Hudaydah is liberated" and "Yemen is breathing" in a tweet. He also announced plans to visit Sanaa with an Omani delegation to consolidate the truce and support prisoner exchanges between Yemeni factions.

7. **Israeli operations expand.** Netanyahu directed the IDF to increase control of Gaza to 70%, according to the [BBC](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/ceqpelq5reqo). Israel struck Beirut and southern Lebanon after issuing large-scale evacuation orders, while the UN added Israel to its sexual violence in warzones blacklist for the first time.

8. **Bahrain condemns Iranian attack.** Bahrain "strongly condemned" an Iranian attack on Kuwait that injured American soldiers when falling debris struck their base last Wednesday, according to [Arabic news sources](https://gate.ahram.org.eg/News/5664173.aspx). The Foreign Ministry called the incident a violation of Kuwaiti sovereignty.

9. **Qatar PM appointed.** Sheikh Mohammed bin Abdulrahman Al Thani expressed honor at being appointed Prime Minister by Emir Sheikh Tamim, saying he looks forward to serving the nation under the Emir's wise leadership. Majed Al Ansari was appointed as advisor and official spokesman for the Foreign Ministry.

10. **Saudi sports preparation.** Saudi Arabia's national football team played a friendly match against Ecuador (1-2 loss) in preparation for the 2026 World Cup. Al-Ekhbariya TV monitored expectations from Saudi fans in New Jersey ahead of the team's World Cup participation.
