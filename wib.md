1. **Yemen's former president died.** Abdrabbuh Mansour Hadi, 80, passed away after a political career marked by civil conflict and Houthi opposition. The internationally recognized government figure led Yemen during its transition period before being ousted by Ansar Allah forces in 2015. [AP News reported](https://apnews.com/article/yemen-expresident-abdrabbuh-mansour-hadi-died-b3afd7b3679af14d335578e9560c7cd8) his death, with condolences flowing from across the region.

2. **US-Iran ceasefire talks progressed.** American and Iranian negotiators reached a tentative 60-day agreement to reduce tensions, though implementation awaits Trump administration approval. The deal aims to restore Strait of Hormuz navigation security to pre-February 2026 conditions. [Reuters confirmed](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/iran-us-trade-air-strikes-after-trump-dismisses-report-hormuz-deal-2026-05-28/) the outline agreement following recent military exchanges between both nations.

3. **Trump warned Oman over Hormuz tolls.** The US administration cautioned Muscat against facilitating any Iranian toll collection scheme in the vital shipping lane. Treasury Secretary Bessent confirmed Oman provided assurances it would not support such measures. Multiple Arabic outlets including [Al Jazeera covered](https://www.aljazeera.net/news/2026/5/28/%D8%AA%D8%B1%D9%85%D8%A8-%D9%8A%D9%87%D8%AF%D8%AF-%D8%A8%D9%80%D9%86%D8%B3%D9%81-%D8%B9%D9%85%D8%A7%D9%86-%D8%A5%D8%B0%D8%A7-%D8%AF%D8%B9%D9%85%D8%AA-%D8%A5%D9%8A%D8%B1%D8%A7%D9%86) the diplomatic pressure on the sultanate.

4. **Saudi Crown Prince hosted Chad's leader.** MBS received President Mahamat Idriss Déby at Mina Palace during the Hajj season to discuss bilateral cooperation and regional issues. The meeting occurred as pilgrims completed their holy journey rituals. Prince Khalid bin Salman's Twitter account documented the high-level diplomatic engagement during Islam's holiest period.

5. **Saudi Foreign Minister praised Trump's diplomacy.** Prince Faisal bin Farhan welcomed the US president's decision to pursue negotiations over military action in the Gulf crisis. The kingdom expressed appreciation for extending diplomatic opportunities to resolve maritime security concerns. His official Twitter statement emphasized Saudi support for peaceful conflict resolution in regional disputes.

6. **Iraq condemned Kuwait attacks.** Baghdad rejected missile and drone strikes targeting its neighbor, calling for restraint and dialogue instead of military escalation. The Iraqi government emphasized regional security threats require diplomatic solutions rather than armed responses. [KUNA reported](http://ow.ly/nRv730sW6tK) Iraq's position condemning acts that destabilize Gulf security.

7. **UAE leaders marked national achievements.** Sheikh Mohammed bin Rashid reviewed the country's 2025 tourism sector performance during government meetings in Abu Dhabi. The leadership highlighted the UAE's global ranking as first in artificial intelligence talent concentration growth. WAM documented the strategic planning sessions focused on sustainable economic development.

8. **Bahrain rescued children from house fire.** Civil Defense forces evacuated five children from a blaze in the Aali area without casualties reported. Authorities launched investigations to determine the fire's cause while praising emergency responders' swift action. The Interior Ministry confirmed the successful rescue operation through its official channels.

9. **Qatar appointed new Prime Minister.** Sheikh Mohammed bin Abdulrahman Al Thani accepted Emir Tamim's nomination to lead the government while retaining his foreign affairs portfolio. His Twitter account expressed gratitude for the leadership's trust and commitment to national advancement. The appointment maintains continuity in Qatar's diplomatic and domestic policy directions.

10. **Oman Sultan performed Eid prayers.** Haitham bin Tarik led religious observances at Sultan Qaboos Mosque in Sohar, marking the holy celebration across the sultanate. [ONA English documented](http://n9.cl/adorv) the ceremonial prayers in North Al Batinah Governorate as citizens gathered for the blessed occasion.
