**Gulf Regional Briefing**

1. **Yemen leader's death.** Former President Abdrabbuh Mansour Hadi died at 80, according to [AP reports](https://apnews.com/article/yemen-expresident-abdrabbuh-mansour-hadi-died-b3afd7b3679af14d335578e9560c7cd8). The IRG leader, who governed mostly from Saudi exile after being ousted by Houthis in 2014, remained a symbolic figure for the internationally recognized government. His death leaves a leadership vacuum as Yemen's conflict continues.

2. **Saudi Hajj success.** Saudi Arabia declared the 1447 AH Hajj season successful with over 1.7 million pilgrims completing rituals. Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman received congratulations from regional leaders, according to Saudi Press Agency. The achievement reinforces the kingdom's custodianship of Islam's holiest sites as Vision 2030 modernization efforts continue.

3. **Trump-Iran diplomacy.** President Trump said the US is "not satisfied" with Iran nuclear negotiations, while Vice President Vance described talks as "very close" but "not there yet," [BBC reports](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c74dy9jw1q9o). Saudi Foreign Minister Prince Faisal praised Trump's decision to give diplomacy a chance to restore Strait of Hormuz security.

4. **Oman-US tensions.** Reuters reports the US [warned Oman](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/us-warns-oman-not-engage-facilitating-tolls-strait-hormuz-2026-05-28/) against facilitating Strait of Hormuz tolls. Omani Foreign Minister Badr Albusaidi met with Iran's Deputy FM, reflecting the sultanate's diplomatic balancing act between Washington and Tehran as regional tensions persist.

5. **Oil market pressures.** Saudi Arabia may [lower July prices](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/saudi-arabia-may-lower-july-oil-prices-asia-demand-weakens-2026-05-29/) to Asia as demand weakens, Reuters reports. Kuwait oil rose $4.56 to $118.35 per barrel. Japan's April oil imports hit their lowest since 1962 due to Iran war supply disruptions, highlighting regional conflict's global energy impact.

6. **Iran regional strikes.** Iran said it targeted an American base after fresh US strikes, [BBC reports](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c98r2qy5809o). The escalating military exchanges between US and Iranian forces threaten to widen the regional conflict beyond current boundaries, affecting Gulf shipping and energy security.

7. **UAE AI leadership.** Sheikh Abdullah bin Zayed announced the UAE ranked first globally in AI talent concentration, growing 121% between 2019-2025. The achievement reflects the Emirates' strategy to position itself as a global technology hub under its economic diversification plans.

8. **Bahrain condemns Iran.** Bahrain's Foreign Ministry [strongly condemned](https://bit.ly/4dAMr6j) Iranian attacks on Kuwait. The statement reflects growing Gulf Cooperation Council unity against Iranian regional activities as member states coordinate their security responses.

9. **Qatar diplomatic appointments.** Prime Minister Mohammed bin Abdulrahman Al Thani appointed Majed Al Ansari as adviser and official Foreign Ministry spokesperson. The reshuffle strengthens Qatar's diplomatic communications apparatus during ongoing regional mediation efforts.

10. **Regional Eid celebrations.** Gulf leaders exchanged Eid Al-Adha greetings as the Islamic holiday concluded. Sultan Haitham offered prayers in Sohar, while UAE and other GCC rulers received well-wishers, demonstrating continued religious and cultural unity across the region despite political tensions.
