**Gulf Daily Briefing**

1. **Yemen prisoner exchange.** The Yemeni government (IRG) and Ansar Allah/Houthis agreed to cancel recent banking restrictions and resume Yemenia Airlines flights between Sanaa and Jordan. The deal includes increasing daily flights to three and operating routes to Cairo and India. This represents one of the most significant prisoner-exchange breakthroughs in the Yemen conflict, per [Houthi negotiator Mohammed Abdulsalam](https://www.reuters.com/markets/commodities/asias-imports-us-crude-surge-cant-offset-hormuz-losses-2026-06-02/).

2. **US-Iran confrontation escalates.** American forces bombed Iranian military sites after Tehran fired missiles at US troops stationed in Kuwait, according to AP News. Secretary of State Marco Rubio confirmed the US has not offered Iran sanctions relief to reopen the Strait of Hormuz. Iran subsequently stopped communicating with mediators over Israel's Lebanon operations, per [AP reporting](https://apnews.com/article/iran-us-irael-war-kuwait-strikes-88daa9f90b48baaa7beb18e35515c59d).

3. **Former Yemeni president dies.** Abd Rabbuh Mansour Hadi, Yemen's former president and key ally of the Saudi-led coalition, passed away. Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman and the Saudi leadership conveyed condolences to Hadi's family, while Prince Khalid bin Salman praised him as "a wise leader keen on his country's security and development." The IRG government and international partners [mourned his death](https://spa.gov.sa/N2604388).

4. **Saudi drug bust.** Narcotics Control seized 845,087 amphetamine tablets hidden inside cardboard sheets in the Eastern Region, the Saudi Interior Ministry announced. This represents one of the largest recent drug seizures in the Kingdom. Authorities also [arrested 18 environmental law violators](https://timesofoman.com//article/172508-workshop-to-enhance-statistical-and-informational-knowledge-organised) across multiple regions within a week.

5. **Arab nations condemn Al-Aqsa raids.** Foreign ministers from Saudi Arabia, UAE, Qatar, Jordan, Turkey, Egypt, Indonesia, and Pakistan issued a joint statement condemning Israeli settler incursions into Al-Aqsa Mosque. The ministers reaffirmed support for Palestinian self-determination and an independent Palestinian state on 1967 borders with East Jerusalem as capital, according to [Qatar News Agency](https://www.aljazeera.net/news/2026/6/2/%D9%88%D8%B2%D9%8A%D8%B1-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AF%D9%88%D9%84%D8%A9-%D9%84%D8%B4%D8%A4%D9%88%D9%86-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AF%D9%81%D8%A7%D8%B9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%82%D8%B7%D8%B1%D9%8A-%D9%8A%D9%84%D8%AA%D9%82%D9%8A).

6. **Kuwait condemns Iranian attacks.** Kuwait's Cabinet strongly condemned repeated Iranian missile and drone attacks targeting the country's territory, describing them as dangerous escalation and sovereignty violation. Bahrain's Foreign Ministry separately condemned Iran's targeting of Kuwait, while Bahrain's Interior Ministry banned citizens from traveling to Iran and Iraq due to [ongoing security tensions](https://kuna.net.kw/ArticleDetails.aspx?id=3306224).

7. **Oman-UK royal diplomacy.** Prince William received Omani Culture Minister Sayyid Theyazin bin Haitham Al Said at Windsor Castle to review bilateral relations. The meeting launched the "Oman: The Jewel of Arabia" expedition project, reflecting strengthened ties between the sultanate and Britain. Sultan Haitham separately departed for a three-day [state visit to Belgium](https://timesofoman.com//article/172507-transport-ministry-launches-sas-for-excellence-initiative).

8. **UAE AI leadership.** The UAE ranked first globally in artificial intelligence talent concentration, growing 121% between 2019 and 2025, according to government data. Dubai separately ranked first regionally and 12th internationally in a "Cities Shaping the Future" report. The UAE launched its first batch of AI assistants in procurement, taxation, and customer services as part of a broader [digital transformation initiative](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/iran-war-hands-syria-windfall-airlines-reroute-over-its-airspace-2026-06-01/).

9. **Qatar PM appointment.** Emir Tamim bin Hamad Al Thani appointed Mohammed bin Abdulrahman Al Thani as Prime Minister, retaining his Foreign Minister role. Al Thani expressed gratitude for the trust and commitment to serving Qatar under the Emir's leadership. The appointment maintains continuity in Qatar's foreign policy approach, particularly regarding [regional mediation efforts](https://www.reuters.com/world/us/us-lawmakers-grill-rubio-iran-war-enters-fourth-month-2026-06-02/).

10. **Saudi Hajj preparations.** The Kingdom successfully concluded this year's Hajj season, with nearly 2 million pilgrims completing their rituals. Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman and King Salman congratulated security forces and government sectors for their service. The Saudi Interior Ministry highlighted that serving pilgrims remains an "established national priority and consistent Saudi approach" following completion of the [annual pilgrimage](https://timesofoman.com//article/172504-rop-invites-people-to-join-whatsapp-channel-for-better-communication).
