1. **Saudi Hajj success.** Saudi Arabia declared the 2026 Hajj season a success, with authorities providing 2.5 million health services to pilgrims. Prince Khalid bin Salman conveyed condolences for former Yemeni President Abd Rabbuh Mansour Hadi's death, praising him as a [wise leader](https://spa.gov.sa/N2603513) who prioritized Yemen's security and stability.

2. **Iran nuclear talks.** President Trump met with advisors for a "final determination" on Iran nuclear negotiations but announced no deal. Vice President Vance said the US and Iran are "very close" to an agreement but [not there](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c87qng40wz9o) yet, while Saudi Foreign Minister Prince Faisal praised Trump's decision to give diplomacy additional time.

3. **Yemen former president dies.** Former Yemeni President Abd Rabbuh Mansour Hadi died, with Saudi Defense Minister Prince Khalid bin Salman conveying King Salman's condolences. The IRG government confirmed his death through official channels, with Mohammed Al-Eryani describing Hadi as committed to [Yemen's stability](https://spa.gov.sa/N2603513) and development.

4. **Oman maritime security.** Oman's Maritime Security Centre detected a suspected floating mine in the Strait of Hormuz, according to Times of Oman. The discovery highlights ongoing [security concerns](https://timesofoman.com//article/172387-omans-maritime-security-centre-detects-suspected-floating-mine-in-strait-of-hormuz) in the strategically vital waterway that carries significant global oil shipments.

5. **UAE economic growth.** The UAE economy recorded 6.2% growth in 2025, reaching 1.9 trillion dirhams, according to Mubasher Info. Sheikh Abdullah bin Zayed highlighted the UAE's global leadership in artificial intelligence talent concentration, which [grew 121%](https://twitter.com/ABZayed) between 2019 and 2025.

6. **Oman lithium project.** Oman launched a strategic project to produce lithium battery materials, per Arabic media reports. The initiative represents the sultanate's efforts to diversify its economy and capitalize on the global transition to [renewable energy](https://www.alarabiya.net/amp/aswaq/economy/2026/05/30) and electric vehicle technologies.

7. **Qatar PM appointment.** Mohammed bin Abdulrahman Al Thani was appointed Qatar's new Prime Minister by Emir Tamim bin Hamad. Al Thani expressed gratitude for the trust placed in him and pledged to work with fellow ministers in [serving Qatar](https://twitter.com/MBA_AlThani_) under the Emir's wise leadership.

8. **Trump Oman statement.** President Trump made controversial statements about Oman, prompting strong reactions from Omani officials. Times of Oman described Trump's remarks as "not a passing mistake" but a "historical sin," reflecting [diplomatic tensions](https://timesofoman.com//article/172388-president-donald-trump-your-statement-against-oman-is-not-a-passing-mistake-it-is-a-historical-sin) between Washington and Muscat.

9. **Yemen Houthi operations.** Houthi naval forces conducted operations in the Red Sea, with spokesman Mohammed Abdulsalam stating their actions support Palestinians in Gaza. The group announced agreements on banking procedures and flight resumptions while maintaining their [naval operations](https://twitter.com/abdusalamsalah) continue for Palestinian solidarity.

10. **Bahrain civil defense.** Bahrain's Civil Defense rescued five children from a house fire in Aali area without casualties. The ministry reported investigations are underway to determine the fire's cause, demonstrating the kingdom's emergency response [capabilities](https://policemc.gov.bh/en/news/78966/).
