1. **Former Yemen President Hadi dies.** Abdrabbuh Mansour Hadi, former president of the internationally recognized Yemeni government, died at age 80, [AP News reported](https://apnews.com/article/yemen-expresident-abdrabbuh-mansour-hadi-died-b3afd7b3679af14d335578e9560c7cd8). Hadi served as president from 2012 to 2022 following the Arab Spring uprising. IRG Information Minister Mohammed Al-Eryani mourned "the great fighter Field Marshal Abd Rabbuh Mansour Hadi" in a statement.

2. **Iran attacks Kuwait with missiles, drones.** Kuwait said its air defenses intercepted Iranian missile and drone attacks, [Reuters reported](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/kuwaiti-army-says-air-defences-intercepting-hostile-missile-drone-attacks-2026-05-28/). Arab League chief strongly condemned the Iranian assault, per KUNA, as Egypt and other Arab states expressed solidarity with Kuwait. The UAE Foreign Ministry received a call from Kuwait's foreign minister regarding the condemnation of Iran's attack.

3. **US launches fresh strikes on Iran.** The US conducted new strikes against Iranian military sites and drones following previous escalation, [Reuters reported exclusively](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/us-carries-out-new-strikes-iran-against-military-site-official-says-2026-05-27/). Iran said it targeted an American base after the fresh US strikes, according to BBC coverage. Three oil and LNG tankers exited the Strait of Hormuz with transponders turned off.

4. **Iran-US ceasefire talks continue.** Reports emerged of a tentative US-Iran deal to extend ceasefire and begin nuclear talks, though Trump said the US is "not satisfied" with the Iran deal yet, per BBC reporting. Saudi Foreign Minister Prince Faisal bin Farhan praised Trump's decision to "give diplomacy a chance" to restore Strait of Hormuz security. Qatar denied reports it offered $12 billion to Iran for a deal.

5. **Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman meets Chad leader.** Saudi Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman received Chad's President at Mina Palace to review bilateral relations and cooperation prospects, Saudi Press Agency reported. Prince Khalid bin Salman met with UK National Security Adviser Jonathan Powell to discuss Saudi-British cooperation and regional de-escalation efforts. Interior Minister Prince Abdulaziz bin Saud received Malaysia's Interior Minister during Hajj season.

6. **Mohammed AlJabir announces Yemen visit.** Saudi Yemen envoy Mohammed AlJabir said he will [visit Sanaa](https://twitter.com/mohdsalj) with an Omani delegation to consolidate the truce, support prisoner exchanges, and discuss dialogue between Yemeni factions. AlJabir noted nearly 2 million Yemenis work in Saudi Arabia, transferring over $4 billion annually to families in Yemen. He declared "Al-Hudaydah is liberated" and "Ma'rib War is in the hands of the Yemeni state."

7. **UAE showcases AI achievements.** Sheikh Abdulla bin Zayed highlighted that the UAE ranks first globally in artificial intelligence talent concentration, growing 121% between 2019 and 2025. Sheikh Mohammed bin Rashid launched new AI assistants for procurement, taxes, and customer service during a government retreat. The UAE announced restoration of Damascus' Umayyad Mosque with support from Sheikha Fatima bint Mubarak.

8. **Qatar appoints new Prime Minister.** Sheikh Mohammed bin Abdulrahman Al Thani [announced his appointment](https://twitter.com/MBA_AlThani_) as Qatar's Prime Minister by Emir Sheikh Tamim. Majed Al Ansari was appointed as advisor and official spokesman for the Foreign Ministry. Both officials extended Eid Al-Adha congratulations to the Qatari people and Islamic nations.

9. **Oman celebrates Eid with diplomatic meetings.** Sultan Haitham performed Eid prayers at Sultan Qaboos Mosque in Sohar, per Oman News Agency. Prince William received Sayyid Theyazin bin Haitham at Windsor Castle to discuss UK-Oman relations. Foreign Minister Badr Albusaidi met with Iran's Deputy FM, emphasizing Oman's role in supporting diplomatic engagement and regional de-escalation.

10. **Southern Yemen leadership reshuffles.** The IRG appointed Ahmed Hamid Lamlas as governor of Aden Governorate, according to Saba News Agency. STC President Aidarous al-Zubaidi performed Umrah rituals while Amr Bidh attended a European Council meeting in London to discuss southern Yemen developments. Abu Zaraa announced Starlink satellite internet activation as an important step for Yemen's development and connectivity.
