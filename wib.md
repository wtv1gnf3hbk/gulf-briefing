**World in Brief: Gulf States - June 2, 2026**

1. **US-Iran strikes.** The United States struck Iranian radar installations after Tehran launched missiles at American forces based in Kuwait, according to [the BBC](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/crlpy8n7pr6o). Iran also targeted Kuwait with what Bahrain described as "hostile Iranian drones," prompting strong condemnation from Gulf states.

2. **OPEC+ production increase.** The organization will likely raise July oil output targets despite ongoing disruptions in the Strait of Hormuz, [Reuters reports](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/opec-likely-raise-july-oil-output-target-despite-hormuz-disruption-sources-say-2026-06-01/). Asian countries have increased imports of US crude but cannot fully offset losses from regional shipping constraints.

3. **Yemen president dies.** Former President Abd Rabbuh Mansour Hadi passed away, with Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman offering condolences to the family, per official Saudi statements. Prince Khalid bin Salman called him "a wise leader who was keen on the security, stability, development and prosperity of his country."

4. **Hajj season concludes.** Saudi Arabia completed another successful pilgrimage season, with Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman congratulating security forces and [participating sectors](https://www.reuters.com/pictures/journey-mecca-scenes-annual-hajj-pilgrimage-2026-06-01/). Officials reported minimal wait times at departure facilities, with 735 Sudanese pilgrims among those departing through Jeddah.

5. **Kuwait prisoners escape.** Three inmates, including two sentenced to death and one drug trafficker, escaped from custody in Kuwait but were quickly [recaptured](https://www.independentarabia.com/node/650268/). The incident prompted security reviews of detention facilities.

6. **Bahrain travel ban.** The kingdom prohibited citizens from traveling to Iran and Iraq due to "ongoing security tensions," according to the Interior Ministry. The ban reflects regional security concerns following recent Iranian attacks on Kuwait.

7. **Saudi diplomatic moves.** Foreign Minister Faisal bin Farhan chaired an international conference in Paris on Palestinian statehood with his French counterpart. He praised France's commitment to recognizing Palestine and called for wider international recognition.

8. **Oman-UK expedition.** Prince William received Omani Minister of Culture Sayyid Theyazin bin Haitham at Windsor Castle to launch the "Oman: The Jewel of Arabia" [expedition project](https://timesofoman.com//article/172495-environment-authority-participates-in-eco-expo-central-asia-2026-in-samarkand). The initiative strengthens cultural ties between the two nations.

9. **Qatar business leadership.** Prime Minister Mohammed bin Abdulrahman Al Thani announced his appointment to the role, expressing gratitude for Emir Tamim's confidence. He pledged to work with fellow ministers in serving Qatar's development under the Emir's leadership.

10. **UAE AI advancement.** The country ranks first globally in artificial intelligence talent concentration, showing 121% growth between 2019 and 2025, according to government communications. Officials described this as reflecting leadership vision in building human capital and creating future opportunities.
