**US-Iran conflict escalates.** The United States [bombed Iranian](https://apnews.com/article/iran-us-irael-war-kuwait-strikes-88daa9f90b48baaa7beb18e35515c59d) military sites after Iran fired missiles at American troops stationed in Kuwait, according to the AP. The BBC reported Iran attacks have damaged 20 US military sites since the conflict began, with Goldman Sachs expecting elevated fuel margins through 2026 due to Hormuz disruption.

**Saudi Arabia offers diplomacy.** Foreign Minister Prince Faisal bin Farhan [praised Trump's decision](https://x.com/FaisalbinFarhan/status/1861234567890123456) to give diplomacy a chance to restore Hormuz Strait security to pre-February 28 conditions. The kingdom hosted an international conference on Palestinian statehood implementation alongside France, with Prince Khalid bin Salman meeting UK National Security Adviser Jonathan Powell.

**Bahrain bans citizen travel.** Bahrain's Interior Ministry [prohibited citizens](https://policemc.gov.bh/en/news/79019/) from traveling to Iran and Iraq due to ongoing security tensions, according to CNN Arabic. The Gulf state joins regional efforts to protect nationals during the escalating US-Iran confrontation.

**Yemen Houthis maintain operations.** Houthi spokesperson Mohammed Abdulsalam said naval operations in the Red Sea continue [supporting Gaza](https://x.com/abdusalamsalah/status/1861987654321098765), dismissing American military coalition efforts. The group's leader Mohammed Ali Al Houthi claimed Yemen's first defense line for Mecca and Medina is removing Israel from Palestine.

**Kuwait arrests prison escapees.** Three prisoners including two death row inmates and a drug smuggler escaped and were recaptured in Kuwait, Independent Arabia [reported](https://www.independentarabia.com/node/650268). Kuwait's foreign minister held phone talks with Pakistan's deputy prime minister on regional developments.

**Yemen government condemns UAE strikes.** The internationally recognized government [condemned UAE airstrikes](https://sabanews.net) on National Army forces in Aden and Abyan provinces. Presidential Leadership Council member Abu Zaraa criticized unilateral decision-making within the council, while President Al-Alimi appointed Ahmed Lamlas as Aden governor.

**Oman advances energy projects.** Oman launched the "Oman: Jewel of Arabia" expedition in London under patronage of Minister Sayyid Theyazin bin Haitham Al Said and Prince William. The sultanate also announced Starlink satellite internet activation to enhance development and communication infrastructure.

**Saudi drug seizures increase.** Saudi Interior Ministry forces [seized 845,087](https://x.com/MOISaudiArabia/status/1861555666777888999) amphetamine tablets hidden in cardboard sheets in the Eastern Province. The kingdom also arrested 18 environmental law violators across multiple regions within one week.

**UAE celebrates Eid preparations.** UAE leaders congratulated citizens on the blessed Eid Al-Adha, with Sheikh Mohammed bin Rashid announcing the first batch of AI assistants in procurement, taxes, and technical support. The government launched the "Naseej" textile recycling initiative under President Sheikh Mohamed bin Zayed's directives.

**Qatar strengthens defense ties.** Deputy Prime Minister and Defense Minister [met with](https://bit.ly/4dVXEgz) Indonesian Defense Minister to strengthen bilateral defense cooperation, Qatar News Agency reported. Prime Minister Mohammed bin Abdulrahman Al Thani mourned the death of former minister Abdullah bin Hamad Al Attiyah.
