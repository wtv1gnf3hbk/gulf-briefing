**World in Brief**

1. **US-Iran ceasefire violated.** Iran's Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps [struck Saudi](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/saudi-arabias-east-west-oil-pipeline-hit-iranian-attack-damage-being-assessed-2026-04-08/) Arabia's East-West oil pipeline and facilities at Yanbu hours after announcing a ceasefire with the United States, according to Reuters. The IRGC also launched 17 ballistic missiles and 35 drones at the UAE on April 8, which Emirati air defenses intercepted, the UAE Defense Ministry reported.

2. **Israel expands Lebanon strikes.** Israeli forces killed at least 182 people across Lebanon in a large wave of strikes, the [BBC reports](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c0j6d538l6qo?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss), threatening the fragile US-Iran ceasefire. US Vice President JD Vance said Lebanon was not part of the truce agreement, while Iran maintained it was included in the deal.

3. **Strait of Hormuz blocked again.** Iran closed the Strait of Hormuz for a second time following Israeli operations in Lebanon, according to the [Times of Oman](https://timesofoman.com//article/170444-israels-offensive-on-hezbollah-puts-ceasefire-in-danger-iran-blocks-hormuz-again). Chinese tankers joined queues to test passage through the strait following the US-Iran ceasefire announcement, Bloomberg reported.

4. **Gulf states seek Ukrainian drones.** Gulf nations are exploring purchases of cheap Ukrainian interceptor drones as Iranian attacks deplete their expensive missile defense stocks, [Reuters reports](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/gulf-states-eye-cheap-ukrainian-interceptor-drone-iranian-attacks-drain-missile-2026-04-08/). Saudi defenses intercepted 9 drones and 5 missiles on Wednesday alone, according to Al Ekhbariya TV.

5. **Pakistan brokered Iran truce.** Pakistan's Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif secured the US-Iran ceasefire through last-ditch diplomatic efforts when "talks were almost dead," [Reuters reports](https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/talks-were-almost-dead-pakistans-last-ditch-effort-secure-iran-war-truce-2026-04-08/). Sharif termed Pakistan's mediation a "Historic Moment of Honour" and vowed continued efforts for regional stability.

7. **Bankers eye UAE return.** International bankers began considering returning to the UAE within hours of the US-Iran ceasefire announcement, [Bloomberg reports](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-04-08/bankers-start-weighing-uae-return-hours-after-us-iran-ceasefire). Many financial institutions had relocated staff during the conflict as the region became a theater of operations.

8. **US casualties disclosed.** The Pentagon reported that 140 US troops were injured in the Iran conflict, with 8 severely wounded, according to [AP News](https://apnews.com/article/iran-israel-us-latest-march-10-2026-be298023201a3e4b709b11372aa6e045). The figure represents the first official casualty count since fighting began between US and Iranian forces.

9. **Qatar track meet postponed.** Qatar's Diamond League athletics meeting was moved from May to June following ongoing regional uncertainty, [AP News](https://apnews.com/article/qatar-diamond-league-track-iran-6ae3c9622660d82a983f2abfc86b47e4) reported. The postponement reflects continued concern over the security situation despite ceasefire announcements.

10. **Exxon production disrupted.** ExxonMobil expects 6% of its global production to be disrupted in the first quarter due to the Iran conflict, according to [Asharq Business](https://asharqbusiness.com/companies/127505/%D8%A5%D9%83%D8%B3%D9%88%D9%86-%D8%AA%D8%AA%D9%88%D9%82%D8%B9-%D8%AA%D8%B9%D8%B7%D9%84-6-%D8%A8%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%A6%D8%A9-%D9%85%D9%86-%D8%A5%D9%86%D8%AA%D8%A7%D8%AC%D9%87%D8%A7-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D9%8A-%D8%A8%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B1%D8%A8%D8%B9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A3%D9%88%D9%84/). The energy giant cited regional instability and shipping disruptions as primary factors affecting operations.
