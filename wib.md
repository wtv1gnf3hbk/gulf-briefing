**Gulf Brief - Friday, April 3, 2026**

1. **UAE missile defense.** The UAE intercepted 19 ballistic missiles and 26 drones launched from Iran on April 2, according to the Defense Ministry. Since the conflict began, UAE air defenses have [engaged 457](https://twitter.com/modgovae) ballistic missiles from Iranian attacks. Iran targeted Bahrain's Amazon distribution center for the second time, per Arabic media reports.

2. **Saudi defense meetings.** Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman held calls with Russian President Putin and met with defense ministers from Greece, Britain, and Hungary this week. Prince Khalid bin Salman condemned Iran's "continued flagrant attacks on the Kingdom and other regional states" during the [defense meetings](https://twitter.com/kbsalsaud), discussing regional security implications.

3. **Hormuz Strait diplomacy.** The UAE asked the UN to approve measures, including force, to reopen the Strait of Hormuz, Bloomberg reported. Bahrain hopes for a UN Security Council vote on a draft resolution regarding the strait, according to Asharq media. Saudi Deputy Foreign Minister Walid Al-Khereiji participated in a UK-chaired 35-country meeting on [Hormuz reopening](https://twitter.com/KSAMOFA).

4. **OPEC production talks.** OPEC+ will likely weigh further oil output increases at Sunday's meeting, Reuters sources said. The potential production hike comes as global energy markets face disruption from the Iran conflict and [Hormuz concerns](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/opec-likely-weigh-further-oil-output-hike-sunday-sources-say-2026-04-02/).

5. **SpaceX Saudi talks.** SpaceX held discussions with Saudi Arabia's Public Investment Fund about a possible $5 billion investment in the company's planned IPO, according to Reuters sources. The talks represent potential Saudi involvement in one of the world's most valuable [private companies](https://www.reuters.com/business/finance/spacex-has-held-talks-with-saudi-fund-possible-5-billion-investment-ipo-sources-2026-04-02/).

6. **Yemen prisoner talks.** Saudi Yemen envoy Mohammed Al-Jabir announced plans to visit Sanaa with an Omani delegation to consolidate the ceasefire and support prisoner exchanges. Al-Jabir emphasized that nearly 2 million Yemenis work in Saudi Arabia, transferring over $4 billion annually to [support families](https://twitter.com/mohdsalj) in Yemen.

7. **Palestinian conference.** Saudi Foreign Minister Prince Faisal bin Farhan co-chaired an international conference in Paris with French counterpart Jean-Noel Barrot on implementing the two-state solution. The prince commended France's resolve to recognize Palestine, saying it [bolsters peace](https://twitter.com/FaisalbinFarhan) efforts in the region.

8. **Iran steel shutdown.** Iran's two largest steel plants shut down due to air strikes, companies told the BBC. The closures highlight the expanding economic impact of the conflict on Iranian industrial infrastructure, following weeks of [escalating attacks](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c1w4pdexxqpo) across the region.

9. **Bahrain air defense.** Bahrain sounded air raid sirens multiple times, with the Interior Ministry urging citizens to remain calm and seek safe shelter. The kingdom's Foreign Ministry is pushing for international action to protect navigation through the [Strait of](https://twitter.com/bahdiplomatic) Hormuz.

10. **Qatar government reshuffle.** Qatar's Emir Sheikh Tamim appointed Mohammed bin Abdulrahman Al Thani as Prime Minister, replacing Sheikh Khalid bin Khalifa. The new PM discussed regional escalation with UAE's Sheikh Mohammed bin Zayed and Saudi Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman, emphasizing [coordinated responses](https://twitter.com/MBA_AlThani_) to security threats.
