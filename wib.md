**GULF BRIEFING | May 30, 2026**

1. **U.S.-Iran ceasefire talks.** The US and Iran appeared close to a tentative deal extending the current ceasefire and beginning new nuclear negotiations, according to [AP News](https://apnews.com/article/iran-us-war-ceasefire-nuclear-talks-cac5206df0f0c7b79fe9321c08d63096). Vice President Vance said the sides were "very close" but "not there yet," per the [BBC](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c87qng40wz9o). Trump said he was not yet satisfied with the emerging agreement.

2. **Strait of Hormuz security.** Oman and Iran reaffirmed their commitment to securing the Strait of Hormuz during discussions between their foreign ministers, [Times of Oman](https://timesofoman.com//article/172358-oman-and-iran-reaffirm-commitment-to-strait-of-hormuz-security) reported. Oman's Foreign Minister Badr Albusaidi met with Iran's Deputy FM, reflecting the "constructive nature of Oman-Iran dialogue," he said on [Twitter](https://twitter.com/badralbusaidi).

3. **Saudi Hajj completion.** Saudi Arabia successfully concluded the 1447 AH Hajj season, with officials from across the Gulf congratulating King Salman and Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman. Qatar's Amir, Kuwait's Emir, and Oman's Sultan all sent congratulatory messages, according to [Saudi Press Agency](https://spa.gov.sa) posts. Prince Khalid bin Salman thanked God for "honoring us with serving the Two Holy Mosques."

4. **Saudi oil pricing.** Saudi Arabia may lower its July oil prices to Asia as demand weakens, [Reuters reported](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/saudi-arabia-may-lower-july-oil-prices-asia-demand-weakens-2026-05-29/). The potential price cuts reflect broader market pressures, with Japan's April oil imports hitting their lowest level since 1962 due to Iran war disruptions.

5. **Yemen government changes.** Yemen's internationally recognized government appointed Ahmed Hamid Lamlas as the new governor of Aden Governorate, according to the [IRG's Saba News Agency](https://sabanew.net). The appointment comes as the Presidential Leadership Council called for unity following recent political tensions within the coalition.

6. **Trump-Oman tensions.** Trump threatened to target Oman, describing it as his "ally," according to [DW Arabic](https://www.dw.com/ar/%D8%AA%D8%B1%D8%A7%D9%85%D8%A8-%D9%8A%D9%87%D8%AF%D8%AF-%D8%A8%D9%80%D9%86%D8%B3%D9%81-%D8%AD%D9%84%D9%8A%D9%81%D8%AA%D9%87-%D8%B3%D9%84%D8%B7%D9%86%D8%A9-%D8%B9%D9%85%D8%A7%D9%86-%D9%85%D8%A7%D8%B0%D8%A7-%D9%88%D8%B1%D8%A7%D8%A1-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A3%D9%83%D9%85%D8%A9/a-77337688). The threat prompted the first direct contact between Iran's and Oman's foreign ministers following Trump's comments, [CNN Arabic](https://arabic.cnn.com/middle-east/article/2026/05/29/first-contact-between-foreign-ministers-of-iran-and-oman-trumps-threat) reported.

7. **Saudi-Yemeni coordination.** Saudi Yemen envoy Mohammed AlJabir announced plans to visit Sanaa with an Omani delegation to consolidate the truce, support prisoner exchanges, and discuss dialogue between Yemeni factions. "Nearly 2 million Yemenis work in the Kingdom," he noted on [Twitter](https://twitter.com/mohdsalj), transferring over $4 billion annually to Yemen.

8. **UAE AI leadership.** The UAE ranked first globally in artificial intelligence talent concentration, growing 121% between 2019 and 2025, according to Sheikh Abdullah bin Zayed on [Twitter](https://twitter.com/ABZayed). "The UAE does not wait for the future, but rather creates it," he said, citing the achievement as evidence of the leadership's vision.

9. **Qatar cabinet appointments.** Majed Al Ansari was appointed advisor to Qatar's Deputy Prime Minister and official spokesman for the Foreign Ministry, following the appointment of Mohammed bin Abdulrahman Al Thani as Prime Minister. Al Ansari expressed honor at the appointment on [Twitter](https://twitter.com/majedalansari).

10. **Southern Yemen politics.** The Southern Transitional Council's President Aidarous al-Zubaidi performed Umrah rituals, while STC officials continued political coordination efforts. Amr Bidh emphasized that "political agreements will not stand in the way of the lives and livelihoods of citizens" on [Twitter](https://twitter.com/AmrAlBidh).
