**GULF BRIEFING**
*Monday, June 2, 2026*

1. **US-Iran military escalation.** The US [bombed Iranian](https://apnews.com/article/iran-us-irael-war-kuwait-strikes-88daa9f90b48baaa7beb18e35515c59d) military sites following Tehran's missile attacks on American forces in Kuwait. Sirens sounded over Kuwait City as air defense systems intercepted incoming projectiles, according to AP News reports. The Pentagon confirmed strikes on Iranian radar installations while Iran claimed its operations targeted US bases housing troops supporting Israel.

2. **Former Yemen president dies.** Abd Rabbuh Mansour Hadi, former president of Yemen, died, prompting condolences from across the region. Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman called his son to offer sympathies, while Saudi Defense Minister Prince Khalid bin Salman [conveyed condolences](https://spa.gov.sa/N2603513) from King Salman and the Crown Prince to the family.

3. **OPEC+ likely to raise output.** OPEC+ is expected to [increase July](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/opec-likely-raise-july-oil-output-target-despite-hormuz-disruption-sources-say-2026-06-01/) oil production targets despite ongoing Strait of Hormuz disruptions, sources told Reuters. Saudi Aramco raised LPG official selling prices by up to 3% for June shipments while Algeria's Sonatrach cut prices by 18-31%.

4. **Saudi condemns Iran attacks on Kuwait.** The Saudi Foreign Ministry issued strong condemnation of repeated Iranian attacks on Kuwait, calling them "sinful" assaults on the sisterly state. Prince Faisal bin Farhan praised President Trump's decision to [give diplomacy](https://x.com/FaisalbinFarhan) a chance to restore maritime navigation security in the Strait of Hormuz.

5. **Hajj season concludes successfully.** Saudi Arabia completed another successful Hajj season with millions of pilgrims. Prince Khalid bin Salman thanked God for honoring the Kingdom with serving the Two Holy Mosques and [caring for](https://x.com/kbsalsaud) pilgrims, asking for acceptance of their rituals and obedience.

6. **OQ expands in Libya.** Oman's national oil company OQ signed an [energy pact](https://timesofoman.com//article/172479-oq-expands-international-footprint-with-libyan-energy-pact) with Libya, expanding its international footprint. The deal represents OQ's growing presence beyond traditional Gulf markets into North African energy sectors.

7. **UAE AI leadership highlighted.** The UAE ranks first globally in artificial intelligence talent concentration growth, rising 121% between 2019 and 2025, according to Foreign Minister Abdullah bin Zayed. The statistic reflects the Emirates' [vision of](https://x.com/ABZayed) building people and attracting minds as core national policy.

8. **Qatar mediates Iran-Pakistan talks.** Qatar discussed Pakistani mediation efforts and Lebanese developments with Iran, according to Al Jazeera reports. The conversations reflect Qatar's continued role as a regional diplomatic facilitator during ongoing Middle East tensions.

9. **Technology contracts in Saudi Arabia.** Technology Patterns company won a [314.5 million riyal](https://www.alarabiya.net/aswaq/companies/2026/06/02/%D8%A7%D9%86%D9%85%D8%A7%D8%B7-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D9%82%D9%86%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%AA%D9%81%D9%88%D8%B2-%D8%A8%D8%B9%D9%82%D8%AF-%D9%85%D9%86-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B3%D8%B9%D9%88%D8%AF%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D9%84%D9%84%D8%B7%D8%A7%D9%82%D8%A9-%D8%A8%D9%803145-%D9%85%D9%84%D9%8A%D9%88%D9%86-%D8%B1%D9%8A%D8%A7%D9%84) contract from Saudi Energy Ministry. The deal highlights continued investment in technology infrastructure across the Kingdom's energy sector.

10. **Cross-border market linkage.** The Amman Stock Exchange activated an electronic [link with](https://www.cnbcarabia.com/152855/2026/02/06/%D8%AA%D9%81%D8%B9%D9%8A%D9%84-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B1%D8%A8%D8%B7-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A5%D9%84%D9%83%D8%AA%D8%B1%D9%88%D9%86%D9%8A-%D8%A8%D9%8A%D9%86-%D8%A8%D9%88%D8%B1%D8%B5%D8%A9-%D8%B9%D9%85%D8%A7%D9%86-%D9%88%D8%B3%D9%88%D9%82-%D8%A3%D8%A8%D9%88%D8%B8%D8%A8%D9%8A-%D9%84%D9%84%D8%A3%D9%88%D8%B1%D8%A7%D9%82-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%B9%D8%A8%D8%B1-%D9%85%D9%86%D8%B5%D8%A9-%D8%AA%D8%A8%D8%A7%D8%AF%D9%84) Abu Dhabi Securities Market through an exchange platform, facilitating cross-border trading between Jordan and the UAE.
