**US-Iran strikes escalate.** The US bombed Iranian military sites after Iran fired missiles at American troops stationed in Kuwait, according to [the BBC](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/crlpy8n7pr6o). The strikes followed Iranian attacks on 20 US military positions since the conflict began, satellite imagery showed.

**OPEC+ set for July output hike.** OPEC+ producers were likely to raise oil production targets for July despite disruptions in the Strait of Hormuz, according to [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/opec-likely-raise-july-oil-output-target-despite-hormuz-disruption-sources-say-2026-06-01/). Asian imports of US crude surged but failed to offset losses from Hormuz disruptions.

**Yemen pilgrimage death mourned.** Prince Khalid bin Salman conveyed Saudi leadership condolences for former Yemeni President Abd Rabbuh Mansour Hadi's death, calling him "a wise leader keen on his country's security and stability." The IRG leadership and Saudi Ambassador to Yemen Mohammed AlJabir also paid respects.

**Qatar-Iran mediation efforts.** Qatar discussed with Iran ongoing Pakistani mediation efforts and developments in Lebanon, according to [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.net/news/2026/6/2/%D9%82%D8%B7%D8%B1-%D8%AA%D8%A8%D8%AD%D8%AB-%D9%85%D8%B9-%D8%A5%D9%8A%D8%B1%D8%A7%D9%86-%D8%AC%D9%87%D9%88%D8%AF-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%88%D8%B3%D8%A7%D8%B7%D8%A9). Oman's Foreign Minister Badr Albusaidi held parallel discussions with Iran's Deputy Foreign Minister on diplomatic engagement to reduce regional tensions.

**UAE AI talent dominance.** The UAE ranked first globally in artificial intelligence talent concentration, growing 121% between 2019 and 2025, Sheikh Abdulla bin Zayed announced. He called the figure evidence of leadership vision making "building people and attracting minds a solid national approach."

**Oman export ranking top.** Oman ranked first globally in the Export Supply Chain Start-Up Index, according to [Times of Oman](https://timesofoman.com/article/172480-oman-ranks-first-globally-in-export-supply-chain-start-up-index). State energy firm OQ also signed an energy pact to expand its international presence in Libya.

**Oman fertilizer IPO planned.** The largest fertilizer producer in Oman approached an initial public offering on the Muscat Stock Exchange, according to [Asharq Economy](https://asharqbusiness.com/financial-markets/134867/%D8%A3%D9%83%D8%A8%D8%B1-%D9%85%D9%86%D8%AA%D8%AC-%D8%A3%D8%B3%D9%85%D8%AF%D8%A9-%D9%81%D9%8A-%D8%B9%D9%85%D8%A7%D9%86-%D9%8A%D9%82%D8%AA%D8%B1%D8%A8-%D9%85%D9%86-%D8%B7%D8%B1%D8%AD-%D8%B9%D8%A7%D9%85-%D8%A3%D9%88%D9%84%D9%8A-%D9%81%D9%8A-%D8%A8%D9%88%D8%B1%D8%B5%D8%A9-%D9%85%D8%B3%D9%82%D8%B7/). The move would bring another major Omani firm to public markets.

**Saudi tech contract awarded.** Technology Patterns won a 314.5 million riyal contract from Saudi Energy, according to [Al Arabiya](https://www.alarabiya.net/aswaq/companies/2026/06/02/%D8%A7%D9%86%D9%85%D8%A7%D8%B7-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D9%82%D9%86%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%AA%D9%81%D9%88%D8%B2-%D8%A8%D8%B9%D9%82%D8%AF-%D9%85%D9%86-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B3%D8%B9%D9%88%D8%AF%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D9%84%D9%84%D8%B7%D8%A7%D9%82%D8%A9-%D8%A8%D9%803145-%D9%85%D9%84%D9%8A%D9%88%D9%86-%D8%B1%D9%8A%D8%A7%D9%84). **Hajj pilgrimage concludes.** Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman received congratulations on the successful Hajj season, which Saudi officials described as exceptionally well-organized. Prince Khalid bin Salman thanked God for "honoring us with serving the Two Holy Mosques and caring for the pilgrims."

**Bahrain house fire rescue.** Bahrain Civil Defense rescued five children from a house fire in Aali area without casualties, according to the [Interior Ministry](https://policemc.gov.bh/en/news/78966/). Investigations were underway to determine the cause of the blaze.
