**Gulf Brief**

1. **Lebanon-Israel ceasefire.** A 10-day ceasefire between Israel and Lebanon [took effect](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/4/17/celebrations-in-lebanon-as-10-day-ceasefire-with-israel-begins?traffic_source=rss), sparking celebrations across Lebanon following months of escalating conflict. Saudi Arabia welcomed President Trump's announcement of the truce, with Foreign Minister Prince Faisal praising Lebanese President Joseph Aoun's role in the negotiations.

2. **Saudi sports funding shift.** Saudi Arabia is pressuring its sports investments to become profitable, clouding LIV Golf's future as the kingdom shifts from unlimited spending to demanding returns on Vision 2030 projects. [Bloomberg reported](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-04-16/saudis-now-want-sports-to-make-money-clouding-liv-golf-s-future) the change reflects broader economic priorities as Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman's transformation program matures.

3. **Iran war economic fallout.** The IMF warned Middle Eastern states face uneven economic impacts from the ongoing Iran conflict, with energy producers benefiting from price spikes while importers struggle. [Reuters noted](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/imf-says-middle-east-states-face-uneven-fallout-iran-war-2026-04-16/) TotalEnergies expects sharp first-quarter earnings gains despite Iranian production hits.

4. **US-Iran military posture.** Pentagon chief Pete Hegseth declared US forces "locked and loaded" to strike Iran's power plants and energy infrastructure if ordered by President Trump. Defense Minister Prince Khalid bin Salman met with counterparts from Greece, Britain and Hungary to discuss [Iran's continued attacks](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/us-forces-ready-restart-combat-if-iran-doesnt-agree-deal-says-hegseth-2026-04-16/) on Saudi Arabia and regional implications.

5. **Strait of Hormuz tensions.** Bahrain and Morocco discussed post-war planning for reopening the Strait of Hormuz, according to Egyptian media reports. Qatar's foreign ministry advisor Majed Al Ansari emphasized Qatar's commitment to ensuring "safe & unhindered passage through the Strait of Hormuz" in coordination with Britain.

6. **Yemen prisoner exchange.** Saudi envoy Mohammed AlJabir announced plans to visit Sanaa with an Omani delegation to consolidate the truce and support prisoner exchanges between Yemeni factions. The visit aims to advance dialogue between [various Yemeni components](https://www.youm7.com/story/2026/4/17/%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A8%D8%AD%D8%B1%D9%8A%D9%86-%D9%88%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%BA%D8%B1%D8%A8-%D9%8A%D8%A8%D8%AD%D8%AB%D8%A7%D9%86-%D9%85%D8%A7-%D8%A8%D8%B9%D8%AF-%D9%88%D9%82%D9%81-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AD%D8%B1%D8%A8-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A5%D9%8A%D8%B1%D8%A7%D9%86%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D9%88%D9%81%D8%AA%D8%AD-%D9%85%D8%B6%D9%8A%D9%82/7380326) for a comprehensive political solution.

7. **Kuwait journalist detained.** International rights groups demanded the release of journalist Ahmed Shihab El-Din in Kuwait, highlighting growing press freedom concerns across the Gulf. The [BBC reported](https://www.bbc.com/arabic/articles/cy812v4l788o) the case has drawn regional attention as authorities crack down on critical coverage.

8. **Turkey regional diplomacy.** Turkey offered to host Middle East security cooperation talks and urged "constructive" US-Iran negotiations to end the current conflict. The move positions Ankara as a potential mediator while it develops transport networks targeting [Europe-Gulf trade routes](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-04-16/turkey-to-improve-transport-network-as-it-eyes-europe-gulf-route).

9. **Palestinian statehood conference.** Prince Faisal bin Farhan co-chaired an international conference in Paris on Palestinian statehood with French Foreign Minister Jean-Noël Barrot. The Saudi foreign minister praised France's commitment to recognizing Palestine and called for accelerated international recognition of a Palestinian state.

10. **Houthi naval operations.** Houthi spokesman Mohammed Abdulsalam defended Yemen's naval operations in the Red Sea as legitimate support for Palestinians, claiming a US warship fired "hysterically" at a Yemeni reconnaissance aircraft. The incident underscores continued [maritime tensions](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/us-forces-ready-restart-combat-if-iran-doesnt-agree-deal-says-hegseth-2026-04-16/) despite ceasefire efforts elsewhere.
