**US-Iran Escalation.** The United States struck Iranian military sites and downed Iranian missiles targeting American forces in Kuwait, according to the [BBC reported](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/crlpy8n7pr6o). Saudi Arabia strongly condemned Iran's attacks on Kuwait while appreciating Trump's decision to give diplomacy a chance to end the war, Foreign Minister Prince Faisal bin Farhan said.

**OPEC Output Decision.** OPEC+ sources told Reuters the group will likely [raise July](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/opec-likely-raise-july-oil-output-target-despite-hormuz-disruption-sources-say-2026-06-01/) oil output targets despite disruptions in the Strait of Hormuz. The decision comes as Saudi Aramco raised LPG official selling prices by up to 3% for June deliveries.

**Qatar Mediation Efforts.** Qatar's Prime Minister discussed mediation efforts between Washington and Tehran with Iran's Foreign Minister by telephone, according to [Youm7 reported](https://www.youm7.com/story/2026/6/2/رئيس-وزراء-قطر-ووزير-خارجية-إيران-يبحثان-هاتفيا-جهود-الوساطة/7435138). A CNN source revealed Qatar worked with the Trump administration to prevent Israeli strikes on Beirut. Al Jazeera reported Qatar also discussed Pakistani mediation efforts and Lebanon developments with Iran.

**Oman Global Rankings.** Oman ranked first globally in the Export Supply Chain Start-Up Index, while state energy company OQ expanded its international footprint with a [Libyan energy](https://timesofoman.com//article/172479-oq-expands-international-footprint-with-libyan-energy-pact) pact, according to Times of Oman. The sultanate also issued royal decrees on cybercrime laws and cultural heritage amendments.

**Yemen Leadership Deaths.** Saudi Defense Minister Prince Khalid bin Salman conveyed condolences to the family of former Yemeni President Abd Rabbuh Mansour Hadi on his death, calling him "a wise leader who was keen on the security, stability, development and prosperity of his country." Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman also offered condolences by telephone.

**Saudi Energy Contracts.** Technology Patterns won a 314.5 million riyal contract from Saudi Energy, according to [Al Arabiya](https://www.alarabiya.net/amp/aswaq/companies/2026/06/02/انماط-التقنية-تفوز-بعقد-من-السعودية-للطاقة-بـ3145-مليون-ريال). The kingdom promoted 327 Public Prosecution members to various judicial ranks in a royal order issued by King Salman.

**Bahrain Civil Defense.** Bahrain's Civil Defense rescued five children from a house fire in Aali without casualties, the Interior Ministry announced. The kingdom also strongly condemned Iranian attacks on Kuwait, calling for an end to such hostile actions targeting Gulf states.

**Israeli-Lebanon Tensions.** Israel ordered strikes on Beirut suburbs as the Hezbollah conflict escalated, the [BBC reported](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c4g419e2xlvo). Clashes continued in Lebanon despite both Israel and Hezbollah accepting a US partial ceasefire plan, while Iran attacks damaged 20 US military sites since the war began.

**UAE AI Leadership.** The UAE ranked first globally in artificial intelligence talent concentration with 121% growth between 2019 and 2025, Sheikh Abdullah bin Zayed said. The UAE Government Media Office launched an AI-powered government communication network and announced 6.2% economic growth for 2025.

**Syrian Aviation Windfall.** Iran's war handed Syria a windfall as airlines reroute over its airspace, Reuters [exclusively reported](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/iran-war-hands-syria-windfall-airlines-reroute-over-its-airspace-2026-06-01/). Major investment pledges were also announced at France's Choose France summit, according to Reuters coverage.
