**Gulf Brief: Monday Evening**

1. **Hajj operations success.** Saudi Arabia declared the 2026 Hajj season concluded without epidemic outbreaks following successful pilgrimage management. Interior Minister Prince Abdulaziz bin Saud credited Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman's directives for enhanced planning efficiency. Over 8,000 residency, work and border security violations were addressed during the [week-long](https://timesofoman.com//article/172382-hormuz-disruption-remains-most-consequential-variable-for-indias-external-price-outlook-finance-ministry-review) operations.

2. **Iran nuclear talks status.** President Trump delayed making a "final determination" on Iran negotiations following high-level meetings. Foreign Minister Prince Faisal bin Farhan said Saudi Arabia "highly appreciates" Trump's decision to give diplomacy additional opportunity to restore Strait of Hormuz [security](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c0r2d40r91qo?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss) and navigation freedom.

3. **Yemen peace initiative.** Saudi Yemen envoy Mohammed AlJabir announced plans to visit Sanaa with an Oman delegation, building on the Kingdom's 2021 peace initiative. The visit aims to consolidate ceasefire arrangements, support prisoner exchanges and discuss dialogue between Yemeni [components](https://apnews.com/article/iran-us-war-ceasefire-nuclear-talks-cac5206df0f0c7b79fe9321c08d63096) for comprehensive political solutions.

4. **UAE economic expansion.** The Emirates reported 6.2% economic growth with GDP rising to AED 1.9 trillion in 2025, according to government media office. The UAE also achieved first place globally in artificial intelligence talent concentration, growing 121% between 2019 and [2025](https://timesofoman.com//article/172380-oman-hosts-a-strategic-project-for-production-of-lithium-ion-battery-materials), Sheikh Abdullah bin Zayed announced.

5. **Oman strategic projects.** The sultanate launched a lithium-ion battery materials production project as Invest Oman unveiled projects with guaranteed offtake agreements. Sultan Haitham bin Tarik offered Eid prayers at Sultan Qaboos Mosque in Sohar, North Al [Batinah](https://timesofoman.com//article/172379-invest-oman-unveils-projects-with-guaranteed-offtake) governorate.

6. **Qatar leadership transition.** Emir Sheikh Tamim appointed Mohammed bin Abdulrahman Al Thani as Prime Minister, who expressed gratitude for the trust and commitment to serving under the Emir's wise leadership. Majed Al Ansari was named advisor and official spokesman for the Foreign [Ministry](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cx21le869n1o?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss).

7. **Yemen political developments.** The IRG-backed Presidential Leadership Council appointed Ahmed Hamid Lamlas as governor of Aden. Former President Abd Rabbuh Mansour Hadi's funeral took place in Aden with senior leadership attendance, according to Information Minister [Mohammed](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/ceqpelq5reqo?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss) Al-Eryani.

8. **Bahrain security operations.** Civil Defense rescued five children from a house fire in Aali area without casualties. The Kingdom strongly condemned Iranian attacks on Kuwait, reaffirming commitment to regional [security](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cj3pgrpmlklo?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss) cooperation.

9. **Kuwait diplomatic activities.** The Prime Minister congratulated Croatia's President on National Statehood Day. Kuwait oil price dropped 84 cents to $117.51 per barrel, according to Kuwait Petroleum Corporation [data](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMiVkFVX3lxTE5ldDFVSllhRVZhd2pGSkNHTmttdFdFMnNPRXdfNzN1OU9pMENOSlNKR3pTUU9vVDlzX2c1WENBU2I2QUhjYnBRXzJqdXIyNzNNLW56LVVR?oc=5).

10. **Regional Eid celebrations.** Gulf leaders exchanged congratulations for Eid Al-Adha following successful Hajj completion. Sheikh Mohammed bin Rashid received well-wishers at Za'abeel Majlis in Dubai, while regional rulers praised Saudi Arabia's pilgrimage [management](https://arabic.cnn.com/middle-east/article/2026/05/30/oman-warns-suspected-mine-seen-in-strait-of-hormuz).
