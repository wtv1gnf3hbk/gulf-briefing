1. **Iran-Kuwait missile strikes.** Iranian forces launched missile and drone attacks on Kuwaiti territory, prompting Kuwait's air defenses to intercept the hostile projectiles. The United States military accused Iran of violating an apparent [ceasefire agreement](https://apnews.com/article/iran-us-war-oil-may-28-2026-8f5ed2813ba63df7ae9ccbe991688d29), according to AP News. Gulf Cooperation Council states condemned the attacks as violations of Kuwaiti sovereignty.

2. **US-Iran military escalation.** The US conducted new strikes against Iranian military sites and drone facilities following the Kuwait attacks, Reuters [exclusively reported](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/us-carries-out-new-strikes-iran-against-military-site-official-says-2026-05-27/). Iran said it targeted American bases in response to fresh US strikes. Multiple news outlets reported discussions of a potential ceasefire extension pending President Trump's approval.

3. **Yemen leadership transition.** Former Yemeni President Abd Rabbuh Mansour Hadi died, prompting condolences from Saudi King Salman and Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman, according to the Saudi Press Agency. The internationally recognized government's Information Minister Mohammed Al-Eryani mourned Hadi as a "brave leader" who defended Yemen's republican system against what he called the "new Imamate project."

4. **Saudi Hajj operations.** Approximately two million Muslim pilgrims performed Hajj rituals in Saudi Arabia during extreme heat conditions, with temperatures soaring during Eid al-Adha celebrations. Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman received well-wishers at Mina Palace, while security forces [sprayed water](https://timesofoman.com//article/172334-air-cargo-demand-rises-4-in-april-despite-middle-east-disruptions-iata) on pilgrims to provide relief from the heat, according to Saudi state media.

5. **Saudi diplomatic outreach.** Foreign Minister Prince Faisal bin Farhan chaired an international conference on Palestinian statehood implementation with France, praising Paris for its resolve to recognize Palestine. Defense Minister Prince Khalid bin Salman met with British National Security Adviser Jonathan Powell to discuss regional coordination and [de-escalation efforts](https://timesofoman.com//article/172331-blatant-violation-of-international-law-tehran-condemns-alleged-us-ceasefire-breaches).

6. **UAE government modernization.** Dubai rulers Mohammed bin Rashid and Mansour bin Zayed attended a national government retreat to implement artificial intelligence systems across federal operations. The initiative aims to transform half of government services and operations using AI technology, with more than 400 officials participating in the transformation planning sessions.

7. **Oman-Iran dialogue.** Omani Foreign Minister Badr Albusaidi held discussions with Iran's Deputy Foreign Minister, describing the talks as reflecting "constructive dialogue" between the two nations. Albusaidi reaffirmed Oman's commitment to diplomatic engagement and supporting efforts to [reduce regional](https://timesofoman.com//article/172330-khamenei-charts-post-war-economic-roadmap-for-irans-parliament) tensions through peaceful coexistence.

8. **Hormuz shipping activity.** Three oil and liquefied natural gas tankers exited the Strait of Hormuz with their tracking transponders turned off, Reuters [reported](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/three-oil-lng-tankers-exit-hormuz-with-transponders-off-2026-05-28/). The development came as maritime tensions increased following the Iran-US military exchanges and attacks on Kuwait.

9. **Qatar diplomatic appointments.** Emir Sheikh Tamim bin Hamad appointed Mohammed bin Abdulrahman Al Thani as Prime Minister, with Al Thani expressing gratitude for the trust placed in him. Majed Al Ansari was appointed as adviser to the Deputy Prime Minister and official spokesman for Qatar's Foreign Ministry.

10. **Regional air cargo resilience.** Global air cargo demand rose 4% in April despite Middle East disruptions, the International Air Transport Association reported. The growth occurred while regional conflicts and military operations continued to affect [transportation networks](https://timesofoman.com//article/172334-air-cargo-demand-rises-4-in-april-despite-middle-east-disruptions-iata) across the Arabian Peninsula.
