**Iran strikes Kuwait.** Iran fired missiles at US forces stationed in Kuwait, prompting American retaliatory strikes on Iranian radar sites, according to the [BBC](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/crlpy8n7pr6o). The [Associated Press reports](https://apnews.com/article/iran-us-irael-war-kuwait-strikes-88daa9f90b48baaa7beb18e35515c59d) that air raid sirens sounded across Kuwait City following the US response. The Iranian attacks were part of escalating regional tensions tied to the Gaza conflict.

**OPEC+ considers output increase.** OPEC+ is [likely to raise](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/opec-likely-raise-july-oil-output-target-despite-hormuz-disruption-sources-say-2026-06-01/) its July oil production target despite ongoing disruption in the Strait of Hormuz, Reuters reports citing industry sources. Saudi Aramco separately [increased LPG prices](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/saudi-aramco-raises-lpg-osps-by-up-3-june-sonatrach-cuts-prices-by-18-31-2026-06-01/) by up to 3% for June deliveries.

**Saudi condemns Israeli Lebanon incursion.** The Saudi Foreign Ministry [condemned Israel's](https://www.youm7.com/story/2026/6/2/) ground offensive into Lebanon and called for its immediate halt. The kingdom's position came as [Lebanon said](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c202rxp1z15o) Hezbollah agreed to reciprocal halt to attacks on Israel, following Trump's announcement that both sides had agreed to stop exchanges of fire.

**Oman issues cybercrime law.** Sultan Haitham issued [two royal decrees](https://timesofoman.com//article/172462-new-cyberlaw-to-enhance-omans-digital-security), including one promulgating a new law to combat cybercrime and another amending the Cultural Heritage Law, according to Oman News Agency. The measures are designed to enhance digital security frameworks.

**Yemen president dies.** Former Yemeni President Abd Rabbuh Mansour Hadi died, prompting condolences from across the Gulf. Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman [conveyed condolences](https://spa.gov.sa/N2604388) to Hadi's family, while Saudi Ambassador to Yemen Mohammed al-Jaber also [expressed sympathy](https://mohdsalj) for the loss of the leader who guided Yemen during critical transition periods.

**UAE advances AI computing.** MGX and strategic partners announced [expansion of Campus AI](https://ADMediaOffice) in France, targeting development of up to 3 gigawatts of computing capacity nationwide through a second site. The expansion is expected to double the initial investment by the consortium and its partners.

**Bahrain rescues children from fire.** Bahrain's Civil Defense [rescued five children](https://policemc.gov.bh/en/news/78966/) from a house fire in the Aali area without any injuries reported. Investigations are underway to determine the cause of the blaze.

**Saudi Hajj season concludes.** Millions of pilgrims completed the annual Hajj pilgrimage to Mecca, with [Reuters documenting](https://jp.reuters.com/pictures/journey-mecca-scenes-annual-hajj-pilgrimage-2026-06-01/6FLTN6AQJRJ4BEUDMG4IS5BFEU) scenes from the religious gathering. Saudi authorities managed what officials described as one of the largest peaceful assemblies in the world.
