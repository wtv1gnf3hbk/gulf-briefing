**Iran-US military confrontation.** US forces bombed Iranian military sites following Iran's missile strikes on US troops in Kuwait. [The BBC reports](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/crlpy8n7pr6o) Iran fired missiles at American forces, prompting US retaliation. Sirens sounded over Kuwait City during the exchanges.

**Kuwait escapes prisoner security.** Three high-risk prisoners broke out of Kuwait's Central Prison, [according to Independent Arabia](https://www.independentarabia.com/node/650268). The escapees included two individuals sentenced to death and a drug smuggler. They have since been recaptured.

**Bahrain bans Iran-Iraq travel.** Bahrain's Interior Ministry [prohibited citizens](https://arabic.cnn.com/middle-east/article/2026/06/02/bahrains-interior-ministry-announces-travel-ban-on-citizens-to-iran-iraq) from traveling to Iran and Iraq due to ongoing security tensions. Sky News Arabia reports the decision follows escalating regional conflicts.

**Saudi-Iranian diplomatic outreach.** Saudi Ambassador to Yemen Mohammed AlJabir announced plans to visit Sanaa with an Omani delegation to consolidate ceasefire efforts and discuss prisoner exchanges, per his [Twitter post](https://twitter.com/mohdsalj). He emphasized the Kingdom's initiative to end the Yemeni crisis through dialogue.

**Former Yemeni President dies.** Abd Rabbuh Mansour Hadi, former President of Yemen, passed away. Prince Khalid bin Salman conveyed Saudi leadership condolences, [describing Hadi](https://twitter.com/kbsalsaud) as "a wise leader who was keen on the security, stability, development and prosperity of his country."

**Yemen Presidential Council tensions.** Presidential Leadership Council member Abu Zaraa criticized unilateral decision-making within the council, stating such actions were "a major cause of division," according to his [Twitter account](https://twitter.com/AbuZar3a). He emphasized the importance of institutional work and collective decision-making.

**Saudi drug seizure operation.** Saudi authorities seized 845,087 amphetamine tablets hidden inside cardboard sheets in the Eastern Province, the [Interior Ministry reported](https://twitter.com/MOISaudiArabia). The operation was part of the Kingdom's ongoing war on drugs campaign.

**Qatar defense cooperation expanded.** Qatar's Deputy Prime Minister and Defense Minister met with Indonesian Defense Minister to strengthen bilateral defense partnerships, [QNA reported](https://www.aljazeera.net/news/2026/6/2). The discussions focused on enhancing military cooperation between the two nations.

**Oman-Libya energy partnership.** Oman and Libya signed a memorandum of understanding to enhance ties in oil and gas sectors, [Times of Oman reported](https://timesofoman.com/article/172501-oman-libya-sign-mou-to-enhance-ties-in-oil-and-gas). The agreement aims to strengthen energy cooperation between the two countries.

**Oman Sultan begins Belgium visit.** Sultan Haitham bin Tarik departed for a three-day state visit to Belgium, where he will meet King Philippe Leopold Louis Marie, [according to ONA](https://twitter.com/ONA_eng). The visit aims to strengthen bilateral relations between Oman and Belgium.
