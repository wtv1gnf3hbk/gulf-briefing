**Gulf Daily Briefing**
May 31, 2026

1. **Saudi Crown Prince meets Chad President.** Prince Mohammed bin Salman received Chad President Mahamat Déby at Mona Palace, reviewing bilateral relations and cooperation prospects in various fields. According to [Prince Khalid bin Salman](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c0r2d40r91qo?at_medium=RSS&amp;at_campaign=rss), the meeting covered regional issues of mutual interest. The Crown Prince also met with UK National Security Adviser Jonathan Powell to discuss Saudi-British cooperation and joint coordination to address regional challenges.

2. **Trump Iran talks continue without breakthrough.** President Trump held meetings to make a "final determination" on Iran but announced no deal, according to the [BBC reports](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c0r2d40r91qo?at_medium=RSS&amp;at_campaign=rss). US Vice President JD Vance stated the US and Iran are "very close" to an agreement but "not there yet." Foreign Minister Prince Faisal bin Farhan praised Trump's decision to give diplomacy another chance to restore security and freedom of navigation in the Strait of Hormuz.

3. **Former Yemen President Hadi dies.** Prince Khalid bin Salman conveyed Saudi leadership condolences to the family of former Yemeni President Abd Rabbuh Mansour Hadi following his death. The Saudi defense minister described Hadi as "a wise leader who was keen on the security, stability, development and prosperity of his country." The IRG government received multiple condolences from regional leaders.

4. **UAE economy grows 6.2% with GDP reaching AED 1.9 trillion.** The UAE announced robust economic expansion for 2025, according to official [government data](https://timesofoman.com//article/172390-activities-prohibited-for-foreign-investors-under-omans-foreign-capital-investment-law). Sheikh Mohammed bin Rashid reviewed tourism sector achievements during a government retreat focused on artificial intelligence integration. The UAE ranks first globally in AI talent concentration, growing 121% between 2019 and 2025.

5. **Oman detects suspected mine in Strait of Hormuz.** Oman's Maritime Security Centre identified a suspected floating mine in the strategic waterway, according to [Times of Oman](https://timesofoman.com//article/172387-omans-maritime-security-centre-detects-suspected-floating-mine-in-strait-of-hormuz). The discovery comes as maritime security remains a key concern following regional tensions. Prince William of Wales received HH Sayyid Theyazin bin Haitham Al Said at Windsor Castle to discuss UK-Oman relations.

8. **Qatar appoints new Prime Minister.** Sheikh Mohammed bin Abdulrahman Al Thani was appointed Prime Minister by Emir Sheikh Tamim bin Hamad Al Thani. In a [Twitter statement](https://timesofoman.com//article/172390-activities-prohibited-for-foreign-investors-under-omans-foreign-capital-investment-law), the new PM expressed gratitude for the trust placed in him and commitment to serving Qatar's advancement.
