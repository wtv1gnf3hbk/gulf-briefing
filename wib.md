Gulf Briefing — Monday June 2, 2026

1. **Iran-US military escalation.** The United States [bombed Iranian](https://apnews.com/article/iran-us-irael-war-kuwait-strikes-88daa9f90b48baaa7beb18e35515c59d) military sites following Iran's missile attacks on US forces in Kuwait. Reuters reports oil prices jumped over $6 after the strikes. Saudi Arabia condemned Iran's attacks on Kuwait through its foreign ministry statement, calling them "sinful and repeated."

2. **Saudi Hajj operations.** Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman offered condolences to the family of former Yemeni President Abd Rabbuh Mansour Hadi following his death. The kingdom managed over 1.7 million pilgrims during this year's Hajj season. Prince Khalid bin Salman conveyed the royal family's condolences directly to Hadi's family members.

3. **UAE economic growth.** The UAE government announced 6.2% new economic growth with GDP expected to rise to 1.9 trillion dirhams during 2025. Abu Dhabi's MGX investment firm announced expansion of its Campus AI project in France with partners Bpifrance and Mistral. The UAE leads globally in artificial intelligence talent concentration, growing 121% between 2019 and 2025.

4. **Yemen government transitions.** The IRG-aligned government appointed Ahmed Hamid Lamlas as governor of Aden while condemning UAE air strikes on National Army forces in Aden and Abyan. Mohammed Al-Eryani praised Congressman Joe Wilson for submitting an amendment to the US defense budget aimed at strengthening US-Yemen defense cooperation.

5. **Qatar diplomatic moves.** Prime Minister Mohammed bin Abdulrahman Al Thani received a phone call from Iran's foreign minister, according to Qatar News Agency. The prime minister reaffirmed Qatar's support for efforts to reach a comprehensive agreement to resolve regional crises. Majed Al Ansari was appointed as official spokesman for the foreign ministry.

6. **OPEC+ production decisions.** Reuters reports OPEC+ is likely to raise July oil output targets despite the Hormuz strait disruption, according to sources. Saudi Aramco raised LPG official selling prices by up to 3% for June. The energy market volatility follows regional military tensions affecting shipping routes.

7. **Oman cybersecurity law.** Sultan Haitham issued two royal decrees, with the first promulgating a new law on combating cybercrime. The second decree amended provisions of the Cultural Heritage Law. Oman's foreign minister met with Iran's deputy foreign minister to discuss regional diplomatic engagement.

8. **Lebanon ceasefire developments.** President Trump announced that Israel and Hezbollah agreed to halt mutual attacks and cease all exchanges of fire, according to Kuwait News Agency. Iran warned that Israeli attacks in Lebanon threaten any potential ceasefire with the US. The BBC reports Israel ordered strikes on Beirut suburbs as the Hezbollah conflict escalated.

9. **Bahrain condemns attacks.** The Kingdom of Bahrain strongly condemned Iranian attacks on Kuwait through missile and drone strikes. The foreign ministry called for protecting civilians and stressed the immediate priority of ending violence. Civil Defense rescued five children from a house fire in the Aali area without injuries.

10. **Saudi-France cooperation.** Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman received a phone call from French President Emmanuel Macron discussing bilateral cooperation and regional developments. Prince Faisal bin Farhan chaired an international conference with French Foreign Minister Jean-Noël Barrot on the peaceful resolution of the Palestinian issue and implementing the two-state solution.
