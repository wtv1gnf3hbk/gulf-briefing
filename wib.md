**Gulf News Briefing - June 2, 2026**

1. **US-Iran military escalation.** The United States bombed Iranian military sites after Iran fired missiles at American troops in Kuwait, according to [AP reports](https://apnews.com/article/iran-us-irael-war-kuwait-strikes-88daa9f90b48baaa7beb18e35515c59d). Sirens sounded over Kuwait City following the US strikes. The BBC says the US targeted Iranian radar installations. This marks a significant escalation in the regional conflict affecting multiple Gulf states.

2. **Israel-Hezbollah ceasefire agreement.** President Trump announced that Israel and Hezbollah agreed to halt mutual attacks and cease all exchanges of fire, [Reuters reports](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/iran-war-hands-syria-windfall-airlines-reroute-over-its-airspace-2026-06-01/). Lebanon confirmed Hezbollah agreed to the reciprocal halt to attacks on Israel. This follows Israeli airstrikes on Beirut suburbs as the conflict had escalated in recent weeks.

3. **Saudi condemnation of Iranian attacks.** Saudi Arabia's Foreign Ministry "condemned and denounced in the strongest terms the sinful and repeated Iranian attacks on the sister State of Kuwait," according to official [Saudi MFA posts](https://twitter.com/KSAMOFA). Prince Faisal bin Farhan praised US President Trump's decision to give diplomacy a chance to end the war and restore freedom of navigation in the Strait of Hormuz.

4. **Death of Yemen's former president.** Former Yemeni President Abd Rabbuh Mansour Hadi died, prompting condolences from across the Gulf. Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman called Hadi's son to offer condolences, according to Saudi Press Agency. The IRG's Mohammed Al-Eryani visited Hadi's family and called the former president "one of the nation's loyal men."

5. **OPEC production adjustments.** OPEC+ is likely to raise July oil output targets despite the Hormuz Strait disruption, [Reuters reports](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/opec-likely-raise-july-oil-output-target-despite-hormuz-disruption-sources-say-2026-06-01/). Saudi Aramco raised LPG official selling prices by up to 3% for June. Oil jumped more than 4% according to Qatar News Agency reports on market movements.

6. **Qatar-Iran diplomatic talks.** Qatar discussed Pakistani mediation efforts and developments in Lebanon with Iran, according to [Al Jazeera reports](https://www.aljazeera.net/amp/news/2026/6/2/%D9%82%D8%B7%D8%B1-%D8%AA%D8%A8%D8%AD%D8%AB-%D9%85%D8%B9-%D8%A5%D9%8A%D8%B1%D8%A7%D9%86-%D8%AC%D9%87%D9%88%D8%AF-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%88%D8%B3%D8%A7%D8%B7%D8%A9). Qatar's foreign ministry said using the Strait of Hormuz as pressure deepens the crisis. Oman's Foreign Minister Badr Albusaidi met with Iran's Deputy Foreign Minister, emphasizing diplomatic engagement.

7. **Oman cybercrime law.** Sultan Haitham issued two royal decrees, with the first promulgating a new Law on Combating Cybercrime, according to [Times of Oman](https://timesofoman.com//article/172462-new-cyberlaw-to-enhance-omans-digital-security). The second decree amended provisions of the Cultural Heritage Law. The new legislation aims to enhance Oman's digital security infrastructure.

8. **UAE AI expansion.** The UAE ranks first globally in artificial intelligence talent concentration, growing 121% between 2019 and 2025, Sheikh Abdullah bin Zayed posted on social media. Abu Dhabi's MGX and partners will expand Campus AI in France, developing up to 3GW of compute capacity nationwide through a second site, according to official announcements.

9. **Bahrain condemns Kuwait attacks.** Bahrain's Foreign Ministry "strongly condemned the Iranian attack on the State of Kuwait," according to official [Bahrain diplomatic posts](https://twitter.com/bahdiplomatic). The kingdom expressed solidarity with Kuwait following the missile and drone attacks. Civil Defense also rescued five children from a house fire in Aali.

10. **Hajj season conclusion.** Saudi authorities concluded this year's Hajj season with Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman and other officials expressing gratitude for serving pilgrims. More than 85,000 visitors from six Islamic cultures in "Abra" cultural sites were reported by Saudi Press Agency. The kingdom recorded the first birth of an Asian wild ass in over 100 years at the Prince Mohammed bin Salman Royal Reserve.
