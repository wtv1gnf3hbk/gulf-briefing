1. **Hajj season success.** Saudi Arabia declared the completion of the 1447 AH Hajj season successful, with pilgrims continuing rituals on the second day of Tashreeq. Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman said the kingdom was honored to serve the Two Holy Mosques and pilgrims. Regional leaders including Kuwait's Emir and Oman's Sultan congratulated [Saudi leadership](https://spa.gov.sa/N2602764) on the achievement.

2. **Yemen's former president dies.** Former Yemeni President Abdrabbuh Mansour Hadi, who governed mostly from exile during the country's civil war, died at age 80, according to [AP News](https://apnews.com/article/yemen-expresident-abdrabbuh-mansour-hadi-died-b3afd7b3679af14d335578e9560c7cd8). IRG officials and the Presidential Leadership Council attended his burial ceremony, marking the end of a turbulent political career spanning Yemen's transition period.

3. **US-Iran negotiations continue.** Foreign Minister Prince Faisal bin Farhan said Saudi Arabia "highly appreciates" President Trump's decision to give diplomacy a chance to reach an acceptable agreement ending the war and restoring Strait of Hormuz navigation security. According to the [BBC](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c87qng40wz9o), the US and Iran are "very close" to a deal but "not there yet."

4. **Israel strikes Lebanon.** Israel conducted strikes on Beirut and southern Lebanon following large-scale evacuation orders, according to [BBC reports](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cj3pgrpmlklo). Qatar News Agency reported the death toll from Israeli aggression on Lebanon has risen to 3,355. The escalation comes following regional tensions over Gaza operations.

5. **Oman crude prices fall.** The official selling price of Oman crude dropped to $102 per barrel in July, reflecting weakening demand, Al Arabiya reported. Saudi Arabia may also [lower July](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/saudi-arabia-may-lower-july-oil-prices-asia-demand-weakens-2026-05-29/) oil prices to Asia as demand weakens, according to Reuters sources familiar with the matter.

6. **Gulf diplomatic engagement.** Oman's Foreign Minister Badr Albusaidi met with Iran's Deputy Foreign Minister, reflecting the "constructive nature of Oman-Iran dialogue." The meeting emphasized diplomatic engagement and reducing regional tensions. Qatar's PM appointed Majed Al Ansari as advisor and [official spokesman](https://twitter.com/majedalansari) for the Foreign Ministry.

7. **UAE AI leadership.** The UAE ranked first globally in artificial intelligence talent concentration, growing 121% between 2019 and 2025. Sheikh Abdullah bin Zayed highlighted this achievement as reflecting the leadership's vision of building people and attracting minds. The UAE continues advancing its [digital transformation](https://twitter.com/HHShkMohd) initiatives under government modernization programs.

8. **Yemen prisoner exchange.** Mohammed Abdulsalam, the Ansar Allah spokesman, announced an agreement including the cancellation of recent banking decisions and resumption of Yemenia Airlines flights between Sanaa and Jordan. The deal aims to increase flights to three daily and operate routes to Cairo and India as needed.

9. **Kuwait oil prices rise.** Kuwait crude prices increased $4.56 to $118.35 per barrel, according to Kuwait Petroleum Corporation. The price rise contrasts with broader regional crude pricing trends as markets respond to supply disruptions and demand fluctuations across Gulf producers.

10. **Oman-UK cooperation.** Prince William received Omani Minister of Culture Sayyid Theyazin bin Haitham Al Said at Windsor Castle, reviewing bilateral relations. The meeting launched the "Oman: The Jewel of Arabia" expedition project, building on long-standing ties between [Oman and](https://timesofoman.com/article/172355) the United Kingdom in cultural and educational cooperation.
