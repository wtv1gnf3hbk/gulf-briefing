**GULF NEWS BRIEFING**

1. **US-Iran ceasefire.** The United States and Iran agreed to a two-week ceasefire following Trump's ultimatum, with Pakistan hosting ongoing diplomatic talks. The White House called it a [victory while](https://apnews.com/article/iran-us-israel-trump-lebanon-april-7-2026-421ee64fdc9a5c26460df8119c7d1b3f) Iran's Supreme National Security Council claimed it represented a "humiliating" setback for Trump, according to Times of Oman.

2. **Iranian strikes on Gulf states.** Iran's IRGC attacked Saudi Arabia's Jubail petrochemical complex during the ceasefire talks, while Iranian missiles targeted multiple Gulf states. Qatar reported [four injuries](https://www.reuters.com/ar/world/6IP3FBVBHVJJTMIZ7DLZXAP7F4-2026-04-07/) from falling shrapnel after intercepting Iranian missiles, and UAE air defenses engaged one ballistic missile and 11 drones launched from Iran, per Reuters.

3. **Kuwait lockdown order.** Kuwait's government instructed citizens and residents to remain at home starting midnight, according to Arabic news sources, as Iranian missile threats targeted Gulf infrastructure. The directive came as [Kuwait condemned](https://www.ksamofa.gov.sa) the storming of its consulate in Basra, Iraq, with Saudi Arabia expressing solidarity through its Foreign Ministry.

4. **Saudi defensive posture.** Prince Khalid bin Salman discussed Iran's "continued flagrant attacks on the Kingdom" with defense ministers from Greece, Britain and Hungary, emphasizing regional security concerns. Saudi Civil Defense issued [safety alerts](https://twitter.com/SaudiDCD) warning citizens against filming missile interceptions, while some Saudi firms extended work-from-home policies ahead of Iran's ultimatum deadline.

5. **Gulf oil production collapse.** OPEC output fell by the most in decades last month due to regional conflict, Bloomberg reported. Middle East crude production could drop by [9 million](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-04-07/mideast-crude-output-seen-dropping-by-9-million-barrels-a-day) barrels daily, while Iran and Oman were reportedly granted rights to charge for Strait of Hormuz passage.

6. **UAE gas facility fire.** Abu Dhabi authorities responded to a fire incident at the Habshan gas-processing facility, with the UAE Interior Ministry advising the public to obtain information only from [official sources](https://twitter.com/moiuae) and avoid circulating unverified reports about the incident.

7. **Bahrain emergency measures.** Bahrain's Interior Ministry sounded air raid sirens, urging citizens to seek safe shelter as Iranian missile threats materialized. The kingdom condemned Iran's [terrorist targeting](https://twitter.com/bahdiplomatic) of Saudi energy infrastructure while coordinating regional defensive responses with Gulf allies.

8. **Pakistan debt pressure.** Pakistan's unexpected repayment of a $3.5 billion UAE loan placed pressure on its economy, Reuters reported. The repayment came as Pakistan Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif confirmed hosting [US-Iran talks](https://timesofoman.com//article/170395-shehbaz-sharif-confirms-immediate-ceasefire-us-iran-talks-in-pakistan) aimed at establishing the immediate ceasefire.

9. **Desalination vulnerability concerns.** Gulf desalination plants face heightened security risks following Iranian attacks on regional infrastructure, with Reuters examining the vulnerability of facilities serving tens of millions. The analysis highlighted potential threats to critical [water supplies](https://www.reuters.com/sustainability/land-use-biodiversity/gulf-desalination-plants-lifeline-tens-millions-face-iran-threat-2026-04-07/) across the Arabian Peninsula.

10. **Nuclear reactor strike risks.** Experts warned that striking Iran's atomic reactor could risk radiation exposure across the Gulf region, Bloomberg reported. The assessment came as Trump threatened to eliminate Iran's nuclear capabilities, raising concerns about [environmental fallout](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-04-07/striking-iran-atomic-reactor-risks-gulf-radiation-experts-say) affecting neighboring Gulf states.
