**Kuwait Under Iranian Attack**

1. **Kuwait missile strikes.** Iranian forces launched [missile and](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/kuwaiti-army-says-air-defences-intercepting-hostile-missile-drone-attacks-2026-05-28/) drone attacks against Kuwait, which Kuwaiti air defenses intercepted. Gulf states including UAE and Bahrain [strongly condemned](https://www.france24.com/ar/%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A3%D8%AE%D8%A8%D8%A7%D8%B1%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%B3%D8%AA%D9%85%D8%B1%D8%A9/20260528-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A5%D9%85%D8%A7%D8%B1%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%AA%D9%8F%D8%AF%D9%8A%D9%86-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%87%D8%AC%D9%85%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AC%D8%AF%D9%8A%D8%AF%D8%A9-%D8%B9%D9%84%D9%89-%D8%AF%D9%88%D9%84%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%83%D9%88%D9%8A%D8%AA) the Iranian attacks, marking a significant escalation in regional tensions following recent US-Iran military exchanges.

2. **US-Iran air strikes.** The United States conducted [new strikes](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/us-carries-out-new-strikes-iran-against-military-site-official-says-2026-05-27/) against Iranian military sites and drone facilities, according to Reuters. Iran retaliated by [targeting American](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c98r2qy5809o?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss) bases, per the BBC. President Trump dismissed reports of a Strait of Hormuz deal while Iran suggested potential reopening within a month if terms were agreed.

3. **Former Yemeni President dies.** Abd Rabbuh Mansour Hadi, former president of Yemen, died, prompting [condolences from](https://kuna.net.kw/ArticleDetails.aspx?id=3305606) the Saudi King and Crown Prince, Kuwait's leadership, and IRG Presidential Council Chairman Al-Alimi. Hadi led Yemen's transitional government following the 2011 Arab Spring and served as internationally recognized president during the ongoing civil war.

4. **Saudi Hajj operations.** Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman received Eid al-Adha well-wishers at Mina Palace, including Gulf leaders and military commanders participating in Hajj security operations, according to Saudi Press Agency. Approximately two million Muslim pilgrims performed [Hajj rituals](https://apnews.com/article/islam-hajj-mina-saudi-arabia-muslim-908f0c90c0f2308496ab90997f78488a) in extreme heat during the annual pilgrimage season.

5. **Hormuz shipping disruption.** Three oil and LNG tankers exited the Strait of Hormuz with their [transponders turned](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/three-oil-lng-tankers-exit-hormuz-with-transponders-off-2026-05-28/) off, Reuters reported. The development occurred during escalating US-Iran military exchanges near the crucial waterway that handles roughly one-fifth of global oil shipments.

6. **Saudi Yemen aid.** Saudi Arabia approved urgent support worth $150 million in oil derivatives to Yemen through the Saudi Development and Reconstruction Program, IRG Minister Mohammed Al-Eryani announced on Twitter. The aid comes as Yemen faces ongoing humanitarian challenges following years of civil war between IRG forces and Houthi/Ansar Allah fighters.

7. **Iran internet restrictions.** Iran expanded [restrictions on](https://apnews.com/article/iran-media-restrictions-reporting-distribution-ec8938bd08663f934fcf302695eeac99) international media content distribution while ending an internet shutdown that had left citizens feeling "like prisoners being released," the BBC reported. Iranian Supreme Leader Khamenei outlined a post-war economic roadmap for the country's parliament, according to Times of Oman.

8. **UAE tourism growth.** Sheikh Mohammed bin Rashid reviewed the UAE tourism sector's achievements for 2025, UAE Government Media Office announced on Twitter. The briefing highlighted continued economic diversification efforts as the UAE prepares for the 10th anniversary of Vision 2030, Princess Reema noted in her social media posts.

9. **Qatar diplomatic appointments.** Qatar's new Prime Minister Mohammed bin Abdulrahman Al Thani appointed Majed Al Ansari as advisor and official spokesman for the Foreign Ministry. Al Ansari denied reports that Qatar offered $12 billion to Iran for a diplomatic deal, calling the claims false and designed to sabotage regional de-escalation efforts.

10. **Oman-Iran dialogue.** Oman's Foreign Minister Badr Albusaidi held discussions with Iran's Deputy Foreign Minister, describing them as reflecting "constructive nature" of bilateral dialogue on Twitter. Prince William received Oman's Culture Minister in London to launch the "Oman: The Jewel of Arabia" expedition project, strengthening UK-Oman cultural ties.
