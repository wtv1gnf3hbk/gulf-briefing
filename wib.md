Gulf Bureau Briefing — May 30, 2026

1. **US-Iran negotiations.** The US and Iran reached a tentative deal extending the current ceasefire and starting new nuclear talks, according to AP reports. Trump has yet to make a "final determination" on whether to proceed with the agreement, the BBC reported. Saudi Foreign Minister Prince Faisal bin Farhan [praised Trump's](https://www.twitter.com/FaisalbinFarhan) decision to "give diplomacy a chance" to restore Strait of Hormuz security to pre-February 28 conditions.

2. **Hajj season concludes.** Saudi Arabia completed another successful Hajj season as pilgrims performed final farewell circumambulation rituals. GCC leaders congratulated King Salman and Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman on the achievement. According to official Twitter posts, nearly 2 million pilgrims completed the rituals. Interior Minister Prince Abdulaziz bin Saud met with security sector leaders and Hajj forces following the conclusion.

3. **Oman-Iran diplomatic coordination.** Oman's Foreign Minister Badr Albusaidi held discussions with Iran's Deputy Foreign Minister Gharibabadi, reaffirming the "constructive nature" of bilateral dialogue. The Times of Oman reported both countries committed to maintaining [Strait of Hormuz](https://timesofoman.com//article/172358-oman-and-iran-reaffirm-commitment-to-strait-of-hormuz-security) security cooperation. Oman pledged to continue supporting regional tension reduction efforts.

4. **Saudi oil pricing strategy.** Saudi Arabia may lower July oil prices to Asia as demand weakens, Reuters reported. The kingdom is adjusting its pricing strategy following Japan's April oil imports hitting their lowest level since 1962 due to Iran war disruptions. The move reflects broader regional energy market volatility following the recent conflict.

5. **UAE tourism achievements.** Sheikh Mohammed bin Rashid reviewed the UAE's tourism sector performance for 2025, according to government media offices. The Emirates also announced it leads globally in artificial intelligence talent concentration, with 121% growth between 2019 and 2025. Abu Dhabi highlighted employment opportunities for Emiratis through its Mawaheb Talent Hub.

6. **Qatar leadership transition.** PM Mohammed bin Abdulrahman Al Thani was formally appointed as Prime Minister by Emir Sheikh Tamim. Al Thani expressed gratitude for the trust placed in him and commitment to serving under the Emir's leadership. The appointment comes as Qatar continues its regional diplomatic role following the successful Hajj season.

7. **Yemen political developments.** IRG officials appointed Ahmed Hamid Lamlas as governor of Aden Governorate, according to Saba News Agency. Presidential Leadership Council Chairman Rashad Al-Alimi called for unity and focusing energies on restoring state institutions. The council received congratulations from Saudi leadership on Yemen's National Day.

8. **Bahrain fire rescue.** Bahrain's Civil Defence successfully rescued five children from a house fire in the Aali area without casualties. The Ministry of Interior confirmed investigations are underway to determine the fire's cause. The incident demonstrates the kingdom's emergency response capabilities.

9. **Kuwait Hajj delegation.** Kuwait's Hajj Mission chief praised Saudi Arabia's services and the successful management of this year's pilgrimage season. KUNA reported the delegation expressed appreciation for the kingdom's efforts in facilitating the pilgrimage. The commendation reflects broader GCC satisfaction with Saudi Hajj management.

10. **Regional diplomatic activity.** Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman met with Chad's President at the Royal Court in Mona Palace to discuss bilateral relations and cooperation prospects. The UAE's Tahnoon bin Zayed received US Representative Josh Gottheimer to discuss strategic relations between the Emirates and United States.
