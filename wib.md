**Iran nuclear talks.** US and Iranian negotiators reached a tentative deal to extend the ceasefire and start new nuclear talks, according to [AP News](https://apnews.com/article/iran-us-war-oil-may-28-2026-8f5ed2813ba63df7ae9ccbe991688d29). Saudi Foreign Minister Prince Faisal bin Farhan tweeted that the Kingdom "highly appreciates" Trump's decision to give diplomacy another chance to end the war and restore Strait of Hormuz navigation security. The agreement remains under review by President Trump, who has not yet made a final decision.

1. **Saudi Hajj operations.** Saudi Arabia concluded its 2026 Hajj season, with Interior Minister Prince Abdulaziz bin Saud congratulating King Salman and Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman on the success. GCC leaders, including Qatar's Emir and Oman's Sultan, sent congratulatory messages to the Saudi leadership, according to official Saudi Press Agency reports. Oman's Hajj mission won a silver award for excellence in pilgrim services from the [Times of Oman](https://timesofoman.com//article/172370-oman-hajj-mission-wins-silver-labaytum-award-for-excellence-in-pilgrim-services).

2. **Yemen political developments.** Yemeni IRG leadership announced the appointment of Ahmed Hamid Lamlas as governor of Aden, while former President Abd Rabbuh Mansour Hadi died and was buried in a state funeral attended by Presidential Leadership Council members. Saudi Ambassador to Yemen Mohammed AlJabir tweeted that he will visit Sanaa with an Omani delegation to consolidate the truce and support prisoner exchanges.

3. **UAE economic growth.** The UAE economy expanded 6.2%, with GDP rising to AED 1.9 trillion in 2025, according to government media office announcements. Sheikh Mohammed bin Rashid reviewed tourism sector achievements for 2025 during a government retreat focused on artificial intelligence transformation. The UAE ranked first globally in AI talent concentration growth at 121% between 2019 and 2025.

4. **Qatar government changes.** Mohammed bin Abdulrahman Al Thani was appointed as Qatar's Prime Minister by Emir Sheikh Tamim, with Majed Al Ansari named as his advisor and official Foreign Ministry spokesman. The appointments follow the death of former Deputy Prime Minister Abdullah bin Hamad Al Attiyah, who received state condolences from senior officials.

5. **Bahrain-Iran tensions.** Bahrain strongly condemned Iranian attacks on Kuwait, according to the Foreign Ministry's official statements. The Kingdom expressed solidarity with Kuwait over what it characterized as Iranian territorial violations, reflecting broader regional tensions over Iran's military activities in the Gulf.

6. **Trump regional threats.** President Trump threatened to "blow up" Oman if Iran continues obstructing Strait of Hormuz shipping lanes, according to Arabic media reports from DW and CNN Arabic. Omani Foreign Minister Badr Albusaidi held discussions with Iranian Deputy Foreign Minister Gharibabadi on diplomatic engagement and regional coexistence efforts.

7. **Oman-UK relations.** Prince William received Omani Culture Minister Sayyid Theyazin bin Haitham Al Said at Windsor Castle, launching the "Oman: The Jewel of Arabia" exhibition project. The initiative builds on longstanding UK-Oman ties and cultural cooperation between the two nations, according to [Oman News Agency](http://n9.cl/adorv).

8. **Israeli operations expansion.** Israeli Prime Minister Netanyahu announced directing the IDF to increase control of Gaza to 70%, while Israel was placed on the UN sexual violence blacklist for the first time, the [BBC reported](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cx21le869n1o?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss). Israeli forces also struck Beirut and southern Lebanon following large-scale evacuation orders.

9. **Kuwait diplomatic activities.** Kuwait's leadership sent congratulations to Croatia's president on the country's Statehood Day, with messages from the Emir, Crown Prince, and Prime Minister. Somalia expressed solidarity with Kuwait regarding attacks on its territory, according to [Youm7](https://www.youm7.com/story/2026/5/30/%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B5%D9%88%D9%85%D8%A7%D9%84-%D8%AA%D8%B9%D8%B1%D8%A8-%D8%B9%D9%86-%D8%AA%D8%B6%D8%A7%D9%85%D9%86%D9%87%D8%A7-%D9%85%D8%B9-%D8%AF%D9%88%D9%84%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%83%D9%88%D9%8A%D8%AA-%D8%A5%D8%B2%D8%A7%D8%A1-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%87%D8%AC%D9%85%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D9%8A/7432367).
