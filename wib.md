**Hajj completion and US-Iran tensions.** Saudi Arabia declared the 1447 AH Hajj season successful as millions of pilgrims completed rituals in Mecca. Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman met with Chad's president during the annual Mina Palace reception, while regional leaders congratulated the kingdom on managing the pilgrimage. Prince Khalid bin Salman emphasized the kingdom's honor in serving the Two Holy Mosques.

**Yemen's former president dies.** Former Yemeni President Abdrabbuh Mansour Hadi [died at 80](https://apnews.com/article/yemen-expresident-abdrabbuh-mansour-hadi-died-b3afd7b3679af14d335578e9560c7cd8), according to AP News. IRG Information Minister Mohammed Al-Eryani mourned the "great fighter Field Marshal" who led Yemen's government mostly from exile following the 2014 Houthi takeover.

**Saudi envoy to visit Sanaa.** Saudi Yemen envoy Mohammed AlJabir announced he would visit Sanaa with an Omani delegation to consolidate the truce, support prisoner exchanges, and discuss dialogue between Yemeni factions. AlJabir emphasized that nearly 2 million Yemenis work safely in Saudi Arabia, transferring over $4 billion annually to support 15 million people in Yemen.

**US warns Oman over Strait tolls.** [Reuters reported](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/us-warns-oman-not-engage-facilitating-tolls-strait-hormuz-2026-05-28/) the US warned Oman against facilitating tolls for Strait of Hormuz passage. The warning comes as Trump reportedly considers a deal that could involve Iran reopening the strait within a month if terms are agreed, according to Iranian state television.

**Iran-US escalation continues.** Iran struck American targets after the US conducted fresh strikes on Iranian positions, according to the BBC. US officials said American forces hit Iran again following Trump's denial of any deal on Strait of Hormuz reopening. Iran said it targeted an American base in response.

**Saudi oil prices may drop.** [Reuters reported](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/saudi-arabia-may-lower-july-oil-prices-asia-demand-weakens-2026-05-29/) Saudi Arabia may lower July oil prices to Asia as demand weakens. The potential price reduction reflects broader concerns about global energy markets and regional supply disruptions affecting consumer confidence.

**Kuwait oil rises sharply.** Kuwait News Agency reported oil prices jumped $4.56 to $118.35 per barrel, reflecting market volatility following regional tensions. Kuwait's Prime Minister congratulated Saudi Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman on the successful Hajj season organization.

**UAE tourism achievements.** Sheikh Mohammed bin Rashid reviewed UAE tourism sector achievements for 2025, highlighting the country's continued economic diversification efforts. The UAE also announced it ranks first globally in artificial intelligence talent concentration, growing 121% between 2019 and 2025.

**Yemen road reopening.** IRG Presidential Leadership Council member Abu Zaraa announced the opening of the Marib-Sanaa Al-Fardha road, calling it progress toward dismantling the siege. The road opening could facilitate movement between IRG-controlled Marib and Houthi-controlled Sanaa.

**Al-Hudaydah developments.** Saudi envoy Mohammed AlJabir posted that "#Al-Hudaydah is liberated.....#Yemen is breathing," though details of the claim remain unclear. The port city has been contested between IRG-allied forces and Houthi/Ansar Allah forces throughout the conflict.
