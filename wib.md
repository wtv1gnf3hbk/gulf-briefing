**Gulf Bureau Briefing**
Monday, May 30, 2026

1. **Former Yemen President dies.** Abd Rabbuh Mansour Hadi died, [according to](https://spa.gov.sa/N2603513) Saudi Defense Minister Prince Khalid bin Salman, who conveyed condolences from King Salman and Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman. Hadi led Yemen's internationally recognized government from 2012 until 2022, when he transferred power to the Presidential Leadership Council.

2. **Hajj concludes successfully.** Saudi authorities completed the 2026 Hajj pilgrimage with 2.5 million health services provided to pilgrims, the [health ministry announced](https://www.youm7.com/story/2026/5/30/%D8%B5%D8%AD%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B3%D8%B9%D9%88%D8%AF%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%AA%D8%B9%D9%84%D9%86-%D8%AA%D9%82%D8%AF%D9%8A%D9%85-2-5-%D9%85%D9%84%D9%8A%D9%88%D9%86-%D8%AE%D8%AF%D9%85%D8%A9-%D8%B5%D8%AD%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D9%84%D9%84%D8%AD%D8%AC%D8%A7%D8%AC/7433062). The Haramain high-speed railway transported 1.9 million passengers during the pilgrimage. Regional leaders congratulated Saudi Arabia on the successful completion of the Islamic world's largest annual gathering.

3. **UAE economy grows 6.2%.** The UAE economy expanded 6.2% with GDP rising to AED 1.9 trillion during 2025, the [government announced](https://www.mubasher.info/news/4619456/1-9-%D8%AA%D8%B1%D9%8A%D9%84%D9%8A%D9%88%D9%86-%D8%AF%D8%B1%D9%87%D9%85-%D8%A7%D9%82%D8%AA%D8%B5%D8%A7%D8%AF-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A5%D9%85%D8%A7%D8%B1%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D9%8A%D8%B3%D8%AC%D9%84-%D9%86%D9%85%D9%88%D8%A7-6-2-%D9%81%D9%8A-2025/). The figures reflect continued economic diversification and expansion across multiple sectors in the federation.

4. **Oman floating mine detected.** Oman's Maritime Security Centre detected a suspected floating mine in the [Strait of Hormuz](https://timesofoman.com//article/172387-omans-maritime-security-centre-detects-suspected-floating-mine-in-strait-of-hormuz), raising concerns about navigation safety in the critical waterway. The discovery comes as regional tensions persist over maritime security in Gulf shipping lanes.

5. **Trump considers Iran approach.** President Trump pondered whether to pursue an Iran deal but has not yet decided, [AP reported](https://apnews.com/article/iran-us-war-ceasefire-nuclear-talks-cac5206df0f0c7b79fe9321c08d63096). Saudi Foreign Minister Prince Faisal bin Farhan welcomed Trump's decision to give diplomacy a chance to restore Strait of Hormuz navigation security to pre-February 2026 levels.

6. **Yemen ambassador visits Sanaa.** Saudi Yemen envoy Mohammed Al Jabir will visit Sanaa with an Omani delegation to consolidate the truce, support prisoner exchanges, and discuss dialogue between Yemeni components for a comprehensive political solution. The visit aims to build on the Kingdom's 2021 initiative to end the Yemen crisis.

7. **Bahrain condemns Iran attack.** The Kingdom of Bahrain strongly condemned an Iranian attack on Kuwait, the [foreign ministry said](https://bit.ly/4dAMr6j). The statement reflects growing Gulf concern over Iranian regional activities and their impact on maritime and border security.

8. **Oman launches lithium project.** Oman embraced a strategic project to produce lithium battery materials, [Al Arabiya reported](https://www.alarabiya.net/amp/aswaq/economy/2026/05/30/%D8%B9-%D9%85%D8%A7%D9%86-%D8%AA%D8%AD%D8%AA%D8%B6%D9%86-%D9%85%D8%B4%D8%B1%D9%88%D8%B9%D8%A7-%D8%A7%D8%B3%D8%AA%D8%B1%D8%A7%D8%AA%D9%8A%D8%AC%D9%8A%D8%A7-%D9%84%D8%A7%D9%86%D8%AA%D8%A7%D8%AC-%D9%85%D9%88%D8%A7%D8%AF-%D8%A8%D8%B7%D8%A7%D8%B1%D9%8A%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%84%D9%8A%D8%AB%D9%8A%D9%88%D9%85). The initiative represents part of Oman's economic diversification strategy and growing focus on renewable energy infrastructure.

9. **Qatar PM takes new role.** Mohammed bin Abdulrahman Al Thani was honored by Emir Tamim's appointment as Prime Minister, expressing commitment to serving the nation under wise leadership. The appointment maintains continuity in Qatar's foreign policy approach during regional diplomatic developments.

10. **Trump criticism of Oman.** Times of Oman responded to President Trump's statement against Oman, calling it "not a passing mistake but a [historical sin](https://timesofoman.com//article/172388-president-donald-trump-your-statement-against-oman-is-not-a-passing-mistake-it-is-a-historical-sin)." The strongly worded editorial reflects Muscat's displeasure with perceived unfair characterization of its neutrality-focused foreign policy.
