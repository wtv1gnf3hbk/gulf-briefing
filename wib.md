**Saudi-Iran tensions.** Saudi Arabia condemned Iranian missile attacks on Kuwait as "sinful and repeated" in a Ministry of Foreign Affairs statement posted on X. The attacks prompted sirens in Kuwait City following US strikes on Iranian military sites. Prince Khalid bin Salman conveyed Saudi [leadership condolences](https://apnews.com/article/iran-us-irael-war-kuwait-strikes-88daa9f90b48baaa7beb18e35515c59d) to former Yemeni President Abd Rabbuh Mansour Hadi's family.

**OPEC production outlook.** OPEC+ is likely to raise its July oil output target despite disruptions in the Strait of Hormuz, [Reuters reports](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/opec-likely-raise-july-oil-output-target-despite-hormuz-disruption-sources-say-2026-06-01/). Asian imports of US crude have surged but cannot fully offset losses from Hormuz shipping routes. Saudi Foreign Minister Prince Faisal bin Farhan praised Trump's decision to give diplomacy a chance to restore Red Sea navigation.

**US-Iran military escalation.** US forces bombed Iranian military sites after Tehran fired missiles at American troops stationed in Kuwait, according to [AP News](https://apnews.com/article/iran-us-irael-war-kuwait-strikes-88daa9f90b48baaa7beb18e35515c59d). The BBC reports Iran has damaged 20 US military sites since the conflict began. Bahrain banned its citizens from traveling to Iran and Iraq due to security tensions.

**Kuwait security operation.** Kuwaiti authorities arrested three escaped prisoners, including two sentenced to death and a drug smuggler, [per Independent Arabia](https://www.independentarabia.com/node/650268/%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A3%D8%AE%D8%A8%D8%A7%D8%B1/%D9%85%D8%AD%D9%83%D9%88%D9%85%D8%A7%D9%86-%D8%A8%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A5%D8%B9%D8%AF%D8%A7%D9%85-%D9%88%D9%85%D9%87%D8%B1%D8%A8-%D9%85%D8%AE%D8%AF%D8%B1%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%AA%D9%81%D8%A7%D8%B5%D9%8A%D9%84-%D9%87%D8%B1%D9%88%D8%A8-3-%D8%B3%D8%AC%D9%86%D8%A7%D8%A1-%D9%88%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%82%D8%A8%D8%B6-%D8%B9%D9%84%D9%8A%D9%87%D9%85-%D9%81%D9%8A-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%83%D9%88%D9%8A%D8%AA). Kuwait News Agency confirmed the swift security operation captured all fugitives. Two firearms were seized during the arrests.

**Yemen political developments.** The IRG appointed Ahmed Hamid Lamlas as governor of Aden, while President Al-Alimi renewed calls for Saudi Arabia to halt UAE interference in southern Yemen. The IRG condemned UAE airstrikes on National Army forces in Aden and Abyan. Mohammed Al-Eryani visited the family of [deceased former](https://www.reuters.com/pictures/journey-mecca-scenes-annual-hajj-pilgrimage-2026-06-01/) President Abd Rabbuh Mansour Hadi.

**Oman business expansion.** The Oman India Fertiliser Company will offer 25% of its shares for public subscription on the Muscat Securities Market, [Times of Oman reports](https://timesofoman.com//article/172494-omifco-to-offer-25-of-its-shares-for-public-subscription-on-msx). **UAE tech initiatives.** The UAE achieved 121% growth in artificial intelligence talent concentration between 2019 and 2025, ranking first globally. Abu Dhabi Gaming partnered with Aspire to advance AI-driven game development projects. Sheikh Mohammed bin Rashid launched artificial intelligence assistants for government procurement and customer services.
