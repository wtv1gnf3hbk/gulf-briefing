**World in Brief — Gulf Region**

1. **Saudi Hajj completion.** Saudi Arabia declared the 2026 Hajj season successful as pilgrims completed their rituals. Prince Khalid bin Salman conveyed leadership condolences to families of former Yemeni President Abd Rabbuh Mansour Hadi following his death. Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman met Chad's president to discuss [bilateral relations](https://timesofoman.com//article/172387-omans-maritime-security-centre-detects-suspected-floating-mine-in-strait-of-hormuz) and regional cooperation.

2. **Iran-US diplomacy window.** Foreign Minister Prince Faisal bin Farhan praised President Trump's decision to give diplomacy another opportunity to end regional conflicts. Saudi Arabia expressed appreciation for extending negotiations to restore security and freedom of navigation in the Strait of Hormuz to pre-February 2026 conditions. The [BBC reported](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c87qng40wz9o) US and Iran remain "very close" to a deal.

3. **Maritime security concerns.** Oman's Maritime Security Centre detected a suspected floating mine in the Strait of Hormuz, according to [Times of Oman](https://timesofoman.com//article/172387-omans-maritime-security-centre-detects-suspected-floating-mine-in-strait-of-hormuz). The discovery comes during heightened regional tensions affecting critical shipping lanes. Yemen's Houthi naval forces continued operations in the Red Sea, with spokesman Mohammed Abdulsalam defending maritime actions as legitimate resistance.

4. **UAE economic growth.** The UAE announced 6.2% economic growth with GDP rising to AED 1.9 trillion in 2025, according to government media office statements. Sheikh Mohammed bin Rashid reviewed tourism sector achievements and launched new artificial intelligence initiatives. Abu Dhabi buildings illuminated in orange for World Multiple Sclerosis Day awareness campaigns.

5. **Yemen political developments.** The IRG appointed Ahmed Hamid Lamlas as Aden governor while condemning UAE airstrikes on National Army forces. Saudi ambassador Mohammed AlJabir announced plans to visit Sana'a with an Omani delegation to consolidate ceasefire efforts. Houthi leader Abdul-Malik Al-Houthi issued Eid Al-Adha statements defending [Yemeni operations](https://timesofoman.com//article/172388-president-donald-trump-your-statement-against-oman-is-not-a-passing-mistake-it-is-a-historical-sin).

6. **Qatar leadership changes.** Sheikh Mohammed bin Abdulrahman Al Thani expressed honor at his appointment as Prime Minister by Emir Sheikh Tamim. Majed Al Ansari was appointed as advisor to the Deputy Prime Minister and Foreign Ministry spokesman. Qatar's leadership congratulated regional partners on successful Hajj season completion.

7. **Bahrain diplomatic activity.** Bahrain condemned Iranian attacks on Kuwait, with the Foreign Ministry issuing strong statements. Crown Prince Salman bin Hamad met with Malaysia's newly appointed Prime Minister and Japan's Crown Prince. Civil Defense rescued five children from a house fire in Aali without casualties.

8. **Kuwait regional developments.** American soldiers reportedly sustained injuries following Iranian attacks on Kuwait, according to Egyptian media reports. Kuwait Airways received the first return flight of pilgrims at Terminal 4. A celestial alignment captivated onlookers as the full moon positioned above a [mosque minaret](https://www.reuters.com/business/) crescent.

9. **Oman diplomatic engagement.** Sultan Haitham bin Tarik offered Eid prayers at Sultan Qaboos Mosque in Sohar. Foreign Minister Badr Albusaidi met with Iran's Deputy Foreign Minister, emphasizing constructive dialogue. Prince William received Sayyid Theyazin bin Haitham to discuss UK-Oman relations and launch the "Oman: Jewel of Arabia" expedition project.

10. **Trump statement controversy.** Oman criticized President Trump's recent statements about the sultanate, with [Times of Oman](https://timesofoman.com//article/172388-president-donald-trump-your-statement-against-oman-is-not-a-passing-mistake-it-is-a-historical-sin) calling his remarks "not a passing mistake but a historical sin." The criticism comes during sensitive regional diplomatic negotiations involving multiple Gulf states.
