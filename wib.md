**Iran-US escalation.** The United States bombed Iranian military sites after Iran fired missiles at American forces based in Kuwait, according to the BBC. AP News reports sirens sounded over Kuwait City following the US strikes. Saudi Arabia's foreign ministry condemned Iran's [repeated attacks](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/crlpy8n7pr6o) on Kuwait.

**Saudi condemnation of Israeli strikes.** The Kingdom's foreign ministry condemned Israeli aggression on Lebanese territory and rejected Israel's incursion into Lebanon. Prince Faisal bin Farhan said Saudi Arabia appreciates US President Trump's decision to give diplomacy a chance to reach an acceptable agreement to [end the war](https://spa.gov.sa/N2604388).

**Former Yemeni president dies.** Abd Rabbuh Mansour Hadi, former president of the Republic of Yemen, died. Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman offered condolences by telephone to Hadi's son Nasser, calling the former leader a wise man keen on his country's security and [stability](https://spa.gov.sa/N2604388).

**OPEC production uncertainty.** OPEC+ is likely to raise July oil output targets despite disruption in the Strait of Hormuz, according to Reuters sources. Oil prices rose more than 2% as Israel moved further into Lebanon and US-Iran tensions [escalated](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/oil-prices-rise-more-than-2-israel-moves-further-into-lebanon-2026-05-31/).

**Saudi Aramco pricing.** Saudi Aramco raised LPG official selling prices by up to 3% for June, while Algeria's Sonatrach cut prices by 18% and 31%, Reuters reports. The moves reflect different regional market [dynamics](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/saudi-aramco-raises-lpg-osps-by-up-3-june-sonatrach-cuts-prices-by-18-31-2026-06-01/).

**UAE AI leadership.** The UAE ranked first globally in artificial intelligence talent concentration, growing 121% between 2019 and 2025. Sheikh Abdullah bin Zayed called this growth a reflection of leadership vision that makes building people and creating the future a [national approach](https://twitter.com/ABZayed).

**Oman cybersecurity law.** Sultan Haitham issued two royal decrees, with the first promulgating a new Law on Combating Cybercrime and the second amending provisions of the Cultural Heritage Law. The moves enhance Oman's [digital security](https://timesofoman.com/article/172462-new-cyberlaw-to-enhance-omans-digital-security) framework.

**Qatar PM appointment.** Sheikh Mohammed bin Abdulrahman Al Thani was appointed as Qatar's new Prime Minister by Emir Tamim bin Hamad Al Thani. The new PM expressed gratitude for the trust placed in him and looked forward to working with fellow ministers in serving the [nation](https://twitter.com/MBA_AlThani_).

**Bahrain house fire rescue.** Bahrain's Civil Defence rescued five children from a house fire in the Aali area without causing any injuries. Investigations are underway to determine the [cause](https://policemc.gov.bh/en/news/78966/) of the fire.

**Yemen envoy activities.** Saudi Ambassador Mohammed Al-Jabir announced plans to visit Sanaa with an Omani delegation to consolidate the truce and ceasefire, support prisoner exchanges, and discuss dialogue between Yemeni components for a comprehensive political [solution](https://twitter.com/mohdsalj).
