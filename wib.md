**Iran-US Exchange.** Iran fired ballistic missiles at US forces stationed in Kuwait, prompting US airstrikes on Iranian military radar sites according to the [BBC](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/crlpy8n7pr6o?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss). The Biden administration said the strikes targeted Iranian radar installations in response to attacks on American personnel. Saudi Arabia's Foreign Ministry condemned Iran's "sinful and repeated attacks" on Kuwait, according to the [Saudi MFA Twitter](https://twitter.com/KSAMOFA) account. Bahrain also condemned the Iranian attacks on its GCC neighbor.

**Israeli-Hezbollah Ceasefire.** US President Donald Trump announced that Israel and Hezbollah agreed to halt mutual attacks and cease fire exchanges, according to [KUNA](https://kuna.net.kw/ArticleDetails.aspx?id=3306090). The BBC reported that Lebanon said Hezbollah agreed to a reciprocal halt to attacks on Israel. Israel's Prime Minister ordered strikes on Beirut suburbs as the conflict escalated before the ceasefire announcement.

**Saudi Yemen Condolences.** Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman offered condolences to the family of former Yemeni President Abd Rabbuh Mansour Hadi following his death, according to the [Saudi Press Agency](https://spa.gov.sa/N2604388). Prince Khalid bin Salman conveyed condolences on behalf of Saudi leadership, calling Hadi "a wise leader who was keen on his country's security, stability, development and prosperity." Saudi Ambassador to Yemen Mohammed AlJabir also expressed condolences.

**OPEC Production Decision.** OPEC+ is likely to raise its July oil output target despite disruptions in the Strait of Hormuz, [Reuters reported](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/opec-likely-raise-july-oil-output-target-despite-hormuz-disruption-sources-say-2026-06-01/). Saudi Aramco raised LPG prices by up to 3% for June while Algeria's Sonatrach cut prices by 18% and 31%.

**Qatar-Iran Diplomacy.** Qatar discussed Pakistani mediation efforts and Lebanon developments with Iran, [Al Jazeera reported](https://www.aljazeera.net/news/2026/6/2/%D9%82%D8%B7%D8%B1-%D8%AA%D8%A8%D8%AD%D8%AB-%D9%85%D8%B9-%D8%A5%D9%8A%D8%B1%D8%A7%D9%86-%D8%AC%D9%87%D9%88%D8%AF-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%88%D8%B3%D8%A7%D8%B7%D8%A9). Qatar's Foreign Ministry warned that using the Strait of Hormuz as a pressure tactic deepens regional crisis. Prime Minister Al Thani was appointed by Emir Tamim bin Hamad, according to his [Twitter account](https://twitter.com/MBA_AlThani_).

**Oman Cybersecurity Law.** Sultan Haitham issued two royal decrees, including one promulgating a new law on combating cybercrime, [Oman News Agency reported](https://timesofoman.com//article/172462-new-cyberlaw-to-enhance-omans-digital-security). The second decree amended provisions of the Cultural Heritage Law. Oman's Foreign Minister Badr Albusaidi met with Iran's Deputy Foreign Minister, emphasizing diplomatic engagement.

**UAE Economic Growth.** The UAE economy is projected to grow 6.2% with GDP rising to 1.9 trillion dirhams in 2025, the [UAE Media Office announced](https://twitter.com/UAEmediaoffice). The UAE launched Naseej, a national textile circularity initiative to transition the sector toward a circular economic system. MGX expanded its Campus AI project in France through partnerships with Bpifrance and Mistral.

**Bahrain Civil Defense.** Bahrain's Civil Defense rescued five children from a house fire in Aali without injuries, according to the [Interior Ministry](https://policemc.gov.bh/en/news/78966/). Investigations are underway to determine the cause of the fire.

**Yemen Political Developments.** The IRG appointed Ahmed Hamid Lamlas as governor of Aden, [Saba News Agency reported](https://sabanew.net). The government condemned a UAE airstrike on National Army forces in Aden and Abyan. Yemen's Information Minister Al-Eryani thanked US Congressman Joe Wilson for introducing an amendment to strengthen US-Yemen defense cooperation.
