1. **US-Iran nuclear talks.** US and Iranian negotiators reached a tentative agreement to extend the current ceasefire and begin new nuclear discussions, AP [reports](https://apnews.com/article/iran-us-war-oil-may-28-2026-8f5ed2813ba63df7ae9ccbe991688d29). Vice President Vance said the parties are "very close" to a deal but "not there yet," according to the BBC. The agreement follows months of escalating tensions over Iran's oil shipments and naval operations in the Gulf.

2. **Hajj season concludes.** The 2026 Hajj pilgrimage concluded successfully, with regional leaders congratulating Saudi Arabia's King Salman and Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman. Foreign Minister Prince Faisal bin Farhan praised President Trump's [decision to give diplomacy a chance](https://www.timesofoman.com//article/172357-iran-war-us-to-lift-naval-blockade-of-ports-trump-says) to end the Iran conflict, according to his Twitter account. Qatar, Kuwait, Bahrain, and Oman's leadership sent congratulatory messages to Riyadh.

3. **Oman-Iran Strait coordination.** Oman and Iran reaffirmed their commitment to securing the Strait of Hormuz, Times of Oman [reports](https://timesofoman.com//article/172358-oman-and-iran-reaffirm-commitment-to-strait-of-hormuz-security). Foreign Minister Badr Albusaidi held discussions with Iran's Deputy Foreign Minister, reflecting "the constructive nature of Oman-Iran dialogue," he tweeted. The talks focused on diplomatic engagement and reducing regional tensions.

4. **Saudi oil pricing outlook.** Saudi Arabia may lower July oil prices to Asia as demand weakens, Reuters [reports](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/saudi-arabia-may-lower-july-oil-prices-asia-demand-weakens-2026-05-29/). The potential price adjustment reflects softer Asian demand and ongoing market volatility. Oil markets have been disrupted by the Iran conflict, with Japan's April imports hitting their lowest level since 1962.

5. **Qatar government reshuffle.** Qatar's Prime Minister Mohammed bin Abdulrahman Al Thani announced his appointment by Emir Tamim bin Hamad, expressing gratitude for the trust placed in him. Majed Al Ansari was appointed as advisor to the PM and official Foreign Ministry spokesman. The appointments come as Qatar continues its regional mediation efforts.

6. **Yemen political developments.** Saudi Ambassador to Yemen Mohammed Al Jabir announced plans to visit Sanaa with an Omani delegation to consolidate the truce and discuss prisoner exchanges, according to his Twitter account. The IRG government appointed Ahmed Hamid Lamlas as governor of Aden, while the Southern Transitional Council declared support for southern forces' security operations.

7. **UAE tourism sector review.** Sheikh Mohammed bin Rashid reviewed the UAE's 2025 tourism sector achievements, according to the UAE Media Office. The country recorded a 121% growth in artificial intelligence talent concentration between 2019 and 2025, placing it first globally. The data reflects the Emirates' focus on technological advancement and economic diversification.

8. **Bahrain civil defense operation.** Bahrain's Civil Defense rescued five children from a house fire in Aali, according to the Interior Ministry. The operation was completed without casualties, with investigations ongoing to determine the cause of the blaze. The incident highlights the kingdom's emergency response capabilities during the holiday period.

9. **Kuwait Hajj mission praise.** Kuwait's Hajj Mission chief lauded Saudi Arabia's services during this year's pilgrimage season, KUNA [reports](http://ow.ly/AV0X30sW8c5). Minister Al-Wasmi specifically praised the kingdom's efforts in serving pilgrims. Nearly 2 million Yemenis work in Saudi Arabia, transferring over $4 billion annually to their families, Saudi Ambassador Al Jabir noted.

10. **Oman cultural initiative.** Prince William received Oman's Minister of Culture Sayyid Theyazin bin Haitham Al Said at Windsor Castle to launch the "Oman: The Jewel of Arabia" expedition project in London. The initiative reflects strengthening UK-Oman ties and cultural exchange efforts. Sultan Haitham bin Tarik performed Eid Al Adha prayers at Sultan Qaboos Mosque in Sohar.
