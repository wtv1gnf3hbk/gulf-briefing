1. **US-Iran nuclear talks.** The US and Iranian negotiators reached [a tentative deal](https://apnews.com/article/iran-us-war-oil-may-28-2026-8f5ed2813ba63df7ae9ccbe991688d29) to extend a ceasefire and start new nuclear talks, according to AP News. Vice President Vance said the US was ["very close" to a deal](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c87qng40wz9o?at_medium=RSS&amp;at_campaign=rss) with Iran but "not there yet." Saudi Foreign Minister Prince Faisal tweeted that the Kingdom "highly appreciates" President Trump's decision to give diplomacy a chance to restore Strait of Hormuz security.

2. **Saudi Hajj season concludes.** Saudi Arabia declared the [2026 Hajj season successful](https://timesofoman.com//article/172355-hm-congratulates-saudi-leadership-for-successful-hajj-season-1447-ah), with Gulf leaders congratulating the Kingdom. Prince Khalid bin Salman thanked God for the honor of serving the Two Holy Mosques, while Princess Reema showcased Saudi preparations for the 2026 FIFA World Cup. Oman's Sultan Haitham congratulated Saudi leadership on the pilgrimage's success.

3. **Yemen Leadership Council calls for unity.** IRG President Rashad Al-Alimi called for unity of ranks and directing energies toward restoring state institutions, according to [Saba News Agency](https://www.sabanew.net/news/78966). Ahmed Hamid Lamlas was appointed as governor of Aden Governorate. The IRG also renewed its request to Saudi Arabia to intervene to stop Emirati interference in southern Yemen.

4. **UAE artificial intelligence leadership.** The UAE ranked first globally in artificial intelligence talent concentration, growing 121% between 2019 and 2025, according to [Sheikh Abdulla bin Zayed](https://x.com/ABZayed/status/1843035816370802812). The Emirates launched AI assistants for procurement, taxes, and customer services as part of its federal AI program, with Sheikh Mohammed bin Rashid overseeing government AI transformation.

5. **Saudi oil pricing adjustments.** Saudi Arabia may [lower July oil prices](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/saudi-arabia-may-lower-july-oil-prices-asia-demand-weakens-2026-05-29/) to Asia as demand weakens, Reuters reports. Japan's April oil imports hit the lowest level since 1962 as the Iran war disrupted supply chains. The pricing adjustment reflects broader market pressures following regional conflict disruptions.

6. **Oman-Iran maritime cooperation.** Oman and Iran [reaffirmed commitment](https://timesofoman.com//article/172358-oman-and-iran-reaffirm-commitment-to-strait-of-hormuz-security) to Strait of Hormuz security during diplomatic meetings. Omani Foreign Minister Badr Albusaidi held "good discussions" with Iran's Deputy FM, reflecting the constructive nature of Oman-Iran dialogue and regional coexistence efforts.

7. **Qatar Prime Minister appointment.** Mohammed bin Abdulrahman Al Thani was appointed as Qatar's new Prime Minister by [Emir Tamim](https://x.com/MBA_AlThani_), expressing gratitude for the trust and commitment to serve the nation. The appointment comes during a period of regional diplomatic activity and Gulf cooperation initiatives.

8. **Houthi-IRG prisoner exchange talks.** Mohammed Abdulsalam announced an agreement to cancel banking decisions on both sides and resume Yemenia Airlines flights between Sanaa and Jordan, according to his [official Twitter account](https://x.com/abdusalamsalah). The Houthis agreed to increase flights to three daily and operate routes to Cairo and India as needed.

9. **Bahrain condemns Iranian attack.** Bahrain [strongly condemned](https://bit.ly/4dAMr6j) the Iranian attack on Kuwait, with the EU also condemning the latest Iranian aggression, according to KUNA. The condemnation reflects Gulf solidarity following recent Iranian military actions in the region.

10. **Kuwait security operations.** Kuwait's Interior Ministry arrested suspects attempting to [smuggle 12 kilograms](http://ow.ly/eMgs30sW7VH) of methamphetamine, KUNA reports. The Public Authority for Civil Aviation announced that return flights of Hajj pilgrims would arrive home Saturday and Sunday, completing the seasonal pilgrimage transportation operations.
