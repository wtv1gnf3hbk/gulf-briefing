**Gulf News Briefing**

1. **US-Iran ceasefire talks.** The United States and Iran reached a tentative ceasefire agreement following recent military escalations, according to [multiple reports](https://apnews.com/article/iran-us-war-oil-may-28-2026-8f5ed2813ba63df7ae9ccbe991688d29). Saudi Foreign Minister Prince Faisal bin Farhan welcomed President Trump's decision to "give diplomacy a chance" and restore Strait of Hormuz maritime security. However, Trump said the US remains "not satisfied" with the deal's current terms.

2. **Iranian attacks on Kuwait.** Kuwait's military said its air defenses intercepted hostile missile and drone attacks, with Reuters reporting the [strikes targeted](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/kuwaiti-army-says-air-defences-intercepting-hostile-missile-drone-attacks-2026-05-28/) Kuwait. Egypt's foreign minister condemned Iran's "blatant" attacks in a phone call with his Kuwaiti counterpart. Saudi Arabia and Bahrain both strongly condemned the Iranian strikes on Kuwait.

3. **Yemen leadership transition.** Former Yemeni President Abd Rabbuh Mansour Hadi died, according to announcements from the Saudi Press Agency and Yemeni officials. The Saudi royal court offered condolences to "the government and people of the brotherly Republic of Yemen." His death comes as rival Yemeni factions continue battling for control of the country.

4. **New UAE Prime Minister.** Mohammed bin Abdulrahman Al Thani was appointed as Qatar's new Prime Minister by Emir Sheikh Tamim bin Hamad Al Thani. Al Thani said he was "honored by the trust" and looked forward to "working with my fellow ministers in serving our nation and contributing to its advancement under the wise leadership of His Highness."

5. **Strait of Hormuz shipping disruptions.** Three oil and LNG tankers exited the Strait of Hormuz with their transponders turned off, Reuters reported, indicating continued maritime [security concerns](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/three-oil-lng-tankers-exit-hormuz-with-transponders-off-2026-05-28/) in the critical energy shipping lane. The development follows recent US-Iran military exchanges and ongoing negotiations over Hormuz passage rights.

6. **Saudi Vision 2030 anniversary.** Princess Reema bin Bandar highlighted Saudi Arabia's "unwavering commitment not only to its own progress, but to the prosperity, stability" of the region as the kingdom marks 10 years of Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman's Vision 2030 reform program. The milestone comes as Saudi Arabia continues diversifying away from oil dependency.

7. **UAE artificial intelligence expansion.** Sheikh Mohammed bin Rashid launched the first batch of AI assistants for government procurement, taxes, customer service, and technical support during a federal AI program retreat in Abu Dhabi. The initiative aims to transform "half of the government, its services and operations into intelligence" with over 400 officials participating.

8. **Hajj celebrations continue.** Omani pilgrims celebrated Eid Al-Adha in Mina with traditional customs as the annual Hajj pilgrimage proceeded in Saudi Arabia's holy sites. AP News reported pilgrims [performed rituals](https://apnews.com/article/islam-hajj-mina-saudi-arabia-muslim-908f0c90c0f2308496ab90997f78488a) in soaring heat as Eid Al-Adha celebrations began. Saudi authorities issued administrative decisions against 12 violators of Hajj regulations.

9. **Saudi Yemen aid package.** Saudi Arabia provided $150 million in urgent oil derivatives support to Yemen through the Saudi Development and Reconstruction Program, according to the Saudi Press Agency. The aid comes as Yemen faces continued humanitarian challenges following years of conflict between government forces, Houthis, and southern separatists.

10. **Air cargo demand rises.** Air cargo demand increased 4% in April despite Middle East disruptions, the International Air Transport Association reported. The growth occurred as regional conflicts and maritime security concerns in the Red Sea and Gulf have [disrupted shipping](https://timesofoman.com//article/172334-air-cargo-demand-rises-4-in-april-despite-middle-east-disruptions-iata) routes, forcing cargo to shift to air transport.
