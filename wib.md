**Gulf Regional Briefing — June 2, 2026**

1. **US-Iran military escalation.** The US bombed Iranian military sites after Iran fired missiles at American troops stationed in Kuwait, according to [the BBC](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/crlpy8n7pr6o). Reuters reports that Iran damaged 20 US military sites since the conflict began. Saudi Arabia's Foreign Ministry condemned the "sinful and repeated Iranian attacks" on Kuwait, while Bahrain banned citizens from traveling to Iran and Iraq due to ongoing security tensions.

2. **Yemen former president dies.** Abd Rabbuh Mansour Hadi, former president of Yemen's internationally recognized government (IRG), died, prompting condolences from across the region. Prince Khalid bin Salman conveyed Saudi leadership's sympathies to Hadi's family, describing him as "a wise leader keen on his country's security and stability." The death removes a key figure from Yemen's complex political landscape.

3. **OPEC+ oil production plans.** OPEC+ is likely to raise its July oil output target despite disruptions in the Strait of Hormuz, [Reuters reports](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/opec-likely-raise-july-oil-output-target-despite-hormuz-disruption-sources-say-2026-06-01/). Saudi Aramco separately raised LPG prices by up to 3% for June. The production decision comes as maritime tensions threaten key shipping lanes in the Gulf.

4. **Saudi-Iran ship targeting.** Al Jazeera reports that navigational data shows a ship targeted by Iran in the Gulf was heading to Qatar. The incident adds to concerns about maritime security in vital shipping lanes. Saudi Foreign Minister Prince Faisal bin Farhan praised President Trump's decision to give diplomacy "a chance to reach an acceptable agreement" to restore Strait of Hormuz navigation security.

5. **Oman global trade ranking.** Oman ranked first globally in the Export Supply Chain Start-Up Index, according to [Times of Oman](https://timesofoman.com//article/172480-oman-ranks-first-globally-in-export-supply-chain-start-up-index). The sultanate also announced that state energy company OQ signed an energy pact expanding into Libya. Two royal decrees were issued covering cybercrime law and cultural heritage amendments.

6. **UAE AI leadership.** The UAE leads globally in artificial intelligence talent concentration, growing 121% between 2019 and 2025, according to Sheikh Abdulla bin Zayed. Emirates NBD ranked first in the inaugural AI Index for Banks in the Middle East and Africa region. The government launched new AI assistants for procurement, taxes, and customer services as part of federal digitization efforts.

7. **Qatar leadership changes.** Sheikh Mohammed bin Abdulrahman Al Thani was appointed as Qatar's new Prime Minister by Emir Sheikh Tamim, replacing the previous government. Majed Al Ansari was simultaneously named as advisor and official foreign ministry spokesman. The leadership reshuffle comes as Qatar continues regional diplomatic engagement.

8. **Kuwait Crown Prince anniversary.** Multiple governors praised Crown Prince Sheikh Sabah Al-Ahmad's second anniversary in office, highlighting his "distinguished political and diplomatic career." The Interior Ministry announced it had apprehended escaped prisoners through "high efficiency and intensive security operations."

9. **Yemen economic developments.** Nearly 2 million Yemenis work in Saudi Arabia and transfer over $4 billion annually to Yemen, according to Saudi ambassador Mohammed AlJabir. The IRG government condemned UAE airstrikes on National Army forces in Aden and Abyan. Houthi spokesman Mohammed Abdulsalam announced an agreement covering bank decisions and Yemenia Airlines flight resumptions.

10. **Saudi wildlife milestone.** The first birth of an onager (wild ass) on Saudi soil in over 100 years was recorded at Prince Mohammed bin Salman Royal Reserve, Princess Reema announced. The birth represents a significant conservation achievement for the kingdom's wildlife restoration efforts. Saudi authorities also arrested 18 environmental law violators across multiple regions.
