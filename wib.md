1. **Eid Al-Adha celebrations.** Gulf leaders congratulated citizens and pilgrims following the conclusion of Hajj 1447 AH. Saudi Arabia's King Salman and Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman received congratulatory messages from regional leaders, with [Prince Khalid bin Salman](https://www.timesofoman.com//article/172388-president-donald-trump-your-statement-against-oman-is-not-a-passing-mistake-it-is-a-historical-sin) expressing gratitude for serving the Two Holy Mosques and the pilgrims.

2. **Saudi Arabia-Chad diplomacy.** Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman met with Chad's President at the Royal Court, reviewing bilateral relations and cooperation prospects in various fields. The meeting also addressed [regional issues](https://www.timesofoman.com//article/172388-president-donald-trump-your-statement-against-oman-is-not-a-passing-mistake-it-is-a-historical-sin) of mutual concern and ways to enhance strategic partnerships between the two nations.

3. **Yemen Presidential Leadership Council changes.** The IRG announced the appointment of Ahmed Hamid Lamlas as governor of Aden Governorate. This follows internal tensions within the Presidential Leadership Council over individual decision-making processes, according to member [Abu Zaraa](https://www.timesofoman.com//article/172388-president-donald-trump-your-statement-against-oman-is-not-a-passing-mistake-it-is-a-historical-sin).

4. **Iranian mine threat in Hormuz Strait.** Oman's Maritime Security Centre detected a suspected floating mine in the Strait of Hormuz, according to the [Times of Oman](https://timesofoman.com//article/172387-omans-maritime-security-centre-detects-suspected-floating-mine-in-strait-of-hormuz). The discovery highlights ongoing maritime security concerns in the critical shipping lane.

5. **Saudi Foreign Minister's US-Iran diplomacy.** Prince Faisal bin Farhan commended President Trump's decision to give diplomacy another chance to end the war and restore maritime security in the Strait of Hormuz to pre-February 2026 conditions. He emphasized the need for [comprehensive solutions](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c87qng40wz9o?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss) addressing all regional disagreements.

6. **UAE AI leadership announcement.** The UAE reported leading globally in artificial intelligence talent concentration, with 121% growth between 2019 and 2025. According to Sheikh Abdullah bin Zayed, this reflects the leadership's [vision of building](https://www.timesofoman.com//article/172388-president-donald-trump-your-statement-against-oman-is-not-a-passing-mistake-it-is-a-historical-sin) human capital and creating the future as a national priority.

7. **Yemen former President Hadi's death.** Former Yemeni President Abd Rabbuh Mansour Hadi passed away, prompting condolences from Saudi leadership. Prince Khalid bin Salman conveyed sympathies to Hadi's family and the Yemeni people, describing him as a [wise leader](https://www.timesofoman.com//article/172388-president-donald-trump-your-statement-against-oman-is-not-a-passing-mistake-it-is-a-historical-sin) committed to Yemen's security and prosperity.

8. **Bahrain Civil Defense rescue operation.** Five children were rescued from a house fire in the Aali area without casualties. Civil Defense forces successfully extinguished the blaze, with investigations underway to determine the [fire's cause](https://www.timesofoman.com//article/172388-president-donald-trump-your-statement-against-oman-is-not-a-passing-mistake-it-is-a-historical-sin).

9. **Qatar diplomatic appointments.** Prime Minister Mohammed bin Abdulrahman Al Thani appointed Dr. Majed Al Ansari as advisor and official spokesman for the Ministry of Foreign Affairs. The appointment reinforces Qatar's [diplomatic communications](https://www.timesofoman.com//article/172388-president-donald-trump-your-statement-against-oman-is-not-a-passing-mistake-it-is-a-historical-sin) structure following Al Thani's recent appointment as Prime Minister.

10. **Kuwait missile incident.** American soldiers were injured from falling debris after an Iranian missile attack in Kuwait last Wednesday, according to [Al-Ahram Gate](https://gate.ahram.org.eg/News/5664173.aspx). The incident underscores regional tensions and risks to military personnel stationed in the Gulf.
