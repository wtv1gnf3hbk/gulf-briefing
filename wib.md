**Gulf Daily Brief**

1. **Iran-US conflict escalates.** Tehran rejected President Trump's deadline to reopen the Strait of Hormuz, as Iranian attacks targeted UAE infrastructure and killed two in Haifa, according to [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/liveblog/2026/4/6/iran-war-live-tehran-rejects-trumps-tuesday-deadline-on-strait-of-hormuz?traffic_source=rss). UAE defense systems intercepted Iranian missiles and drones, with shrapnel injuring one person in Abu Dhabi's industrial zone.

2. **OPEC+ raised oil quotas.** The cartel agreed to boost output when the Strait of Hormuz reopens, expressing concern over attacks on energy infrastructure, [Reuters reports](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/opec-debates-theoretical-oil-output-hike-amid-iran-war-paralysis-sources-say-2026-04-05/). Oil prices continued rising as the US-Israeli war with Iran disrupted supply chains.

3. **Saudi non-oil activity contracted.** The kingdom's purchasing managers' index showed business activity shrank in March as regional conflict weighed on the private sector, according to [Reuters data](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/saudi-arabias-non-oil-business-activity-shrinks-march-amid-conflict-pmi-shows-2026-04-05/). Most Gulf stock markets remained subdued following the escalation.

4. **Three Gulf funds backed Warner takeover.** Paramount secured Gulf financial backing for its $81 billion Warner acquisition, the [Wall Street Journal reported](https://www.reuters.com/business/media-telecom/three-gulf-funds-agree-back-paramounts-81-billion-takeover-warner-wsj-reports-2026-04-05/). The deal represents significant Gulf investment in Western media assets.

5. **US rescued airman from Iran.** American forces extracted a downed pilot from Iranian territory as Tehran stepped up attacks on neighboring countries, [Bloomberg reported](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-04-05/missing-us-airman-rescued-after-jet-downed-in-iran-axios). President Trump declared victory but warned of continued threats to US operations.

6. **Pakistan repaid UAE loan deposits.** Islamabad confirmed it would honor matured deposit obligations to the Emirates, Bloomberg reported, as financial pressures mounted following [regional instability](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-04-04/pakistan-says-it-will-repay-matured-loan-deposits-to-uae). The repayment demonstrates continued Gulf-South Asia financial ties.

8. **Syria investigated UAE embassy attack.** Damascus authorities launched a probe into an assault on the Emirates diplomatic mission, Al-Sharq newspaper reported, while Arab states [condemned the incident](https://asharq.com/politics/178153/%D8%B3%D9%88%D8%B1%D9%8A%D8%A7-%D8%AA%D8%AD%D9%82%D9%82-%D9%81%D9%8A-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%B9%D8%AA%D8%AF%D8%A7%D8%A1-%D8%B9%D9%84%D9%89-%D8%B3%D9%81%D8%A7%D8%B1%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A5%D9%85%D8%A7%D8%B1%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D9%88%D8%A5%D8%AF%D8%A7%D9%86%D8%A9-%D8%B9%D8%B1%D8%A8%D9%8A%D8%A9/).
