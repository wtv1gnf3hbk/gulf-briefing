1. **Former Yemeni president dies.** Abd Rabbuh Mansour Hadi, who served as Yemen's president from 2012-2022 and was backed by the Saudi-led coalition against the Iran-aligned Houthis, died at age 80, according to [AP reports](https://apnews.com/article/yemen-expresident-abdrabbuh-mansour-hadi-died-b3afd7b3679af14d335578e9560c7cd8). Yemen's internationally recognized government mourned the "great fighter" who led during the country's civil war.

2. **Iran strikes Kuwait.** Iran launched a ballistic missile at Kuwait, prompting widespread condemnation from Arab states, according to [CNN Arabic reports](https://arabic.cnn.com/middle-east/article/2026/05/28/iranian-missile-targeted-kuwait-centcom-confirms). Egypt, Bahrain, and the Arab League strongly denounced the attack as a violation of Kuwaiti sovereignty, with Kuwait's foreign minister receiving solidarity calls from regional counterparts.

3. **Saudi Crown Prince chairs Eid reception.** Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman held the annual reception for Hajj dignitaries and pilgrims from 104 countries at the conclusion of this year's pilgrimage, according to Saudi state media. The kingdom arrested 12 violators for transporting 43 unauthorized pilgrims, enforcing its "no Hajj without permit" policy.

4. **UAE advances AI government transformation.** Sheikh Mohammed bin Rashid launched the first batch of AI assistants for government procurement, taxes, and customer services as part of transforming half the UAE government operations to artificial intelligence, according to his [official account](https://twitter.com/HHShkMohd). The initiative involves more than 400 officials in a national retreat in Abu Dhabi.

5. **Qatar PM discusses regional tensions with Trump.** Emir Sheikh Tamim received a phone call from US President Trump discussing regional developments, with the Qatari leader stressing the need for diplomatic solutions to consolidate regional security, according to [Qatar News Agency](https://ow.ly/PxeG50Z5lZl). Trump voiced appreciation for Qatar's mediation efforts.

6. **Saudi foreign minister chairs Palestine conference.** Prince Faisal bin Farhan co-chaired an international conference in partnership with France on peaceful resolution of the Palestinian issue and implementing the two-state solution, according to his [Twitter account](https://twitter.com/FaisalbinFarhan). He commended France's resolve to recognize Palestine as supporting a just peace.

7. **UAE restores Damascus mosque.** The UAE announced restoration of the historic Umayyad Mosque in Damascus with support from Sheikha Fatima bint Mubarak, reflecting the country's commitment to preserving Arab and Islamic heritage and supporting Syria, according to [Sheikh Abdullah bin Zayed](https://twitter.com/ABZayed).

8. **Oman launches UK exhibition project.** Oman officially launched the "Oman: The Jewel of Arabia" expedition project in London under patronage of Minister Sayyid Theyazin bin Haitham and Prince William, building on long-standing UK-Oman ties, according to [Oman News Agency](https://2u.pw/NpsltIe4).

9. **Air cargo demand rises despite disruptions.** Global air cargo demand increased 4% in April despite Middle East disruptions, according to [IATA data](https://timesofoman.com//article/172334-air-cargo-demand-rises-4-in-april-despite-middle-east-disruptions-iata) reported by Times of Oman. The growth occurred as regional tensions continued affecting aviation operations.

10. **Bahrain establishes Morocco consulate.** Bahrain's King Hamad issued a decree establishing a consulate general in Laayoune, Morocco, according to [Bahrain News Agency](http://ow.ly/F5JJ50CK25B), strengthening diplomatic ties between the two kingdoms and Bahrain's presence in the disputed Western Sahara territory.
