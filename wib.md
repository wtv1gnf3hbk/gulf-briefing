1. **US-Iran exchange escalates over Kuwait.** The United States said it [struck Iranian](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/crlpy8n7pr6o) radar sites after Iran targeted American forces in Kuwait, according to the BBC. The Saudi Foreign Ministry condemned what it described as repeated Iranian attacks against Kuwait. Oil prices rose more than 2% as the conflict deepened.

2. **Former Yemeni president Hadi dies.** Former Yemeni President Abd Rabbuh Mansour Hadi died, with Saudi Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman offering condolences by telephone to his son, according to the Saudi Press Agency. Information Minister Mohammed al-Eryani of the internationally recognized government called Hadi "one of the nation's loyal men." The IRG-affiliated leadership received multiple condolence messages from regional officials.

3. **Saudi envoy to visit Sanaa for truce talks.** Saudi Yemen envoy Mohammed AlJabir announced he would visit Sanaa with an Omani delegation to consolidate the truce and ceasefire, support prisoner exchanges, and discuss dialogue between Yemeni components for a comprehensive political solution. AlJabir noted that nearly 2 million Yemenis work in Saudi Arabia and transfer over $4 billion annually to Yemen.

4. **Israel expands Lebanon ground offensive.** Israeli PM Benjamin Netanyahu ordered strikes on Beirut suburbs as Israel [seized Beaufort Castle](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cdep04kzz5wo) in southern Lebanon, expanding its ground operations, according to the BBC. Saudi Arabia condemned the Israeli aggression and incursion into Lebanese territory, calling on the international community to stop the military expansion.

5. **Trump seeks Iran deal modifications.** President Trump is seeking edits to the existing US-Iran deal, according to US media reports cited by the BBC. Saudi Foreign Minister Prince Faisal bin Farhan praised Trump's decision to give diplomacy additional time to reach an agreement that would end the war and restore maritime navigation security in the Strait of Hormuz.

6. **Abu Dhabi-Amman stock exchanges link.** The electronic link between the Amman Stock Exchange and the Abu Dhabi Securities Exchange was activated through the "Tabadul" platform, according to Arabic media reports. The move represents deeper financial cooperation between Jordan and the UAE's capital markets.

7. **Saudi Aramco adjusts LPG pricing.** Saudi Aramco [raised LPG](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/saudi-aramco-raises-lpg-osps-by-up-3-june-sonatrach-cuts-prices-by-18-31-2026-06-01/) official selling prices by up to 3% for June, while Algeria's Sonatrach cut prices by 18% and 31%, Reuters reported. The divergent pricing strategies reflect different market conditions and supply considerations across major energy producers.

8. **Yemen's IRG government restructures.** The internationally recognized government appointed Ahmed Hamid Lamlas as governor of Aden Governorate, according to IRG-affiliated Saba News Agency. The IRG presidency also renewed its request to Saudi Arabia to intervene to stop what it called Emirati interference in Yemeni affairs.

9. **Oman issues new cybercrime law.** Sultan Haitham bin Tarik issued royal decrees promulgating a new Law on Combating Cybercrime and amending provisions of the Cultural Heritage Law, according to the Oman News Agency. The moves reflect Oman's efforts to strengthen its legal framework for digital governance and cultural preservation.

10. **Gulf states condemn Kuwait attacks.** Oman and Bahrain strongly condemned Iranian attacks targeting Kuwait, with Oman affirming its solidarity and support for Kuwait's security measures. Qatar also "vehemently condemned" the Iranian strikes, calling them a flagrant violation of Kuwait's sovereignty and international law, according to Qatar News Agency.
