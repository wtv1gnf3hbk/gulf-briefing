Maritime security tensions.** Oman's Maritime Security Centre detected a suspected floating mine in the Strait of Hormuz, according to [Times of Oman](https://timesofoman.com//article/172387-omans-maritime-security-centre-detects-suspected-floating-mine-in-strait-of-hormuz), creating new concerns about navigation safety in the critical oil transit route.

**2. Hajj season concludes.** Saudi authorities declared the 2026 Hajj pilgrimage complete as millions of pilgrims performed farewell circumambulation around the Kaaba. Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman received congratulations from regional leaders, with 8,090 violations of residency and security regulations reported during the final week.

**3. UAE economic growth accelerates.** The UAE economy expanded 6.2% with GDP rising to AED 1.9 trillion in 2025, according to official government statistics. The country also ranked first globally in artificial intelligence talent concentration with 121% growth between 2019 and 2025.

**4. US-Iran nuclear diplomacy continues.** President Trump held meetings to determine next steps on Iran but announced no deal, according to the BBC. Vice President Vance said Washington and Tehran were "very close" but "not there yet" on reaching an agreement.

**5. Former Yemeni President dies.** Abd Rabbuh Mansour Hadi, former president of Yemen, died, with Saudi Defense Minister Prince Khalid bin Salman conveying condolences from the Kingdom's leadership to the Yemeni people. IRG officials received mourners in Aden.

**6. Yemen political developments.** The IRG-backed Yemeni government appointed Ahmed Hamid Lamlas as governor of Aden Governorate. Presidential Leadership Council Chairman Rashad al-Alimi called for unity while addressing institutional challenges following recent unilateral decisions.

**7. Saudi-UK security talks.** Prince Khalid bin Salman met with UK National Security Adviser Jonathan Powell to discuss Saudi-British cooperation and joint efforts to address regional challenges and de-escalate tensions for regional security and stability.

**8. Red Sea operations escalate.** Yemen's Houthi/Ansar Allah naval forces conducted reconnaissance missions across the Red Sea, with Mohammed Abdulsalam stating operations continue in support of Palestinians while maintaining commitment to maritime security under sovereign powers.

**9. Qatar leadership transitions.** Mohammed bin Abdulrahman Al Thani was appointed as Qatar's Prime Minister, with Majed Al Ansari named as advisor and official spokesman for the Ministry of Foreign Affairs under the new leadership structure.

**10. Southern Yemen tensions persist.** The Southern Transitional Council's Amr Bidh emphasized that political agreements should not interfere with citizens' lives, while STC President Aidarous al-Zubaidi received renewed support from southern populations for security operations.
