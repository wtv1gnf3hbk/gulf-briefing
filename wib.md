**GULF BRIEFING — MAY 30, 2026**

1. **Saudi Hajj success.** Saudi Arabia concluded the 1447 AH Hajj season with record-high pilgrim numbers and enhanced health technologies. King Salman and Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman received congratulations from Gulf leaders, Palestinian President Mahmoud Abbas, and regional heads of state for successfully managing the holy pilgrimage. Interior Minister Prince Abdulaziz bin Saud met with security forces who [provided services](https://spa.gov.sa/N2603169) throughout the religious gathering.

2. **Yemen prisoner exchange talks.** Saudi Arabia's Special Envoy to Yemen Mohammed AlJabir announced he will visit Sana'a with an Omani delegation to advance prisoner exchange negotiations and ceasefire consolidation. AlJabir said the visit aims to support dialogue between Yemeni factions toward "a comprehensive and sustainable political solution." He emphasized that nearly two million Yemenis work in Saudi Arabia, [transferring $4 billion](https://twitter.com/mohdsalj) annually to support 15 million family members inside Yemen.

3. **UAE economic growth accelerates.** The UAE economy expanded 6.2% in 2025 with GDP reaching AED 1.9 trillion, according to government media office figures. The UAE also leads globally in artificial intelligence talent concentration, which grew 121% between 2019 and 2025. Sheikh Mohammed bin Rashid reviewed tourism sector achievements for 2025 and launched new AI assistants for [government procurement](https://twitter.com/UAEmediaoffice) and customer services.

4. **Qatar appoints new Prime Minister.** Emir Tamim bin Hamad Al Thani appointed Mohammed bin Abdulrahman bin Jassim Al Thani as Prime Minister and Foreign Minister. The new Prime Minister expressed gratitude for the Emir's confidence and pledged to work with fellow ministers "in serving our nation and contributing to its advancement under wise leadership." Qatar also mourned the [death of](https://twitter.com/MBA_AlThani_) former official Abdullah bin Hamad Al Attiyah.

5. **Trump threatens Oman over Iran talks.** President Trump issued threats against Oman following reports of US-Iran nuclear negotiations facilitated by Muscat, according to German broadcaster Deutsche Welle. The threats come as AP News reported US and Iranian negotiators reached a tentative deal to extend ceasefire arrangements and start new nuclear talks, though Trump has not yet decided whether to [proceed with](https://apnews.com/article/iran-us-war-oil-may-28-2026-8f5ed2813ba63df7ae9ccbe991688d29) the agreement.

6. **Bahrain condemns Iran attack on Kuwait.** Bahrain's Foreign Ministry strongly condemned what it called an Iranian attack on Kuwait, with officials stating the assault threatens regional stability. The statement reflects escalating tensions in the Gulf as Iran faces international pressure over its nuclear program and [regional activities](https://twitter.com/bahdiplomatic).

7. **Yemen Presidential Council divisions.** IRG Presidential Council member Abu Zaraa criticized unilateral decision-making within the leadership body, stating individual decisions have caused major divisions. He emphasized that "unilateral decision-making does not serve institutional work at all" and called for adherence to collective decision protocols. Meanwhile, the IRG appointed Ahmed Hamid Lamlas as [governor of](https://twitter.com/sabanew_) Aden Governorate.

8. **Emirates Arsenal partnership expands.** Emirates Airlines unveiled a special Airbus A380 livery celebrating Arsenal FC's Premier League victory, reflecting the airline's extended partnership with the London club. The bespoke design demonstrates Emirates' commitment to connecting fans with major international sporting events and maintaining its [longstanding relationship](https://twitter.com/DXBMediaOffice) with Arsenal.

9. **Oman diplomatic engagement continues.** Foreign Minister Badr Albusaidi met with Iran's Deputy Foreign Minister, emphasizing Oman's role in regional diplomatic mediation. The discussions reflected "the constructive nature of Oman-Iran dialogue" and reaffirmed "the importance of diplomatic engagement on all fronts." Albusaidi also received credentials from Tanzania's new [ambassador to](https://twitter.com/badralbusaidi) Muscat.

10. **Houthi operations continue.** Houthi naval forces conducted reconnaissance operations in the Red Sea when a US warship opened fire with multiple weapons systems, according to spokesman Mohammed Abdulsalam. He described the American response as "hysterical" and said one missile exploded near a Gabonese-flagged vessel. The Houthis maintain their naval operations aim to [support Palestinians](https://twitter.com/abdusalamsalah) facing Israeli military action in Gaza.
