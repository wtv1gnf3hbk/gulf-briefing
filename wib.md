**Gulf Briefing - May 30, 2026**

**Hajj season conclusion.** Saudi Arabia completed its 2026 Hajj operations as nearly 2 million pilgrims concluded their pilgrimage. The Holy Sites Train transported 1.9 million passengers during the season, according to [official reports](https://www.youm7.com/story/2026/5/30/). Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman received congratulations from regional leaders including Qatar, Palestine, and Kuwait on the successful organization.

**Former Yemen president dies.** Former Yemeni President Abd Rabbuh Mansour Hadi died, prompting condolences from Saudi leadership. Prince Khalid bin Salman conveyed the kingdom's condolences to Hadi's family, describing him as "a wise leader who was keen on his country's security, stability, development and prosperity," according to his [Twitter statement](https://twitter.com/kbsalsaud).

**Trump considers Iran diplomacy.** President Trump met with advisers to make a "final determination" on Iran but announced no deal, according to the [BBC](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c0r2d40r91qo). Foreign Minister Prince Faisal bin Farhan praised Trump's decision to "give diplomacy a chance" to restore Strait of Hormuz security to pre-February 28 conditions.

**Oman maritime security alert.** Oman's Maritime Security Centre detected a suspected floating mine in the Strait of Hormuz, according to [Times of Oman](https://timesofoman.com//article/172387-omans-maritime-security-centre-detects-suspected-floating-mine-in-strait-of-hormuz). The discovery comes as regional tensions remain high over shipping security.

**UAE economy grows 6.2%.** The UAE economy expanded 6.2% with GDP rising to AED 1.9 trillion in 2025, according to government [data](https://twitter.com/UAEmediaoffice). The announcement came as Sheikh Mohammed bin Rashid launched new AI assistants for government procurement, taxes, customer service, and technical support.

**Southern Yemen leadership changes.** The IRG appointed Ahmed Hamid Lamlas as governor of Aden Governorate, according to [Saba News Agency](https://twitter.com/sabanew_). The appointment comes as the IRG condemned UAE airstrikes on National Army forces in Aden and Abyan governorates.

**Kuwait pilgrims return.** Kuwait Airways received its first return flight of Hajj pilgrims at Terminal 4 as groups began arriving back to joyful scenes, according to [KUNA](https://kuna.net.kw). The arrivals marked the start of post-Hajj repatriation operations across the Gulf.

**Bahrain Civil Defense rescue.** Civil Defense teams rescued five children from a house fire in Aali area without casualties, according to [Bahrain's Interior Ministry](https://policemc.gov.bh/en/news/78966/). Investigations are underway to determine the fire's cause.

**Oman UK diplomatic ties.** Prince William received Omani Minister of Culture, Sports and Youth Sayyid Theyazin bin Haitham Al Said at Windsor Castle to discuss bilateral relations, according to [Oman News Agency](https://twitter.com/ONA_eng). The meeting coincided with the launch of "Oman: The Jewel of Arabia" expedition project in London.

**Qatar mourns former minister.** Qatar announced the death of former minister Abdullah bin Hamad Al Attiyah, with Prime Minister Mohammed bin Abdulrahman Al Thani expressing condolences. Al Attiyah was described as "one of the nation's loyal men who made prominent contributions to Qatar's development process," according to official [statements](https://twitter.com/majedalansari).
