**Gulf Brief**

1. **Strait of Hormuz tensions.** Iran closed the Strait of Hormuz to maritime traffic as diplomatic tensions escalate with the United States, according to multiple reports. The BBC confirmed that [ships were attacked](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cx2631x6nelo?at_medium=RSS&amp;at_campaign=rss) during the closure, while Reuters reported that some tankers crossed the strait before shots were fired. Five loaded Qatari LNG vessels approached the waterway as shipping companies assessed transit risks, Bloomberg reported.

2. **Gaza ceasefire diplomacy.** Foreign ministers from Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Jordan and Egypt met in Antalya to discuss ceasefire developments in Gaza, according to Saudi Foreign Minister Prince Faisal bin Farhan's Twitter account. The ministers discussed reconstruction efforts and "categorical rejection of ongoing Israeli practices" that could undermine the peace process. The meeting occurred as [Netanyahu approved talks](https://apnews.com/e8af575b1ab8e82b46fb6ea4be1e185c) with Lebanon following Israeli strikes that threatened Iran ceasefire arrangements, AP reported.

3. **US-Iran peace talks.** Egypt and Pakistan are collaborating on a lasting US-Iran peace plan, Egypt's foreign minister said according to Reuters. Iran's foreign ministry reported "progress" in talks with the United States but said no date had been set for formal negotiations, according to Times of Oman. The diplomatic efforts come as Iran declared that "unconditional transit" through Hormuz is "fiction" following US-Israeli actions.

4. **UNIFIL peacekeeper killed.** A French peacekeeper died in southern Lebanon, the BBC reported, as attacks on UN forces continued. The UAE condemned the targeting of UNIFIL forces in southern Lebanon, according to its foreign ministry Twitter account. Bahrain also condemned what it called a "terrorist attack" on the UN peacekeeping mission.

5. **Yemen prisoner exchange.** Yemen's internationally recognized government and Houthi forces reached an agreement on prisoner exchanges and bank operations, according to Houthi negotiator Mohammed Abdulsalam's Twitter account. The deal includes resuming Yemenia Airlines flights between Sanaa and Jordan with three daily services, and flights to Cairo and India. Saudi Yemen envoy Mohammed AlJabir tweeted that Al-Hudaydah is "liberated" and "Yemen is breathing."

6. **Iraq oil exports.** Iraq will resume oil exports from all fields within days, state news agency reported according to Reuters. The announcement comes as regional energy markets face disruption from Strait of Hormuz tensions. Turkey said its gas pipeline contract with Iran is nearing expiry with no talks yet on extension, Reuters reported.

7. **Saudi defense diplomacy.** Deputy Defense Minister Prince Khalid bin Salman met with Greek Defense Minister Nikos Dendias and British Defense Minister John Healey, discussing regional security threats. Prince Khalid condemned "Iran's continued flagrant attacks on the Kingdom and other regional states" in Twitter posts. He also held talks with Hungary's defense minister about Iranian aggression's impact on regional stability.

8. **Palestine recognition push.** Saudi Foreign Minister Prince Faisal bin Farhan co-chaired an international conference in Paris on Palestinian statehood with French Foreign Minister Jean-Noël Barrot. Prince Faisal praised France's resolve to recognize Palestine and called for mechanisms to accelerate wider international recognition. Saudi Arabia's foreign ministry reaffirmed that the Kingdom's position on Palestinian statehood remains "firm and unwavering."

9. **Oman-Qatar summit.** Omani Sultan Haitham bin Tariq met with Qatari Emir Sheikh Tamim bin Hamad Al Thani at Al Baraka Palace, according to Omani Foreign Minister Badr Albusaidi's Twitter account. The leaders discussed regional developments and stressed cooperation to support regional stability and security. Qatar's Prime Minister Sheikh Mohammed bin Abdulrahman Al Thani described the meeting as embodying "firm trust and depth of fraternal ties."

10. **Saudi Newcastle struggles.** Saudi-controlled Newcastle United plunged in Premier League standings as manager Eddie Howe struggles for answers, AP reported. The team's poor performance comes as Saudi Arabia's Public Investment Fund faces scrutiny over its sports investments. Saudi club Al-Ahli will face Japan's Vissel Kobe in the Asian Champions League semifinals.
