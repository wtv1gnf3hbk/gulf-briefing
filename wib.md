1. **Former Yemen President dies.** Abd Rabbuh Mansour Hadi, who governed mostly from exile during Yemen's civil war, died at 80, according to [AP News](https://apnews.com/article/yemen-expresident-abdrabbuh-mansour-hadi-died-b3afd7b3679af14d335578e9560c7cd8). Hadi led the internationally recognized government but spent most of his presidency in Saudi Arabia after Houthi forces seized Sanaa in 2014.

2. **Saudi Arabia may cut oil prices.** The kingdom is considering lowering July oil prices to Asia as demand weakens, [Reuters reports](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/saudi-arabia-may-lower-july-oil-prices-asia-demand-weakens-2026-05-29/). The potential price reduction reflects concerns about softening Asian energy markets during the summer months.

3. **US-Iran negotiations near conclusion.** Vice President Vance said the US is "very close" to a deal with Iran but "not there yet," according to [the BBC](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c87qng40wz9o). Saudi Foreign Minister Prince Faisal bin Farhan praised Trump's decision to "give diplomacy a chance" to restore Strait of Hormuz security.

4. **Trump threatens Oman over Iran ties.** Following Trump's threat to "blow up" the sultanate, the US Secretary of State threatened sanctions on Muscat, [BBC reports](https://www.bbc.com/arabic/articles/ckgp3213m76o). The threats relate to Oman's diplomatic role in facilitating US-Iran dialogue on regional de-escalation.

5. **Oman-Iran security dialogue continues.** Oman's Foreign Minister Badr Albusaidi held talks with Iran's Deputy Foreign Minister on Strait of Hormuz security, [Times of Oman reports](https://timesofoman.com//article/172358-oman-and-iran-reaffirm-commitment-to-strait-of-hormuz-security). The discussions emphasized diplomatic engagement to reduce regional tensions.

6. **Japan's oil imports hit 1962 low.** April oil imports fell nearly 66% year-over-year as the Iran conflict disrupted supply chains, [Reuters reports](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/japans-april-oil-imports-fall-nearly-66-yy-iran-war-disrupts-supply-2026-05-29/). The shortage has forced Japan to tap strategic reserves and seek alternative suppliers.

7. **Hajj season concludes successfully.** Saudi leadership received congratulations from Gulf rulers on the completion of Hajj 1447 AH, with over 202 million calls handled through AI-enhanced communication networks. Prince Khalid bin Salman thanked God for honoring Saudi Arabia with serving pilgrims to the Holy House.

8. **Bahrain condemns Iranian attack on Kuwait.** The kingdom strongly condemned Iran's latest attack on Kuwait, with the EU also issuing condemnation, [KUNA reports](http://ow.ly/gJLc30sW7VU). The incident marks an escalation in regional tensions as diplomatic talks continue.

9. **Saudi Crown Prince meets Chad President.** Prince Mohammed bin Salman received Chad's President at the Royal Court, reviewing bilateral relations and discussing regional issues of mutual interest. The meeting reflects Saudi Arabia's expanding diplomatic engagement across Africa.

10. **Qatar denies Iran deal reports.** Foreign Ministry spokesperson Majed Al Ansari dismissed reports that Qatar "offered" $12 billion to Iran to secure a deal, calling them false and aimed at sabotaging diplomatic efforts. Qatar emphasized its role in promoting regional de-escalation and stability.
