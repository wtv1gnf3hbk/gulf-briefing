1. **Iran war costs.** The UN Development Programme estimated that one month of war between Iran and regional states has cost Arab countries up to $194 billion, according to [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/3/31/one-month-of-war-in-middle-east-cost-arab-states-up-to-194bn-undp?traffic_source=rss). Bloomberg reported Arab nations may lose $200 billion from the Iran war based on the UN study. The economic toll reflects widespread disruption to trade, energy infrastructure, and regional commerce.

2. **Iranian attacks on Kuwait.** Iran struck a Kuwaiti oil tanker in Dubai waters, causing a fire that was later extinguished, Reuters reported. UAE air defenses intercepted 8 ballistic missiles, 4 cruise missiles, and 36 drones launched from Iran on March 31, according to the UAE Defense Ministry. Bahrain's foreign ministry [condemned the](https://bit.ly/4lXA3Qn) Iranian attacks on Kuwait.

3. **Saudi chemical plant shutdown.** The Aramco-Dow joint venture Sadara Chemical Company halted production due to Middle East turmoil disrupting supply chains, [Reuters reported](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/aramco-dow-joint-venture-sadara-chemical-halts-production-over-middle-east-2026-03-31/). Bloomberg confirmed the major Saudi chemical plant shutdown as war disrupted supply networks. The facility is one of the world's largest integrated chemical complexes.

4. **Gulf allies urge Trump.** Gulf allies privately made the case to Trump to keep fighting until Iran is decisively defeated, according to [AP News](https://apnews.com/article/trump-iran-saudi-arabia-mbs-gulf-war-uae-89f690b952fe28d3140c537b70fa5051). Trump threatened to destroy Iran's desalination plants and again warned of widespread destruction if a deal is not reached "shortly." Defense Secretary Hegseth said coming days will be decisive for Iran.

5. **Abu Dhabi gas investment.** Abu Dhabi's 2PointZero signed a $2.25 billion deal to invest in US natural gas projects, Bloomberg reported. The UAE state-owned investment firm's acquisition represents the latest Gulf energy diversification move. The deal comes as regional energy companies seek to expand [beyond traditional](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-03-31/abu-dhabi-s-2pointzero-splashes-out-2-25-billion-in-us-gas-deal) oil and gas operations.

6. **Qatar diplomatic meetings.** Qatar's Emir Sheikh Tamim discussed regional developments with UAE President Sheikh Mohammed bin Zayed, according to Arabic media reports. Qatar offered to help with US-Iran negotiations, Al Jazeera reported. Prime Minister Sheikh Mohammed bin Abdulrahman Al Thani met separately with Saudi Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman to coordinate [regional responses](https://www.aljazeera.com/video/newsfeed/2026/3/31/qatar-says-ready-to-help-with-us-iran-negotiations?traffic_source=rss).

7. **Iranian pressure on Houthis.** Iran pressed Houthi forces on Red Sea shipping operations, European officials told Bloomberg. The pressure suggests Tehran seeks to manage maritime disruptions while maintaining its proxy network. Yemen's Mohammed Abdulsalam said naval operations aim to support Palestinians and [warned against](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-03-30/iran-presses-houthis-on-red-sea-shipping-european-officials-say) expanding the conflict.

8. **Saudi-Canadian talks.** Saudi Foreign Minister Prince Faisal bin Farhan received Canadian Foreign Minister Anita Anand, who expressed solidarity with the Kingdom and appreciation for assistance to Canadian citizens. The meeting addressed reducing tensions, enhancing maritime security, and protecting vital infrastructure. Both sides agreed on the need for [concerted international](https://twitter.com/KSAMOFA) efforts.

9. **Yemeni lawsuit against veterans.** A Yemeni lawmaker sued US military veterans, accusing them of attempting to kill him, AP News reported. The case involves allegations against veterans working with UAE-linked security contractors. The lawsuit highlights tensions between Yemen's internationally recognized government and [southern separatist](https://apnews.com/article/yemeni-lawmaker-assassination-attempt-uae-spear-operations-c627ad64f9a90a76349fcadfdfe7a17a) forces.

10. **Omani distance learning.** Oman activated distance learning in Al Dhahirah and Muscat governorates, according to Times of Oman. The decision followed weather conditions affecting normal school operations. Oman's Central Bank also announced [auction results](https://timesofoman.com//article/170117-cbo-announces-auction-results-of-bond-issue) for a government bond issue.
