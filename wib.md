**US-Iran nuclear negotiations.** American and Iranian negotiators reached tentative agreement on extending a ceasefire and beginning new nuclear talks, according to [multiple reports](https://apnews.com/article/iran-us-war-oil-may-28-2026-8f5ed2813ba63df7ae9ccbe991688d29). US Vice President J.D. Vance said Washington and Tehran were "very close" to a preliminary agreement but Trump remained [not satisfied](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c74dy9jw1q9o?at_medium=RSS&amp;at_campaign=rss) with the current terms. The proposed 60-day ceasefire extension requires Trump's final approval.

**Oman-US tensions.** The US [warned Oman](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/us-warns-oman-not-engage-facilitating-tolls-strait-hormuz-2026-05-28/) against facilitating tolls for transit through the Strait of Hormuz, according to Reuters. German media reported Trump threatened to "blow up" Oman over the issue. Saudi Foreign Minister Prince Faisal bin Farhan [tweeted](https://twitter.com/FaisalbinFarhan) that the kingdom "highly appreciates" Trump's decision to give diplomacy a chance to restore freedom of navigation.

**Former Yemen president dies.** Abdrabbuh Mansour Hadi, former president of Yemen, [died at 80](https://apnews.com/article/yemen-expresident-abdrabbuh-mansour-hadi-died-b3afd7b3679af14d335578e9560c7cd8), AP News reports. Hadi led the internationally recognized government during much of Yemen's civil war before being sidelined by the Presidential Leadership Council. Yemen Information Minister Mohammed Al-Eryani [mourned](https://twitter.com/ERYANIM) the "great fighter Field Marshal" on Twitter.

**Israel expands Gaza control.** Israeli Prime Minister Netanyahu directed forces to expand control in Gaza to 70 percent of the territory, according to [BBC reports](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/ceqpelq5reqo?at_medium=RSS&amp;at_campaign=rss). The move comes as Israel struck Beirut and southern Lebanon following large-scale evacuation orders. Iran said it targeted an American base after [fresh US strikes](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c98r2qy5809o?at_medium=RSS&amp;at_campaign=rss).

**Saudi Crown Prince hosts Chad.** Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman met with Chad's president at the Royal Court in Mina Palace, according to [Saudi Press Agency](https://twitter.com/spagov). The meeting reviewed bilateral relations and cooperation prospects during the Hajj season. The kingdom also received condolence telegrams from officials following a gas explosion in China.

**Kuwait under attack.** Iran launched drone and missile attacks targeting Kuwait, prompting condemnation from Iraq and the Organization of Islamic Cooperation, [KUNA reports](https://twitter.com/kuna_en). The attacks represent an escalation in regional tensions as nuclear negotiations continue. Bahrain's foreign ministry strongly [condemned](https://twitter.com/bahdiplomatic) the Iranian aggression against Kuwait.

**Oman diplomatic activity.** Omani Foreign Minister Badr Albusaidi held discussions with Iran's Deputy Foreign Minister, [describing](https://twitter.com/badralbusaidi) the "constructive nature" of Oman-Iran dialogue. Sultan Haitham offered Eid prayers in Sohar while Prince William received a senior Omani official in Windsor Castle for talks on bilateral ties.

**UAE AI leadership.** The UAE ranked first globally in artificial intelligence talent concentration, growing 121 percent between 2019 and 2025, according to Sheikh Abdullah bin Zayed's [tweet](https://twitter.com/ABZayed). The Emirates also announced restoration of the Umayyad Mosque in Damascus with support from Sheikha Fatima bint Mubarak. Mohammed bin Rashid reviewed tourism sector achievements for 2025.

**Qatar diplomatic appointments.** Qatar's Emir appointed Mohammed bin Abdulrahman Al Thani as Prime Minister, the new leader [announced](https://twitter.com/MBA_AlThani_) on Twitter. Foreign ministry spokesperson Majed Al Ansari was named advisor to the Deputy PM and official spokesman. Both officials offered Eid greetings to the Qatari people.

**Temperature records.** Temperatures in Oman crossed 47.5 degrees Celsius, [Times of Oman reported](https://timesofoman.com//article/172348-temperature-crosses-475-degrees-in-oman). The extreme heat coincides with ongoing regional tensions and diplomatic negotiations. Blue Origin's New Glenn rocket exploded during testing in Florida, according to multiple Gulf news sources.
