**Gulf Brief: April 5, 2026**

1. **Iran-US military clash.** Iran shot down a US F-15E fighter jet, with one crew member [reportedly rescued](https://www.reuters.com/business/aerospace-defense/trump-israel-pressure-iran-ahead-deadline-search-continues-missing-us-airman-2026-04-04/) and another still missing, according to Reuters. The incident marks a significant escalation as President Trump issued an ultimatum to Tehran following the downing.

2. **Gulf states under attack.** Iranian drones and missiles targeted Kuwait's government complex and oil facilities, while [UAE air defenses](https://www.masrawy.com/news/news_publicaffairs/details/2026/4/5/2967444/%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A5%D9%85%D8%A7%D8%B1%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AF%D9%81%D8%A7%D8%B9%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AC%D9%88%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%AA%D8%AA%D8%B9%D8%A7%D9%85%D9%84-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A2%D9%86-%D9%85%D8%B9-%D8%AA%D9%87%D8%AF%D9%8A%D8%AF-%D8%B5%D8%A7%D8%B1%D9%88%D8%AE%D9%8A) dealt with 23 ballistic missiles and 56 UAVs on April 4, per Masrawy. Bahrain's interior ministry said civil defense controlled fires at targeted facilities without casualties.

3. **Saudi Crown Prince in diplomatic push.** Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman spoke with Russian President Putin about regional escalation's impact on maritime security and the global economy. He also met with Italian Prime Minister Giorgia Meloni, who visited Doha to [secure energy](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-04-04/italy-s-meloni-visits-doha-to-bolster-energy-supplies-hit-by-war) supplies disrupted by the conflict.

4. **Strait of Hormuz tensions.** Iran declared Iraqi ships could pass through the strategic waterway as transits increased, Bloomberg reported. A [French-owned vessel](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cjd8275jrrko?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss) successfully transited the strait according to the BBC, while South Korea asked Gulf nations for steady energy supplies and vessel safety guarantees.

5. **Satellite imagery restricted.** US firm Planet Labs announced it would [indefinitely withhold](https://www.reuters.com/business/media-telecom/satellite-firm-planet-labs-indefinitely-withhold-iran-war-images-2026-04-05/) images from the Iran conflict zone, per Reuters. The decision affects commercial satellite intelligence availability during the escalating military confrontation between Iran and Western forces.

6. **US arrests Iranian relatives.** American authorities detained relatives of late Iranian general Qasem Soleimani, the [BBC reported](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cgqkn4j48qdo?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss). The arrests occurred as Washington warned its citizens to leave Lebanon citing escalating security risks in the region.

7. **Yemen diplomatic movements.** Saudi envoy Mohammed Al-Jabir announced via Twitter he would visit Sanaa with an Omani delegation to consolidate the truce, support prisoner exchanges, and discuss dialogue between Yemeni factions. The visit aims to build on Saudi Arabia's 2021 peace initiative.

8. **Palestinian statehood conference.** Saudi Foreign Minister Prince Faisal bin Farhan co-chaired an international conference in Paris on Palestinian statehood implementation. He praised France's resolve to recognize Palestine and called for mechanisms to accelerate wider recognition and establish a clear two-state solution roadmap.

9. **Qatar energy security talks.** Prime Minister Sheikh Mohammed bin Abdulrahman Al Thani held discussions with UAE President Sheikh Mohammed bin Zayed and Saudi Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman about regional developments. Qatar emphasized the need for coordinated efforts to support peaceful solutions and limit escalation impacts.

10. **Oman diplomatic engagement.** Foreign Minister Badr Albusaidi met with his Belarusian counterpart to launch a joint commission for expanding bilateral ties. He also participated in a call with GCC colleagues, Jordan, and Russia expressing concerns about humanitarian crises stemming from the regional conflict.
