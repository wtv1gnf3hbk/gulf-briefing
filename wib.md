**Gulf Bureau Daily Briefing**

1. **Iranian missile strikes.** Iran launched a coordinated barrage of missiles and drones targeting Gulf states on March 30-31, with [UAE air defenses](https://twitter.com/modgovae) intercepting 11 ballistic missiles and 27 drones according to the defense ministry. Saudi civil defense reported shrapnel from intercepted drones damaged six homes in Al-Kharj governorate with no casualties, while Kuwait announced an Iranian attack hit a Kuwaiti oil tanker in Dubai waters that Dubai authorities later [extinguished](https://timesofoman.com//article/170073-dubai-authorities-put-out-fire-on-kuwaiti-oil-tanker).

2. **Saudi diplomatic push.** Prince Faisal bin Farhan co-chaired an international conference in Paris with his French counterpart on Palestinian statehood and implementing a two-state solution, while separately meeting with Canada's foreign minister who expressed solidarity with the kingdom. The Saudi foreign ministry reaffirmed that the kingdom's position on establishing a Palestinian state remains [firm and unwavering](https://twitter.com/rbalsaud) according to Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman's directives.

3. **US-Iran tensions escalate.** President Trump threatened to destroy Iran's desalination plants and issued new warnings to Tehran while Gulf allies privately urged him to continue fighting until Iran is decisively defeated, according to [AP reporting](https://apnews.com/article/trump-iran-saudi-arabia-mbs-gulf-war-uae-89f690b952fe28d3140c537b70fa5051). The Pentagon denied allegations that Defense Secretary Hegseth's broker sought investment before the Iran war, while Bloomberg reported sustained Iranian missile attacks are adding pressure on the US to defend Gulf shipping lanes.

4. **Yemen envoy announces Sanaa visit.** Saudi Ambassador to Yemen Mohammed Al-Jabir announced he will visit Houthi-controlled Sanaa with an Omani delegation to consolidate a ceasefire, support prisoner exchanges, and discuss dialogue between Yemeni factions. Al-Jabir noted that nearly 2 million Yemenis work in Saudi Arabia and transfer over $4 billion annually to Yemen, benefiting approximately [15 million people](https://twitter.com/mohdsalj) in the country.

5. **Saudi oil pricing disrupted.** Saudi Arabia faces pricing challenges as buyers seek alternatives to kingdom crude, with Bloomberg reporting oil pricing talks taking on added urgency as deadlines approach. The disruption comes as Iran's sustained missile campaign threatens Gulf energy infrastructure and maritime security, with [Chinese container ships](https://www.reuters.com/world/china/chinese-container-ships-pass-through-strait-hormuz-second-attempt-data-shows-2026-03-30/) requiring second attempts to pass through the Strait of Hormuz.

6. **Gulf markets decline.** Regional stock markets retreated following escalating Middle East conflict, with Gulf bourses showing mixed performance as investors weighed security risks against energy sector opportunities. The market volatility reflects growing concerns about sustained Iranian attacks on regional energy infrastructure and [shipping disruptions](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/gulf-markets-retreat-amid-escalating-middle-east-conflict-saudi-edges-higher-2026-03-30/) through critical maritime chokepoints.

7. **UAE Cabinet approves initiatives.** The UAE Cabinet approved an integrative medicine strategy balancing traditional and modern healthcare, plus over 120 international agreements in energy, investment, and diplomatic fields. Sheikh Mohammed bin Rashid praised the "defensive spirit" of UAE armed forces and announced a five-year space sector strategy for an industry worth over [44 billion dirhams](https://twitter.com/HHShkMohd) employing 170 institutions.

8. **Houthi naval operations continue.** Ansar Allah spokesman Mohammed Abdulsalam said Yemen's naval forces aim to support Palestinians confronting the Gaza siege, not challenge maritime powers. He accused a US warship of opening fire "hysterically" near a Yemeni reconnaissance operation, with one missile exploding near a [Gabonese vessel](https://twitter.com/abdusalamsalah) in the Red Sea.

9. **Qatar Energy Texas project.** Qatar Energy and ExxonMobil began producing liquefied natural gas at their joint project in Texas, according to [Asharq Business reporting](https://asharqbusiness.com/power/126057/%D9%82%D8%B7%D8%B1-%D9%84%D9%84%D8%B7%D8%A7%D9%82%D8%A9-%D9%88-%D8%A5%D9%83%D8%B3%D9%88%D9%86-%D8%AA%D8%B7%D9%84%D9%82%D8%A7%D9%86-%D8%A5%D9%86%D8%AA%D8%A7%D8%AC-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%BA%D8%A7%D8%B2-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%B3%D8%A7%D9%84-%D9%81%D9%8A-%D8%AA%D9%83%D8%B3%D8%A7%D8%B3/). Prime Minister Sheikh Mohammed bin Abdulrahman Al Thani discussed regional escalation with Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman, emphasizing coordination and peaceful solutions to limit current tensions.

10. **Bahrain security alert.** Bahrain's interior ministry sounded air raid sirens urging citizens to seek safe shelter, while the kingdom condemned Iranian attacks on Kuwait. Bahrain's foreign ministry participated in a GCC-Jordan video call with Russia's foreign minister, with participants sharing concerns about [humanitarian crises](https://twitter.com/badralbusaidi) that may overwhelm regions worldwide due to ongoing warfare.
