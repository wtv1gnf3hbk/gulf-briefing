1. **Saudi Hajj success.** Saudi Arabia concluded the 2026 Hajj pilgrimage with over 2.6 million participants and no major health incidents. Interior Minister Prince Abdulaziz bin Saud congratulated Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman on the success of this year's [Hajj season](https://spa.gov.sa/ar/w2603422), with authorities arresting 8,090 violators of residency and border regulations during the final week.

2. **Iran nuclear talks extend.** US President Trump postponed a decision on Iran nuclear negotiations after meeting with senior officials. Foreign Minister Prince Faisal bin Farhan praised Trump's willingness to [give diplomacy](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c87qng40wz9o) additional time to reach an agreement ending the war and restoring maritime navigation security in the Strait of Hormuz.

3. **UAE economic growth.** The UAE economy expanded 6.2% in 2025, with GDP rising to AED 1.9 trillion, according to government data. The country also leads globally in artificial intelligence talent concentration, growing 121% between 2019 and [2025](https://twitter.com/UAEmediaoffice), reflecting the leadership's focus on building human capital for future economic transformation.

4. **Yemen leadership transition.** The IRG-controlled Yemeni government appointed Ahmed Hamid Lamlas as governor of Aden. Chairman Rashad Al-Alimi called for unity among ranks and directing energies toward restoring [state institutions](https://twitter.com/sabanew_), following congratulations from Gulf leaders on Yemen's National Day.

5. **Oman diplomatic engagement.** Foreign Minister Badr Albusaidi met with Iran's Deputy Foreign Minister, emphasizing constructive Oman-Iran dialogue. The discussions reaffirmed the importance of diplomatic engagement and Oman's role in supporting efforts to [reduce regional](https://twitter.com/badralbusaidi) tensions and advance peaceful coexistence.

6. **Bahrain Iranian attack condemnation.** Bahrain strongly condemned an Iranian attack on Kuwait, with the Foreign Ministry issuing a statement denouncing the assault. The kingdom called for regional [de-escalation](https://bit.ly/4dAMr6j) and emphasized the need for collective security measures against such provocations.

7. **Qatar investment projects.** Invest Oman unveiled new investment projects with guaranteed offtake agreements, aimed at diversifying the sultanate's economy. The announcement comes as regional economies seek to reduce dependence on [hydrocarbon revenues](https://timesofoman.com//article/172379-invest-oman-unveils-projects-with-guaranteed-offtake) and develop sustainable growth models.

8. **Kuwait oil prices decline.** Kuwait Petroleum Corporation reported crude oil prices fell 84 cents to $117.51 per barrel. The decline reflects broader market volatility as [global energy](https://kuna.net.kw/ArticleDetails.aspx?id=3305807) markets adjust to shifting supply-demand dynamics in the post-pandemic recovery period.

9. **Oman stock market gains.** The Muscat Stock Exchange index rose 143 points in weekly trading, with bond and sukuk issuances exceeding OMR 589 million on the exchange. The sultanate also extended trading hours to align more closely with [global standards](https://timesofoman.com//article/172377-longer-trading-hours-brings-msx-closer-to-global-trading-standards) and attract international investors.

10. **Saudi-Ecuador football.** Saudi Arabia's national team prepared for a friendly match against Ecuador as part of 2026 World Cup preparations. Princess Reema highlighted the team's readiness with 20 days remaining until their World Cup opener, emphasizing the kingdom's [football development](https://twitter.com/rbalsaud) initiatives under Vision 2030.
