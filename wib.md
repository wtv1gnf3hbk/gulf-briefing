1. **Iranian missile attacks escalate.** Iran launched coordinated strikes against multiple Gulf states on March 29-30, with Saudi Arabia intercepting [five ballistic missiles](https://arabic.cnn.com/middle-east/article/2026/03/30/worker-killed-in-attack-on-kuwait-power-plant-news-agency-reports) targeting its eastern region and the UAE engaging 16 ballistic missiles and 42 drones. Kuwait reported one worker killed in an attack on a power station, while six people were injured in Abu Dhabi after a missile was intercepted near the KEZAD industrial zone.

2. **Houthis join broader conflict.** Yemen's Iran-backed Ansar Allah group launched its first direct attack on Israel, with the BBC reporting the [Houthis joined](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cd6l5n8jv4yo?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss) the expanding regional war. The group's spokesman Mohammed Abdulsalam confirmed naval operations would continue "to support the Palestinian people," raising fears of renewed Red Sea shipping disruptions that could further damage the global economy.

3. **Pakistan mediates Iran talks.** Pakistan hosted regional powers for indirect US-Iran negotiations, with Bloomberg reporting that [Saudi Arabia, Egypt, and Turkey](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-03-29/pakistan-says-saudi-egypt-turkey-support-us-iran-peace-talks) supported the peace initiative. Trump claimed progress in the talks while declining to share details, though he hinted at potential military action against Iran's Kharg Island oil hub.

4. **Gulf markets decline on conflict fears.** Most Gulf stock markets fell as investors worried about broader regional escalation, according to [Reuters reports](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/most-gulf-markets-ease-fears-broader-iran-conflict-2026-03-29/). Oil and LNG supplies face "worst-possible scenario" risks, with the Strait of Hormuz closure threat sending crude prices higher as Bloomberg tracked Saudi crude heading to Pakistan in rare transit.

5. **Crown Prince conducts diplomatic outreach.** Saudi Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman held phone calls with leaders from Spain, Uzbekistan, and Mauritania to discuss regional security implications, according to official Saudi Foreign Ministry statements. Defense Minister Prince Khalid bin Salman met with French and Hungarian defense officials, condemning "repeated Iranian attacks targeting the Kingdom."

6. **Oman delivers emergency supplies.** Omani authorities delivered emergency supplies to isolated villages in Wadi Bani Khalid, according to [Times of Oman](https://timesofoman.com//article/170042-emergency-supplies-delivered-to-isolated-villages-in-wadi-bani-khalid) reports. Foreign Minister Badr Albusaidi emphasized Oman's role in seeking regional peace, stating any "reasonable assessment" shows the US and Iran share strong interests in ending the conflict.

7. **Qatar implements financial protection measures.** Qatar's Central Bank announced a package of measures to protect the financial system during the regional crisis, per [Al Arabiya reports](https://www.alarabiya.net/aswaq/economy/2026/03/30/%D9%85%D8%B5%D8%B1%D9%81-%D9%82%D8%B7%D8%B1-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%B1%D9%83%D8%B2%D9%8A-%D9%8A%D8%AA%D8%AE%D8%B0-%D9%85%D8%AC%D9%85%D9%88%D8%B9%D8%A9-%D9%85%D9%86-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D8%AF%D8%A7%D8%A8%D9%8A%D8%B1-%D9%84%D8%AD%D9%85%D8%A7%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%86%D8%B8%D8%A7%D9%85-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%8A). Prime Minister Mohammed bin Abdulrahman Al Thani condemned Iranian attacks on Qatar as "rejected and unjustified under any pretext."

8. **Bahrain sounds air raid sirens.** Bahrain's Interior Ministry activated warning sirens and urged citizens to seek shelter during Iranian missile attacks, posting urgent safety instructions across social media platforms. The kingdom's Foreign Ministry issued statements condemning Iranian strikes against Kuwait and other regional targets.

9. **Yemen government ambassador visits Sanaa.** Saudi Ambassador to Yemen Mohammed Al Jaber announced plans to [visit Sanaa](https://twitter.com/mohdsalj) with an Omani delegation "to consolidate the truce and ceasefire" and discuss prisoner exchanges. Al Jaber noted that nearly 2 million Yemenis work in Saudi Arabia, transferring over $4 billion annually to support 15 million family members in Yemen.

10. **US loses combat aircraft in strike.** The United States suffered its first combat loss of a valuable E-3 surveillance aircraft in a missile strike, according to [Bloomberg reporting](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-03-29/us-sees-first-combat-loss-of-valuable-e-3-jet-in-missile-strike). The loss occurred as American forces deployed additional troops to the region following the escalation of hostilities.
