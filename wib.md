**Gulf News Briefing — May 30, 2026**

1. **Iran nuclear talks.** President Trump met with senior aides to make a "final determination" on Iran nuclear negotiations but announced no deal, according to the [BBC reported](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c0r2d40r91qo?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss). Vice President Vance said the US and Iran are "very close" to an agreement but "not there yet." Foreign Minister Prince Faisal bin Farhan praised Trump's decision to give diplomacy additional time to restore Strait of Hormuz navigation security.

2. **Hajj season concludes.** Saudi authorities successfully managed the 2026 Hajj pilgrimage as approximately 2 million pilgrims completed the final farewell circumambulation around the Kaaba. Interior Minister Prince Abdulaziz bin Saud congratulated Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman on the successful completion of Hajj 1447 AH. Crown Prince Mohammed received congratulations from leaders across the Gulf, including Qatar, Kuwait, Oman, and Bahrain.

3. **UAE economic growth.** The UAE economy expanded 6.2% with gross domestic product rising to AED 1.9 trillion in 2025, according to government [media office announced](https://twitter.com/UAEmediaoffice). The Emirates also leads globally in artificial intelligence talent concentration, growing 121% between 2019 and 2025. Sheikh Mohammed bin Rashid launched new AI assistants for government procurement, taxation, and customer services.

4. **Oman lithium project.** Oman unveiled a strategic lithium-ion battery materials production project, according to [Times of Oman reported](https://timesofoman.com//article/172380-oman-hosts-a-strategic-project-for-production-of-lithium-ion-battery-materials). The Sultanate also activated Starlink satellite internet services to enhance development and secure communications. Sultan Haitham conducted Eid Al-Adha prayers in Sohar while visiting UK officials launched the "Oman: Jewel of Arabia" expedition project.

5. **Strait of Hormuz concerns.** India's Finance Ministry identified Hormuz disruption as the "most consequential variable" for the country's external price outlook, per [Times of Oman](https://timesofoman.com//article/172382-hormuz-disruption-remains-most-consequential-variable-for-indias-external-price-outlook-finance-ministry-review). Separately, Oman warned of a suspected mine spotted in the strait. The waterway remains critical for global energy supplies as regional tensions persist.

6. **Bahrain Iranian attack.** Bahrain strongly condemned an Iranian attack on Kuwait, according to the [foreign ministry stated](https://bit.ly/4dAMr6j). The kingdom's civil defense separately rescued five children from a house fire in Aali without casualties. Crown Prince Salman bin Hamad met with Japan's Crown Prince and received condolences calls regarding recent regional developments.

7. **Gaza casualties rise.** The death toll in Gaza reached 72,938 with 172,919 injured since the Israeli offensive began, Qatar News Agency [reported](https://ow.ly/qmiV50Z5RQk). Israel was added to a UN sexual violence blacklist for the first time. Netanyahu directed the IDF to increase control of Gaza to 70% following large-scale evacuation orders and strikes on Beirut and southern Lebanon.

8. **Yemen political developments.** IRG Chairman Rashad al-Alimi called for unity and directing energies toward restoring state institutions, according to [Saba reported](https://sabanew.net). Former President Abd Rabbuh Mansour Hadi's funeral drew senior officials in Aden. The IRG appointed Ahmed Lamlas as Aden governor while disbursing financial dues to military personnel previously removed from southern governorates.

9. **Saudi security operations.** Saudi authorities arrested 8,090 violators of residency, work, and border security regulations within one week, the [Interior Ministry announced](https://twitter.com/MOISaudiArabia). The kingdom's security forces implemented comprehensive plans enabling smooth pilgrimage operations. Advanced technical systems tracked crowd movements moment by moment during Hajj rituals.

10. **Kuwait oil prices.** Kuwait oil prices fell 84 cents to $117.51 per barrel, [KUNA reported](https://kuna.net.kw/ArticleDetails.aspx?id=3305807). The Kuwait Petroleum Corporation announced the decline as global energy markets remained volatile. Australia announced a trilateral defense framework with the US and Japan for air and missile defense data sharing.
