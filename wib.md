**Gulf News Briefing — Tuesday, April 7, 2026**

1. **Saudi missile defense.** Saudi Arabia [intercepted 7 missiles](https://www.reuters.com/world/saudi-arabia-intercepts-7-missiles-debris-falls-near-energy-facilities-2026-04-06/) overnight, with debris falling near energy facilities in the Eastern Province, Reuters reports. Prince Khalid bin Salman condemned Iran's "continued flagrant attacks" on the Kingdom in meetings with Greek and British defense ministers, while Saudi Civil Defense warned residents against filming interception sites.

2. **UAE under attack.** The UAE Defense Ministry confirmed air defenses engaged [12 ballistic missiles](https://twitter.com/modgovae), 2 cruise missiles and 19 drones launched from Iran on April 6. UAE Interior Ministry urged residents to remain in safe locations during the attacks. The UAE had earlier demanded that Hormuz Strait access be guaranteed in any US-Iran deal.

3. **Iran strikes Kuwait.** Iranian forces [struck US forces](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/iran-struck-us-forces-relocated-kuwaits-bubiyan-island-military-spokesperson-2026-04-06/) relocated to Kuwait's Bubiyan island, according to an Iranian military spokesperson cited by Reuters. The strike represents an escalation targeting American personnel positioned in Gulf states following regional tensions.

4. **Trump Iran ultimatum.** President Trump issued an [expletive-laden threat](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cvg0q6wdzp1o) to Iran over potential Hormuz Strait blockage as negotiations continue, the BBC reports. Al Ekhbariya TV reported Trump saying "We have an active participant in the talks with Iran and they want an agreement" following the rescue of a US airman in Iran.

5. **Oil market turmoil.** Saudi Arabia [raised Asia oil prices](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-04-06/saudis-raise-oil-price-to-record-premium-as-war-riles-markets) to record premiums while the Hormuz closure divided Middle Eastern oil states' fortunes, Bloomberg reports. Gulf equity markets remained mixed as investors awaited clarity on US-Iran negotiations, with energy infrastructure increasingly targeted.

6. **UAE Pakistan loan.** The UAE made a [surprise $3 billion loan move](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-04-06/uae-s-surprise-3-billion-loan-move-puts-pakistan-under-strain) that puts Pakistan under financial strain, according to Bloomberg. The unexpected financial pressure comes as regional powers reassess economic relationships following escalating Middle East tensions.

7. **Bahrain alerts.** Bahrain's Interior Ministry sounded warning sirens urging citizens to "remain calm and head to the nearest safe place" while following official channels. Foreign Minister Abdullatif al-Zayani called the Hormuz crisis requiring "urgent international action to avoid serious humanitarian and economic repercussions."

8. **Yemen prisoner deal.** Houthi negotiator Mohammed Abdulsalam announced an agreement covering bank restrictions, Yemenia Airlines flights to Jordan and Cairo, and prisoner exchanges. Saudi envoy Mohammed Al-Jabir posted "#Al-Hudaydah is liberated.....#Yemen is breathing" while continuing diplomatic efforts in Sanaa with Omani support.

9. **Iran infrastructure fears.** Iranians braced for potential infrastructure strikes as Trump's deadline approached, the [BBC reported](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cvgl0ng8z0do). Iran's Sports Minister called on athletes and artists to form "human chains" around power plants while Ayatollah Mojtaba Khamenei praised the martyrdom of intelligence chief Sayyid Khademi.

10. **Space milestone.** NASA's [Artemis II astronauts](https://timesofoman.com//article/170354-artemis-ii-mission-breaks-record-for-farthest-distance-travelled-by-humans-from-earth) broke the record for farthest human travel from Earth during their lunar mission, Times of Oman reports. The achievement came as regional conflicts dominated headlines across Gulf media outlets.
