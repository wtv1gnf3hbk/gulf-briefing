1. **Oman mine alert.** Oman's Maritime Security Centre detected a suspected [floating mine](https://timesofoman.com//article/172387-omans-maritime-security-centre-detects-suspected-floating-mine-in-strait-of-hormuz) in the Strait of Hormuz, highlighting ongoing maritime security concerns in the critical shipping lane. The detection comes as tensions persist over regional security and freedom of navigation through the waterway that handles roughly 20% of global oil transit.

2. **Yemen former president dies.** Former Yemeni President Abd Rabbuh Mansour Hadi died, prompting condolences from Saudi leadership. Prince Khalid bin Salman conveyed the Saudi leadership's [condolences to](https://timesofoman.com//article/172388-president-donald-trump-your-statement-against-oman-is-not-a-passing-mistake-it-is-a-historical-sin) Hadi's family, describing him as a "wise leader who was keen on the security, stability, development and prosperity of his country."

3. **Saudi Hajj transport completed.** Saudi Arabia successfully transported 1.9 million passengers via the Holy Mashaer Train during the 2026 Hajj season, according to [Youm7 reports](https://www.youm7.com/story/2026/5/30/%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B3%D8%B9%D9%88%D8%AF%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%83%D8%AA%D9%85%D8%A7%D9%84-%D9%86%D9%82%D9%84-1-9-%D9%85%D9%84%D9%8A%D9%88%D9%86-%D8%B1%D8%A7%D9%83%D8%A8-%D8%B9%D8%A8%D8%B1-%D9%82%D8%B7%D8%A7%D8%B1-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%B4%D8%A7%D8%B9%D8%B1/7433120). The completion marks another successful Hajj logistics operation, with Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman and other officials receiving congratulations from regional leaders including the Emir of Kuwait and President of Palestine.

4. **Trump threatens Oman.** President Donald Trump made controversial statements against Oman, described by Times of Oman as [not a "passing mistake"](https://timesofoman.com//article/172388-president-donald-trump-your-statement-against-oman-is-not-a-passing-mistake-it-is-a-historical-sin) but a "historical sin." The comments target the traditionally neutral sultanate, which has long played a mediating role in regional conflicts. Per DW, the threats raise questions about whether they represent confusion or deliberate policy.

5. **UAE economy grows 6.2%.** The UAE economy expanded 6.2% with GDP rising to AED 1.9 trillion in 2025, according to [UAE government](https://twitter.com/UAEmediaoffice) media reports. The growth underscores the Emirates' continued economic diversification efforts and positions it among the region's strongest performers as global economies face various headwinds.

6. **Yemen peace talks advance.** Saudi Ambassador to Yemen Mohammed Al-Jabir announced he will visit Sanaa with an Omani delegation to consolidate the truce and [discuss prisoner](https://twitter.com/mohdsalj) exchanges. The visit aims to support dialogue between Yemeni components toward a comprehensive political solution, building on Saudi Arabia's 2021 peace initiative for the war-torn country.

7. **Bahrain condemns Iranian attack.** Bahrain strongly condemned an Iranian attack on Kuwait, according to [Bahrain's foreign](https://twitter.com/bahdiplomatic) ministry. The incident adds to regional tensions as Gulf states coordinate responses to what they view as Iranian aggression, with Bahrain's statement reflecting broader GCC solidarity on security threats.

8. **Qatar's Hajj congratulations.** Qatar's leadership congratulated Saudi Arabia on the success of the 2026 Hajj season, reflecting the continued normalization of ties between the Gulf neighbors. The congratulations from [Qatari leadership](https://twitter.com/spagov) to the Saudi King and Crown Prince mark another diplomatic exchange following the 2021 Al-Ula Agreement.

9. **Houthi-Yemen government agreement.** A three-point agreement was announced covering bank decisions cancellation, Yemenia Airlines flights resumption to Jordan with three daily flights, and meetings to address remaining issues. The deal was outlined by [Mohammed Abdulsalam](https://twitter.com/abdusalamsalah), the Houthi negotiator, suggesting potential progress in economic and humanitarian arrangements.

10. **Emirates Arsenal livery.** Emirates unveiled a celebratory A380 livery dedicated to Arsenal FC following the club's Premier League victory, according to [Dubai Media](https://twitter.com/DXBMediaOffice). The special aircraft design reflects Emirates' continued sports partnerships and commitment to connecting fans with major international sporting events.
