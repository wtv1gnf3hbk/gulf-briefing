**Iran-US nuclear agreement.** US and Iranian negotiators reached a tentative deal to extend ceasefire and start new nuclear talks, according to [multiple reports](https://apnews.com/article/iran-us-war-oil-may-28-2026-8f5ed2813ba63df7ae9ccbe991688d29). Saudi Foreign Minister Prince Faisal bin Farhan said the kingdom "highly appreciates" Trump's decision to give diplomacy a chance to restore security and freedom of navigation in the Strait of Hormuz to pre-February 28 conditions.

**Yemen president dies.** Former Yemen president Abdrabbuh Mansour Hadi died at 80, [AP News reported](https://apnews.com/article/yemen-expresident-abdrabbuh-mansour-hadi-died-b3afd7b3679af14d335578e9560c7cd8). IRG Information Minister Mohammed Al-Eryani mourned the "great fighter Field Marshal" in a statement announcing prayer and burial arrangements. The General People's Congress described Hadi as completing "a national march full of giving, struggle, and notable patriotic stances."

**Kuwait missile attacks.** Kuwait said air defences intercepted hostile missile and drone attacks, [Reuters reported](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/kuwaiti-army-says-air-defences-intercepting-hostile-missile-drone-attacks-2026-05-28/). The Organisation of Islamic Cooperation condemned the "repeated drone and missile attacks" targeting Kuwait and reaffirmed "full solidarity" with the country. Iraq also condemned the attacks, calling for restraint and peaceful solutions.

**Hormuz shipping disruption.** Three oil and LNG tankers exited the Strait of Hormuz with their transponders turned off, [Reuters reported](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/three-oil-lng-tankers-exit-hormuz-with-transponders-off-2026-05-28/). The unusual move comes as regional tensions continue over maritime security. US Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent separately assured that Oman has committed not to impose tolls on Strait of Hormuz transit.

**Trump threatens Oman.** Trump threatened to "blow up" Oman if it supports Iran, according to multiple Arabic news sources. CNN Arabic reported that a US minister revealed Oman's position regarding the Strait of Hormuz. The threats come as Washington seeks to maintain pressure on regional allies regarding Iran policy.

**Saudi Hajj operations.** Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman held the annual reception ceremony for state officials, Islamic dignitaries, and pilgrims at the Royal Court in Mina Palace. Prince Khalid bin Salman thanked God for "honoring us with serving the Two Holy Mosques and caring for the pilgrims to His Sacred House," asking that their Hajj be accepted.

**UAE AI leadership.** Sheikh Abdullah bin Zayed announced the UAE ranked first globally in artificial intelligence talent concentration, growing 121% between 2019 and 2025. He said the figure "embodies the vision of a leadership that has made building people, attracting minds, and creating the future a solid national approach."

**Eid celebrations.** Leaders across the Gulf extended Eid Al-Adha greetings to their populations and the broader Islamic world. Qatar's Prime Minister Mohammed bin Abdulrahman Al Thani and other officials offered congratulations, while Oman's Sultan Haitham performed Eid prayers at Sultan Qaboos Mosque in Sohar.

**Syria mosque restoration.** The UAE announced restoration of the Umayyad Mosque in Damascus with support from Sheikha Fatima bint Mubarak. Sheikh Abdullah bin Zayed said the initiative reflects the UAE's "firm commitment to preserving our common heritage" and support for the Syrian people.

**Yemen peace talks.** Saudi Yemen envoy Mohammed AlJabir announced he will visit Sanaa with an Omani delegation to consolidate the truce, support prisoner exchanges, and discuss dialogue between Yemeni components. He emphasized nearly 2 million Yemenis work safely in Saudi Arabia, transferring over $4 billion annually to benefit 15 million people in Yemen.
