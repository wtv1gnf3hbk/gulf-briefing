**US-Iran nuclear negotiations.** The US and Iran reached a tentative agreement to extend the Gaza ceasefire and start new nuclear talks, according to AP News. President Trump met with officials to make a "final determination" on the Iran deal but has not yet decided whether to move forward, the BBC reported. US Vice President Vance said the two sides are "very close" to a [deal but](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c87qng40wz9o?at_medium=RSS&amp;at_campaign=rss) "not there yet."

**Saudi Hajj season concludes.** Saudi Arabia declared the 2026 Hajj season successful as pilgrims completed their rituals. Prince Khalid bin Salman tweeted gratitude for serving the Two Holy Mosques and caring for pilgrims. Gulf leaders including Qatar's Emir, Kuwait's Emir, and Oman's Sultan sent congratulatory messages to King Salman and Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman on the [season's success](https://spa.gov.sa/N2603169).

**Iran's Foreign Minister contacts Oman.** Iran's Deputy Foreign Minister held discussions with Oman's Foreign Minister Badr Albusaidi following Trump's threats, according to CNN Arabic. The talks reflected "the constructive nature of Oman-Iran dialogue" and reaffirmed diplomatic engagement efforts, Albusaidi tweeted. Oman continues supporting efforts to reduce regional tensions and advance peaceful [coexistence initiatives](https://arabic.cnn.com/middle-east/article/2026/05/29/first-contact-between-foreign-ministers-of-iran-and-oman-trumps-threat).

**Bahrain condemns Iranian attack on Kuwait.** Bahrain's Foreign Ministry strongly condemned what it called an Iranian attack on Kuwait, according to official statements. The ministry expressed solidarity with Kuwait and condemned the aggressive actions. No details were provided about the nature or timing of the [alleged attack](https://bit.ly/4dAMr6j).

**Japan oil imports fall sharply.** Japan's April oil imports fell nearly 66% year-on-year to the lowest level since 1962 as the Iran conflict disrupted supply chains, Reuters reported. The dramatic decline reflects broader regional instability affecting global energy markets. Energy analysts cited the Iran war as a [major factor](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/japans-april-oil-imports-fall-nearly-66-yy-iran-war-disrupts-supply-2026-05-29/) disrupting traditional supply routes.

**UAE AI talent leadership.** The UAE ranked first globally in artificial intelligence talent concentration growth, increasing 121% between 2019 and 2025, according to Sheikh Abdullah bin Zayed. The figure reflects the leadership's vision of building human capital and attracting global minds. The UAE "does not wait for the future, but rather [creates it](https://x.com/ABZayed)," he tweeted.

**Gaza ceasefire efforts continue.** Lebanese President and US Secretary of State discussed ongoing ceasefire efforts for Gaza, according to Qatar News Agency. International diplomatic pressure continues for a sustainable resolution to the conflict. Meanwhile, Israeli strikes continued in Beirut and southern Lebanon after large-scale [evacuation orders](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cj3pgrpmlklo?at_medium=RSS&amp;at_campaign=rss).

**Yemen government appointments.** The Yemeni IRG government appointed Ahmed Hamid Lamlas as governor of Aden Governorate, according to Saba News Agency. President Al-Alimi called for unity and directing energies toward restoring state institutions. The Presidential Leadership Council continues efforts to consolidate government control across [liberated areas](https://sabanew.net).

**UAE tourism sector growth.** Sheikh Mohammed bin Rashid reviewed the UAE tourism sector's achievements for 2025, according to the Dubai Media Office. The UAE government continues implementing comprehensive development strategies across multiple sectors. The review highlighted progress in positioning the UAE as a [global destination](https://x.com/UAEmediaoffice).

**Pentagon warns on China buildup.** Pentagon chief Lloyd Austin sounded alarm over China's military buildup and urged allies to boost defense spending, Reuters reported. Austin warned about China's construction of launch pads near nuclear missile silos. The Pentagon is pushing for increased allied contributions to address the [growing threat](https://www.reuters.com/world/china/pentagon-chief-urges-allies-boost-defence-spending-amid-alarm-over-chinas-2026-05-30/).
