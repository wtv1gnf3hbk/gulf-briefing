**Gulf Regional Briefing**

1. **Iran-US tensions.** Trump announced the US military has [blockaded Iranian](https://apnews.com/article/iran-us-israel-trump-lebanon-blockade-hormuz-april-13-2026-ed7a6cd4bc61dc47f317a2c82afcc1c9) ports to pressure Tehran following failed peace talks. Iran warned of "provocative US actions" in calls with Russian Foreign Minister Lavrov, according to Times of Oman reports. Trump's blockade threat represents a return to previous hardline tactics as diplomatic efforts collapsed.

2. **OPEC oil crisis.** OPEC output [suffered record](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-04-13/opec-output-suffers-record-plunge-as-iran-war-throttles-exports) plunges as the Iran conflict throttled exports through the Strait of Hormuz. Saudi oil sales to China are expected to halve as the Hormuz crisis lifted prices significantly. Kuwait raised May crude prices for Asia, according to Reuters pricing documents, reflecting supply disruptions.

3. **Gulf market turmoil.** Gulf financial markets traded mixed as [US-Iran peace](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/uae-markets-retreat-us-iran-peace-talks-fail-2026-04-13/) talks failed to produce agreements. UAE markets retreated following the diplomatic breakdown. Morgan Stanley maintained oil price forecasts but predicted slow recovery in supply chains, according to Reuters reports.

4. **Saudi diplomatic coordination.** Foreign Minister Prince Faisal bin Farhan conducted extensive phone consultations with UAE Deputy PM Sheikh Abdullah bin Zayed, Qatar's PM Sheikh Mohammed bin Abdulrahman, and Kuwait's Foreign Minister Sheikh Jarrah Al-Sabah. The calls focused on regional developments and Iran's continued attacks on Gulf states.

5. **European Council Gulf tour.** The President of the European Council began a Gulf tour including UAE, Saudi Arabia, and Qatar, according to Egyptian National Media Authority reports. The visit comes as European leaders seek to coordinate responses to escalating regional tensions and energy security concerns.

6. **Bahrain golden visa program.** Bahrain launched a permanent [golden residency](https://www.alghad.tv/%D8%A7%D8%AE%D8%A8%D8%A7%D8%B1-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%BA%D8%AF/news/%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A8%D8%AD%D8%B1%D9%8A%D9%86-%D8%AA%D8%AA%D9%8A%D8%AD-%D8%AA%D8%A3%D8%B4%D9%8A%D8%B1%D8%A9-%D8%A5%D9%82%D8%A7%D9%85%D8%A9-%D8%B0%D9%87%D8%A8%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%AF%D8%A7%D8%A6%D9%85%D8%A9-%D9%84%D8%AC%D8%B0%D8%A8-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%B3%D8%AA%D8%AB%D9%85%D8%B1%D9%8A%D9%86-%D9%88%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D9%88%D9%87%D9%88%D8%A8%D9%8A%D9%86) visa program to attract investors and talented individuals. The initiative aims to boost economic diversification as Gulf states compete for foreign investment during regional instability.

7. **Pakistan financial talks.** Pakistan entered discussions with Saudi Arabia and China following [UAE's loan](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-04-13/pakistan-in-talks-with-saudi-arabia-china-after-uae-s-loan-move) move, according to Bloomberg reports. The talks reflect shifting Gulf financial relationships as regional powers reassess economic partnerships during the current crisis.

8. **Yemen negotiations progress.** Saudi Yemen envoy Mohammed AlJabir announced plans to visit Sanaa with an Omani delegation to consolidate the truce and support prisoner exchanges. Houthi negotiator Mohammed Abdulsalam confirmed an agreement canceling recent banking decisions and resuming Yemenia Airlines flights between Sanaa and Jordan.

9. **Oman diplomatic engagement.** Foreign Minister Badr Albusaidi participated in the ninth Indian Ocean Conference via video link, emphasizing collective stewardship for regional security and freedom of navigation. He met with Ukraine's Defense Council Secretary, reaffirming that diplomacy remains the only path to peace.

10. **British-Kuwait security commitment.** Britain's Ambassador to Kuwait confirmed his country's firm commitment to Kuwait's security, according to Egyptian Youm7 reports. The assurance comes as Gulf states seek international security guarantees following escalating regional tensions and Iranian threats.
