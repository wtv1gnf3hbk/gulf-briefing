1. **Iran-US negotiations.** US and Iranian negotiators reached a tentative deal to extend a ceasefire and start new nuclear talks, according to [AP News](https://apnews.com/article/iran-us-war-oil-may-28-2026-8f5ed2813ba63df7ae9ccbe991688d29). President Trump is considering whether to move forward with the Iran deal but has not yet decided. Saudi Foreign Minister Prince Faisal bin Farhan praised Trump's decision to give diplomacy a chance to end the war and restore maritime navigation security in the Strait of Hormuz to pre-February 28 conditions.

2. **Hajj season completion.** Saudi Arabia successfully concluded the 2026 Hajj pilgrimage, with millions of Muslim pilgrims completing the final circling around the Kaaba. Regional leaders including the Emir of Kuwait, Sultan of Oman, and President of Palestine congratulated Saudi leadership on organizing a successful Hajj season. Prince Khalid bin Salman thanked God for the honor of serving pilgrims, while the Saudi Ministry of Health declared Hajj 1447 free of epidemic outbreaks despite global health challenges.

3. **UAE economic growth.** The UAE economy expanded 6.2% with GDP rising to AED 1.9 trillion during 2025, according to [government media](https://twitter.com/UAEmediaoffice). The UAE leads globally in artificial intelligence talent concentration, growing 121% between 2019 and 2025. Sheikh Mohammed bin Rashid reviewed achievements in the tourism sector and launched new artificial intelligence assistants for government procurement, taxes, customer service, and technical support.

4. **Yemen political developments.** Saudi envoy Mohammed AlJabir announced he would visit Sanaa with an Omani delegation to consolidate a truce and ceasefire, support prisoner exchanges, and discuss dialogue between Yemeni components for a comprehensive political solution. Meanwhile, former President Abd Rabbuh Mansour Hadi died, with senior IRG officials attending funeral services in Aden. The IRG appointed Ahmed Hamid Lamlas as the new governor of Aden Governorate.

5. **Kuwait oil prices decline.** Kuwait oil prices fell 84 cents to $117.51 per barrel, [KUNA reported](https://kuna.net.kw/ArticleDetails.aspx?id=3305807). The Kuwait Petroleum Corporation provided the pricing update as regional energy markets continued fluctuating. Kuwaiti officials in Jeddah confirmed readiness to assist Kuwaiti pilgrims returning from Hajj.

6. **Qatar transit fees rejected.** Qatar refused to impose permanent fees for ships crossing the Strait of Hormuz, according to [East Economy with Bloomberg](https://asharqbusiness.com/power/134614/قطر-ترفض-فرض-رسوم-دائمة-لعبور-السفن-مضيق-هرمز/). The decision comes as regional tensions continue affecting maritime navigation. Qatar Prime Minister Mohammed bin Abdulrahman Al Thani was recently appointed to the position, with Majed Al Ansari named as his advisor and Foreign Ministry spokesman.

7. **Bahrain security operations.** Bahrain's Civil Defense rescued five children from a house fire in the Aali area without casualties, the [Interior Ministry reported](https://policemc.gov.bh/en/news/78966/). Investigations continue into the fire's cause. Bahrain strongly condemned an Iranian attack on Kuwait, according to the Foreign Ministry.

8. **Oman diplomatic activities.** Sultan Haitham bin Tarik offered Eid Al Adha prayers at Sultan Qaboos Mosque in Sohar. Foreign Minister Badr Albusaidi met with Iran's Deputy Foreign Minister, reflecting constructive Oman-Iran dialogue and Oman's role in reducing regional tensions. Prince William received Sayyid Theyazin bin Haitham Al Said at Windsor Castle to discuss UK-Oman relations.

9. **Yemen Houthis operations.** Houthi spokesman Mohammed Abdulsalam defended Yemeni naval operations in the Red Sea as supporting Palestinians against Israeli aggression, not as provocation. He criticized American-formed coalitions protecting Israel and militarizing sea routes. Mohammed Ali Al Houthi called for Arab armies to mobilize for Jerusalem rather than Yemen, questioning regional priorities in addressing Palestinian issues.

10. **Southern Yemen tensions.** Southern Transitional Council leader Amr Bidh criticized unilateral political decisions within the Presidential Leadership Council, emphasizing institutional work importance. The STC activated Starlink satellite internet as a development step. STC President Aidarous al-Zubaidi performed Umrah rituals, while the council continued asserting southern autonomy against northern Yemeni government control.
