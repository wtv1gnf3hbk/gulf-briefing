1. **US-Iran escalation.** The United States [bombed Iranian](https://apnews.com/article/iran-us-irael-war-kuwait-strikes-88daa9f90b48baaa7beb18e35515c59d) military sites following Iran's missile attacks on US forces in Kuwait. Sirens sounded over Kuwait City as Iranian projectiles targeted American bases, according to the BBC. The strikes mark a dangerous escalation in regional tensions following months of tit-for-tat attacks.

2. **Yemen peace efforts.** Saudi Ambassador Mohammed AlJabir announced plans to visit Sanaa with an Omani delegation to consolidate the ceasefire, support prisoner exchanges, and pursue comprehensive political solutions in Yemen. Prince Khalid bin Salman conveyed condolences on the death of former Yemeni President Abd Rabbuh Mansour Hadi, describing him as a leader committed to his country's security and stability.

3. **Israel-Hezbollah truce.** President Trump confirmed Israel and Hezbollah agreed to [halt mutual](https://apnews.com/article/lebanon-israel-hezbollah-netanyahu-dahiyeh-rubio-ceasefire-airstrikes-a4708d5ed8d75f74463ba88c1cabca33) attacks and cease fire exchanges. Saudi Foreign Minister Prince Faisal bin Farhan praised Trump's decision to give diplomacy a chance to end the conflict and restore security in the Strait of Hormuz. The agreement comes following Israeli strikes on Beirut's southern suburbs.

4. **Former Yemeni president dies.** Abd Rabbuh Mansour Hadi, Yemen's former president, passed away, prompting condolences from across the region. The Saudi Crown Prince personally called Hadi's son to express sympathies, while Defense Minister Prince Khalid bin Salman conveyed official condolences from the Saudi leadership to the family.

5. **Oman cybersecurity law.** Sultan Haitham issued two royal decrees, including one promulgating a new Law on Combating Cybercrime to enhance the country's digital security framework. The [legislation reflects](https://timesofoman.com//article/172462-new-cyberlaw-to-enhance-omans-digital-security) Oman's commitment to strengthening its cyber defenses as regional tensions increase digital warfare concerns.

6. **Saudi energy pricing.** Saudi Aramco raised LPG official selling prices by up to 3% for June deliveries, according to Reuters. The move comes as OPEC+ sources indicate the group will likely [increase July](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/opec-likely-raise-july-oil-output-target-despite-hormuz-disruption-sources-say-2026-06-01/) oil output targets despite ongoing Strait of Hormuz disruptions affecting global energy markets.

7. **Gulf condemnations.** Saudi Arabia and Bahrain strongly condemned Iran's repeated attacks on Kuwait, with the Saudi Foreign Ministry describing them as "sinful and repeated" assaults on a sister state. Bahrain's Foreign Ministry similarly denounced the targeting of Kuwait with missile attacks and hostile Iranian drones.

8. **Qatar World Cup preparations.** Qatar excluded eight players from its final squad for the 2026 World Cup, according to CNN Arabic. The country is also hosting the U-17 World Cup Qatar 2026, with officials highlighting key matches and dates for the youth tournament as part of ongoing football development initiatives.

9. **UAE AI expansion.** The UAE announced plans to expand its AI capabilities, with MGX and partners extending the Campus AI project in France to develop up to 3GW of compute capacity. The UAE reported achieving first place globally in artificial intelligence talent concentration, growing 121% between 2019 and 2025.

10. **Regional diplomatic activity.** Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman received calls from the French President and met with Chad's President at Mona Palace. The discussions covered bilateral cooperation enhancement and regional developments, particularly diplomatic efforts to maintain security, stability, and maritime navigation freedom.
