**Gulf Region Briefing - May 29, 2026**

1. **US-Iran nuclear talks.** The US and Iran reached a tentative agreement to extend ceasefire and start new nuclear negotiations, according to [multiple reports](https://apnews.com/article/iran-us-war-oil-may-28-2026-8f5ed2813ba63df7ae9ccbe991688d29). Saudi Foreign Minister Prince Faisal bin Farhan [praised Trump's](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c74dy9jw1q9o) decision to give diplomacy a chance, while Trump said the US is "not satisfied" with the current deal yet.

2. **Former Yemen president dies.** Field Marshal Abdrabbuh Mansour Hadi, Yemen's former president, [died at 80](https://apnews.com/article/yemen-expresident-abdrabbuh-mansour-hadi-died-b3afd7b3679af14d335578e9560c7cd8), according to AP News. The IRG government mourned the "great fighter" who led during Yemen's transition period before being ousted by the Houthis in 2015.

3. **Saudi Hajj season culminates.** Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman hosted his annual Hajj reception at Mina Palace as over 1.7 million pilgrims completed the [pilgrimage rituals](https://timesofoman.com//article/172353-cyprus-reportedly-seek-brahmos-supersonic-missiles-from-india). Prince Khalid bin Salman thanked God for "honoring us with serving the Two Holy Mosques," while security forces managed crowd flows at the Jamarat facility.

4. **Trump threatens Oman.** US Treasury officials warned Oman against facilitating tolls for the Strait of Hormuz, according to [Reuters reports](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/us-warns-oman-not-engage-facilitating-tolls-strait-hormuz-2026-05-28/). The warning comes as tensions rise over maritime security in the strategic waterway, with Arabic media describing it as a US threat against the sultanate.

5. **Qatar rejects Iran payment claims.** Qatar's Foreign Ministry spokesman Majed Al Ansari denied reports suggesting Qatar "offered" $12 billion to Iran to secure a nuclear deal, calling them "simply not true." He said such reports were being circulated by parties attempting to "sabotage the deal" and undermine diplomatic efforts.

6. **Saudi oil price cuts expected.** Saudi Arabia may lower July oil prices to Asia as demand weakens, according to [Reuters reporting](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/saudi-arabia-may-lower-july-oil-prices-asia-demand-weakens-2026-05-29/). The move would reflect softening Asian demand and follows recent OPEC production decisions. Kuwait oil prices rose $4.56 to $118.35 per barrel.

7. **UAE AI leadership grows.** Sheikh Abdulla bin Zayed highlighted that the UAE ranks first globally in artificial intelligence talent concentration, growing 121% between 2019 and 2025. The government launched new AI assistants for procurement, taxes, and customer services as part of its federal AI program.

8. **Bahrain condemns Iran attack.** Bahrain strongly condemned what it called an Iranian attack on Kuwait, according to the [Foreign Ministry](https://twitter.com/bahdiplomatic). The statement comes following broader regional tensions, though specific details of the alleged incident were not immediately available.

9. **Yemen truce developments.** Saudi envoy Mohammed Al-Jabir announced plans to visit Sanaa with an Omani delegation to "consolidate the truce and ceasefire" and support prisoner exchanges. Meanwhile, Abu Zaraa criticized unilateral decision-making within Yemen's Presidential Leadership Council as divisive.

10. **Regional diplomatic activity.** Oman's Foreign Minister Badr Albusaidi met with Iran's Deputy Foreign Minister, emphasizing diplomatic engagement to reduce regional tensions. Prince William of Wales received Oman's Culture Minister in London to launch the "Oman: The Jewel of Arabia" expedition project.
