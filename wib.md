**Iran-US negotiations.** The US and Iran reached a tentative agreement to extend their ceasefire and begin new nuclear talks, according to [AP News](https://apnews.com/article/iran-us-war-oil-may-28-2026-8f5ed2813ba63df7ae9ccbe991688d29). President Trump remained undecided about moving forward with the deal following meetings to make a "final determination" on Iran policy.

**Saudi Hajj season.** Saudi Arabia concluded the 2026 Hajj pilgrimage with regional leaders congratulating King Salman and Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman on its success. Foreign Minister Prince Faisal bin Farhan [praised Trump's decision](https://twitter.com/FaisalbinFarhan) to give diplomacy a chance to end the war and restore maritime navigation in the Strait of Hormuz.

**UAE tourism achievements.** Sheikh Mohammed bin Rashid reviewed the UAE tourism sector's achievements for 2025, according to the [UAE Gov Media Office](https://twitter.com/UAEmediaoffice). The announcement came during government meetings focused on artificial intelligence implementation across federal services and operations.

**Oman-Iran dialogue.** Oman's Foreign Minister Badr Albusaidi held discussions with Iran's Deputy Foreign Minister, describing them as reflecting the "constructive nature" of Oman-Iran dialogue, according to [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/japans-april-oil-imports-fall-nearly-66-yy-iran-war-disrupts-supply-2026-05-29/).

**Ronaldo league title.** Cristiano Ronaldo scored twice to lead Al-Nassr to the Saudi league title, ending the club's trophy drought, [AP reported](https://apnews.com/article/ronaldo-trophy-saudi-nassr-title-4be06f122a9f7d01763c0384f6f0de43). The Portuguese striker's goals clinched the championship for the Riyadh-based team.

**Yemen government changes.** The IRG announced the appointment of Ahmed Hamid Lamlas as the new governor of Aden Governorate, according to [Saba News Agency](https://sabanew.net). The move comes as the Presidential Leadership Council called for unity of ranks and focusing energies on restoring state institutions.

**Qatar government appointments.** Qatar's Emir appointed Mohammed bin Abdulrahman Al Thani as Prime Minister, with Majed Al Ansari named as his advisor and official Foreign Ministry spokesman, according to official [Qatar News Agency](https://twitter.com/QNAEnglish) announcements.

**Bahrain civil defense.** Bahrain's Civil Defense rescued five children from a house fire in the Aali area without casualties, the [Ministry of Interior](https://twitter.com/moi_bahrain) reported. Investigations are underway to determine the cause of the fire.

**Kuwait Hajj mission.** Kuwait's Hajj Mission chief praised Saudi Arabia's services and the success of this season, [KUNA reported](https://twitter.com/kuna_en). Nearly two million Yemenis work in Saudi Arabia and transfer over $4 billion annually to Yemen, benefiting approximately 15 million people.

**Oman Sultan prayers.** Sultan Haitham bin Tarik offered Eid Al Adha prayers at Sultan Qaboos Mosque in Sohar, North Al Batinah Governorate, according to [Oman News Agency](https://twitter.com/ONA_eng). The Sultan led the traditional Eid celebrations in the northern governorate.
