**US-Iran nuclear talks.** American and Iranian negotiators reached a tentative agreement to extend a ceasefire and begin new nuclear discussions, [AP News reported](https://apnews.com/article/iran-us-war-oil-may-28-2026-8f5ed2813ba63df7ae9ccbe991688d29). President Trump met with officials to make a "final determination" on the deal but has not yet decided whether to proceed. Saudi Foreign Minister Prince Faisal bin Farhan praised Trump's decision to "give diplomacy a chance" to end the war and restore maritime security in the Strait of Hormuz.

1. **Iran missile attack on Kuwait.** An Iranian ballistic missile targeted Ali Al Salem Air Base in Kuwait, injuring two Americans and destroying a drone, according to reports cited in Arabic-language Google News. Kuwaiti air defenses intercepted the missile. [Bahrain condemned](https://bit.ly/4dAMr6j) the Iranian attack, calling it a threat to regional stability. The incident marks a significant escalation in regional tensions.

2. **Hajj season concludes successfully.** Saudi Arabia completed the 1447 AH Hajj pilgrimage without major incidents. Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman and King Salman received congratulatory messages from GCC leaders including Qatar's Emir, Kuwait's Emir, and Oman's Sultan. [Saudi officials honored](https://twitter.com/spagov) the success of the Hajj operations, with Interior Minister Prince Abdulaziz bin Saud meeting security forces and volunteers who facilitated the pilgrimage.

3. **UAE economic growth projections.** The UAE economy is expected to grow 6.2% with GDP rising to AED 1.9 trillion in 2025, [the government announced](https://twitter.com/UAEmediaoffice). The UAE also leads globally in artificial intelligence talent concentration, growing 121% between 2019 and 2025. Sheikh Mohammed bin Rashid launched new AI assistants for government procurement, taxes, and customer services as part of a broader digital transformation initiative.

4. **Yemen leadership changes.** The IRG's Presidential Leadership Council appointed Ahmed Hamid Lamlas as governor of Aden Governorate, [Saba News Agency reported](https://twitter.com/sabanew_). Former President Abd Rabbuh Mansour Hadi died, with IRG officials and ministers attending his funeral in Aden. The Southern Transitional Council declared support for security measures while maintaining tensions with the IRG over governance arrangements.

5. **Qatar Prime Minister appointment.** His Highness Sheikh Tamim bin Hamad Al Thani appointed Mohammed bin Abdulrahman Al Thani as Prime Minister, [according to his Twitter account](https://twitter.com/MBA_AlThani_). Al Thani expressed honor at the appointment and commitment to serving Qatar under the Emir's leadership. Majed Al Ansari was simultaneously appointed as advisor to the Prime Minister and official spokesman for the Ministry of Foreign Affairs.

6. **Saudi-UK security cooperation.** Prince Khalid bin Salman met with UK National Security Adviser Jonathan Powell to review Saudi-British cooperation and coordinate on regional challenges, [he announced](https://twitter.com/kbsalsaud). The discussions focused on de-escalating tensions to preserve regional security and stability. Prince Khalid also held a call with Sweden's Defense Minister Pål Jonson on bilateral defense relations.

7. **Bahrain fire rescue operation.** Bahrain's Civil Defense rescued five children from a house fire in the Aali area without casualties, [the Interior Ministry reported](https://twitter.com/moi_bahrain). Investigations are underway to determine the cause of the fire. The successful rescue operation highlighted the effectiveness of Bahrain's emergency response capabilities.

8. **Oman diplomatic activities.** Sultan Haitham bin Tarik offered Eid Al Adha prayers at Sultan Qaboos Mosque in Sohar, [Oman News Agency reported](http://n9.cl/adorv). Prince William of Wales received Sayyid Theyazin bin Haitham Al Said at Windsor Castle to discuss Oman-UK relations. Foreign Minister Badr Albusaidi received credentials from Tanzania's new ambassador and held farewell talks with Brazil's outgoing ambassador.

9. **Kuwait oil prices decline.** Kuwait oil prices dropped 84 cents to $117.51 per barrel, [KUNA reported](https://kuna.net.kw/ArticleDetails.aspx?id=3305807). The Kuwait Petroleum Corporation announced the price adjustment as global oil markets continued to fluctuate. Kuwaiti leadership sent congratulations to Croatia's President on the country's Statehood Day.

10. **Saudi security operations.** Saudi authorities arrested 8,090 violators of residency, work, and border security regulations within one week, [the Interior Ministry announced](https://twitter.com/MOISaudiArabia). The campaign is part of ongoing efforts to maintain homeland security and enforce immigration laws. Saudi Arabia also reported that nearly 2 million Yemenis work in the Kingdom, transferring over $4 billion annually to Yemen.
