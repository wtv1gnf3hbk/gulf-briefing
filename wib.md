1. **US-Iran nuclear deal.** The United States and Iran moved closer to an agreement on nuclear issues, with US Vice President JD Vance stating the parties are [very close but](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c87qng40wz9o?at_medium=RSS&amp;at_campaign=rss) "not there yet." President Trump denied reports of a final deal but appeared open to negotiations, following a pattern of escalating military strikes and diplomatic overtures between Washington and Tehran.

2. **Saudi hajj season success.** The kingdom declared the 2026 hajj pilgrimage a success as millions of Muslims completed the annual ritual in Mecca. Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman received Chad's president at the Royal Court during hajj, while Deputy Crown Prince Saud bin Mishal [announced the successful](https://spa.gov.sa/N2602703) completion of the 1447 AH hajj season following the traditional ceremonies.

3. **Former Yemen president dies.** Abdrabbuh Mansour Hadi, Yemen's former president who governed largely in exile during the civil war, [died at age 80](https://apnews.com/article/yemen-expresident-abdrabbuh-mansour-hadi-died-b3afd7b3679af14d335578e9560c7cd8). The IRG government mourned his death, with Information Minister Mohammed Al-Eryani describing him as a "great fighter" who made significant contributions to Yemen's development.

4. **Saudi oil pricing strategy.** The kingdom may lower crude oil prices to Asian markets in July as demand weakens, according to Reuters. This potential adjustment comes as [Saudi Arabia considers](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/saudi-arabia-may-lower-july-oil-prices-asia-demand-weakens-2026-05-29/) its pricing strategy following recent global energy market fluctuations and reduced Asian consumption patterns.

5. **Qatar PM appointment confirmed.** Sheikh Mohammed bin Abdulrahman Al Thani officially took office as Qatar's Prime Minister, expressing gratitude for Emir Tamim's confidence in his appointment. The new PM pledged to work with fellow ministers in [serving the nation](https://twitter.com/MBA_AlThani_) and contributing to Qatar's advancement under the Emir's leadership.

6. **Iran-Oman diplomatic engagement.** Omani Foreign Minister Badr Albusaidi held discussions with Iran's Deputy Foreign Minister, emphasizing the constructive nature of bilateral dialogue. The meeting focused on diplomatic engagement across all fronts, with Oman continuing to [support regional efforts](https://twitter.com/badralbusaidi) to reduce tensions and advance peaceful coexistence.

7. **Bahrain condemns Iranian attack.** The Kingdom of Bahrain strongly condemned what it described as an Iranian attack on Kuwait, according to the Bahraini Foreign Ministry. The statement represented another escalation in regional tensions between Gulf states and Iran following recent [maritime security incidents](https://bit.ly/4dAMr6j).

8. **UAE government AI initiatives.** The Emirates launched new artificial intelligence assistants for government procurement, taxation, customer service and technical support as part of a broader digital transformation. Sheikh Mohammed bin Rashid announced the initiative would transform [half of government](https://twitter.com/HHShkMohd) services and operations using artificial intelligence technology.

9. **Kuwait oil price surge.** Kuwait's crude oil price jumped $4.56 to reach $118.35 per barrel, reflecting continued volatility in global energy markets. The Kuwait Petroleum Corporation announced the significant price increase as regional producers [adjust to market](https://kuna.net.kw/ArticleDetails.aspx?id=3305705) conditions following recent geopolitical developments.

10. **Yemen ceasefire developments.** Saudi Arabia's Yemen envoy Mohammed AlJabir announced plans to visit Sanaa with an Omani delegation to strengthen the truce and support prisoner exchanges. The diplomatic mission aims to consolidate the ceasefire and [facilitate dialogue](https://twitter.com/mohdsalj) between Yemeni components toward a comprehensive political solution.
