1. **Saudi Hajj success.** Saudi Arabia concluded the 1447 AH Hajj season with leaders across the region offering congratulations to King Salman and Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman. Prince Khalid bin Salman thanked God for enabling the Kingdom to serve pilgrims and care for Islam's holy sites. Regional heads of state from Qatar, Kuwait, Oman, Bahrain, and Palestine sent congratulatory messages recognizing Saudi Arabia's organizational achievements during [the pilgrimage](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/ceqpelq5reqo?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss).

2. **Trump Iran deliberation.** President Donald Trump met with advisers to make a "final determination" on whether to proceed with a potential Iran deal, according to the BBC. The US and Iran have reportedly reached tentative agreement to extend ceasefire terms and initiate new nuclear talks. Vice President JD Vance stated the sides are "very close" to an agreement but "not there yet," per [AP News](https://apnews.com/article/iran-us-war-ceasefire-nuclear-talks-cac5206df0f0c7b79fe9321c08d63096).

3. **Trump threatens Oman.** President Trump threatened to "blow up" Oman if the Sultanate does not comply with unspecified demands, according to Arabic media reports. Omani Foreign Minister Badr Albusaidi held discussions with Iran's Deputy Foreign Minister, with both sides emphasizing diplomatic engagement and peaceful coexistence. The threat appears connected to regional tensions over [Iran nuclear negotiations](https://arabic.cnn.com/middle-east/article/2026/05/30/oman-iran-reactions-trump-threatening-hormuz-fm).

4. **Yemen ceasefire progress.** Houthi spokesman Mohammed Abdulsalam announced a three-point agreement that includes canceling recent banking decisions, resuming Yemenia Airlines flights between Sanaa and Jordan with increased frequency, and establishing regular meetings. The IRG government expressed support for diplomatic efforts while the Houthis maintained their naval operations target Israeli-linked [shipping exclusively](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c87qng40wz9o?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss).

5. **Saudi Arabia World Cup preparations.** The Saudi national team began final preparations for the 2026 FIFA World Cup with a match against Ecuador. Princess Reema bint Bandar announced preparations were underway in New York, marking 20 days until Saudi Arabia's tournament opener. The Kingdom continues its sports diplomacy initiatives as part of Vision 2030's [diversification goals](https://www.youm7.com/story/2026/5/30/%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B3%D8%B9%D9%88%D8%AF%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%AA%D9%88%D8%A7%D8%AC%D9%87-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A5%D9%83%D9%88%D8%A7%D8%AF%D9%88%D8%B1-%D9%81%D8%AC%D8%B1%D8%A7-%D8%A7%D8%B3%D8%AA%D8%B9%D8%AF%D8%A7%D8%AF%D8%A7-%D9%84%D9%83%D8%A3%D8%B3-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85-2026/7432068).

6. **UAE tourism achievements.** Sheikh Mohammed bin Rashid reviewed the UAE tourism sector's accomplishments for 2025, highlighting continued growth in visitor arrivals and tourism revenue. The announcement came as the UAE government launched new artificial intelligence initiatives in procurement, taxation, and customer service. Dubai Media Office announced Emirates airline unveiled a special Arsenal FC livery on an A380 aircraft following the club's Premier League [title victory](https://timesofoman.com//article/172368-dream-come-true-anthony-gordon-secures-5-year-deal-with-barcelona).

7. **Qatar Eid greetings.** Emir Sheikh Tamim bin Hamad Al Thani and Prime Minister Mohammed bin Abdulrahman Al Thani extended Eid Al-Adha congratulations to Qatari citizens and the broader Islamic community. The Prime Minister expressed gratitude for his appointment and commitment to serving under the Emir's leadership. Qatar's Government Communications Office emphasized themes of unity, blessing, and [regional prosperity](https://bit.ly/4u5PM22).

8. **Iran-Israel tensions.** Iran prepared to hold a large-scale funeral ceremony for Supreme Leader Ali Khamenei following reports of his death, according to Asharq al-Awsat. Tehran and Ansar Allah announced plans to declare passage through the "Lilibani" tunnel following developments in naval operations. Israeli forces reportedly intercepted multiple projectiles over the [Mediterranean Sea](https://www.reuters.com/business/aerospace-defense/russian-oreshnik-missile-fired-january-was-nine-years-old-ukrainian-experts-say-2026-05-29/).

9. **Bahrain diplomatic outreach.** Bahrain's Foreign Ministry strongly condemned an alleged Iranian attack on Kuwait, expressing solidarity with Kuwait's sovereignty and territorial integrity. The Kingdom's Crown Prince and Prime Minister held telephone conversations with regional leaders and congratulated Malaysia's newly appointed Prime Minister. Bahrain established a new Consulate General in [Laayoune, Morocco](http://ow.ly/F5JJ50CK25B).

10. **Oman-UK cooperation.** Prince William of Wales received His Highness Sayyid Theyazin bin Haitham Al Said at Windsor Castle to discuss UK-Oman relations and collaboration opportunities. The meeting preceded the official launch of the "Oman: The Jewel of Arabia" expedition project in London under joint patronage. Sultan Haitham bin Tarik offered Eid Al-Adha prayers at Sultan Qaboos Mosque in [Sohar](http://n9.cl/adorv).
