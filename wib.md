Good morning from the Gulf. Here's what's happening across the region:

1. **US-Iran ship seizure.** The US military seized an Iranian cargo vessel, according to [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/video/newsfeed/2026/4/20/us-military-releases-video-of-marines-seizing-iranian-ship?traffic_source=rss), with Bloomberg reporting the action cast doubt on potential peace talks between Washington and Tehran. Pakistan offered to host multi-day US-Iran negotiations, but Tehran remained uncertain about participation.

2. **Strait of Hormuz reopening.** More than 20 vessels passed through the Strait of Hormuz on Saturday, Reuters [reported](https://www.reuters.com/world/china/more-than-20-vessels-pass-strait-hormuz-saturday-kpler-data-shows-2026-04-20/), citing Kpler data. Cruise ships seized a brief reopening window to flee the Persian Gulf following 50 days of Iranian blockade, according to Bloomberg.

3. **Saudi-India security talks.** Indian National Security Advisor Ajit Doval met with Saudi Foreign Minister Prince Faisal bin Farhan in Riyadh, the Saudi foreign ministry announced on Twitter/X. They reviewed joint cooperation and discussed regional developments, with Undersecretary for Political Affairs Dr. Saud Al-Sati in attendance.

4. **Oil price volatility.** Oil prices jumped as markets reacted to uncertainty over a US-Iran ceasefire, the Times of Oman [reported](https://timesofoman.com//article/170828-oil-prices-jump-markets-shake-amid-us-iran-ceasefire-uncertainty). The International Energy Agency head pitched an Iraq-Turkey pipeline to bypass Hormuz, according to Hürriyet via Bloomberg.

5. **IRGC influence expanded.** IRGC hardliners now control Iran's military and diplomacy with moderates sidelined, the Times of Oman [reported](https://timesofoman.com//article/170824-irgc-hardliners-in-control-of-irans-military-and-diplomacy-moderates-sidelined-report). Tehran vowed never to cede control of the Strait of Hormuz, a senior Iranian politician told the BBC.

6. **Saudi Yemen envoy active.** Saudi Ambassador to Yemen Mohammed Al-Jabir announced on Twitter/X he would visit Sanaa with an Omani delegation to consolidate the truce, support prisoner exchanges, and discuss dialogue between Yemeni components for a comprehensive political solution.

7. **UAE economic performance.** The UAE economy continued its rise and strengthened its global position in 2026, Sky News Arabia reported. Emirates real estate recorded performance in the first quarter that broke expectations, according to Arabic media reports.

8. **Kuwait-Egypt consultations.** Foreign ministers of Egypt and Kuwait held political consultations in Cairo, confirming the depth of fraternal relations between the two countries, Egypt's foreign ministry [announced](https://www.mfa.gov.eg/ar/Ministers/Details/MinisterActivities/4911). The meeting focused on regional developments and bilateral cooperation.

9. **Libya budget welcome.** Saudi Arabia joined nine other countries including Egypt, Qatar, UAE, Turkey, UK, and US in welcoming the signing of a unified budget for Libya for 2026, the Saudi foreign ministry stated. The countries expressed support for Libyan institutional unity.

10. **Kuwait stock changes.** The ownership structure of three companies listed on the Kuwait Stock Exchange changed, Mubasher Info reported. The Wall Street Journal reported that the UAE requested a financial support mechanism from America, according to Reuters Arabic coverage.
