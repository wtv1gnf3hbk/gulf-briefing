Iranian strikes on Kuwait prompt regional condemnation as tensions escalate across the Gulf following reports of missile and drone attacks targeting the emirate. Regional outlets are leading coverage of what appears to be a major escalation, with Egypt, Arab League, and GCC states [strongly condemning](https://www.facebook.com/khamsenetektesad/posts/1024985116949408/) the Iranian attacks on Kuwait as violations of sovereignty and international law.

The strikes come as former Yemeni President Abdrabbuh Mansour Hadi has [died at 80](https://apnews.com/article/yemen-expresident-abdrabbuh-mansour-hadi-died-b3afd7b3679af14d335578e9560c7cd8), according to AP News, marking the end of a key figure in Yemen's political transition. The IRG leadership [announced the death](https://www.facebook.com/khamsenetektesad/posts/1024985116949408/) of the former president who led Yemen during critical periods of the ongoing conflict. Parallel reports suggest continued diplomatic efforts, with Reuters and AP News reporting tentative US-Iran ceasefire agreements and nuclear talks, though these remain unverified by Gulf sources.
