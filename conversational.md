The US and Iran appeared to be closing in on extending their ceasefire agreement and launching new nuclear talks, according to [AP reports](https://apnews.com/article/iran-us-war-ceasefire-nuclear-talks-cac5206df0f0c7b79fe9321c08d63096), though President Trump has not yet made a final decision on moving forward with the deal. Qatar's Emir Sheikh Tamim and Palestinian President Mahmoud Abbas were among regional leaders who praised the Kingdom's management of the pilgrimage, per the Saudi Press Agency. ## Official Signals

Foreign Minister Prince Faisal bin Farhan [praised Trump's decision](https://twitter.com/FaisalbinFarhan) to give diplomacy a chance on the Iran talks, stating that Saudi Arabia "highly appreciates" the US President's approach to ending the war and restoring maritime navigation in the Strait of Hormuz. The statement signals potential Saudi support for renewed US-Iran negotiations.

Omani Foreign Minister Badr Albusaidi [emphasized](https://twitter.com/badralbusaidi) the "constructive nature" of Oman-Iran dialogue following meetings with Iranian Deputy FM Gharibabadi, reaffirming Oman's role in supporting regional de-escalation efforts and "peaceful coexistence."
