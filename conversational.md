Iran's escalating missile and drone attacks across the Gulf region dominated developments today, with Iranian forces [striking a](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/giant-oil-tanker-off-dubai-hit-by-iranian-strike-trump-threatens-obliterate-iran-2026-03-31/) Kuwaiti oil tanker in Dubai waters and launching waves of projectiles at UAE and Saudi targets. UAE air defenses intercepted [48 Iranian](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-03-31/saudi-chemical-plant-shuts-down-as-war-disrupts-supply-chains) missiles and drones on March 31 alone, according to the UAE Defense Ministry, while Saudi Civil Defense reported [minor injuries](https://twitter.com/MOISaudiArabia) from drone debris in Al-Kharj Governorate. The attacks prompted major Saudi petrochemical firm Sadara Chemical to [halt production](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-03-31/saudi-chemical-plant-shuts-down-as-war-disrupts-supply-chains), Bloomberg reports, citing supply chain disruptions from the widening conflict.

Gulf leaders moved quickly to coordinate their response to the Iranian offensive. Qatar's Emir Sheikh Tamim met with UAE President Sheikh Mohammed bin Zayed to [discuss regional](https://www.aljazeera.net/news/2026/3/31/%D8%B9%D8%A7%D8%AC%D9%84-%D8%A3%D9%85%D9%8A%D8%B1-%D9%82%D8%B7%D8%B1-%D8%A8%D8%AD%D8%AB%D8%AA-%D8%AE%D9%84%D8%A7%D9%84-%D9%84%D9%82%D8%A7%D8%A6%D9%8A-%D9%85%D8%B9-%D8%B1%D8%A6%D9%8A%D8%B3) developments and "intensifying joint coordination to protect the security of our countries," according to Qatar's Prime Minister Sheikh Mohammed bin Abdulrahman Al Thani. The economic toll is mounting rapidly, with a new UN study finding the conflict could cost Arab nations [up to](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/3/31/one-month-of-war-in-middle-east-cost-arab-states-up-to-194bn-undp) $194 billion in its first month alone, Al Jazeera reports.

**Energy & Economy**

• The Aramco-Dow joint venture Sadara Chemical [suspended operations](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/aramco-dow-joint-venture-sadara-chemical-halts-production-over-middle-east-2026-03-31/) following Iranian attacks and supply disruptions, Reuters reports, marking the first major Saudi industrial closure due to the conflict.

• Abu Dhabi's 2PointZero sovereign fund secured a [$2.25 billion](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-03-31/abu-dhabi-s-2pointzero-splashes-out-2-25-billion-in-us-gas-deal) US natural gas deal, Bloomberg reports, continuing UAE energy diversification plans despite regional tensions.

• Iran has been [pressuring Houthis](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-03-30/iran-presses-houthis-on-red-sea-shipping-european-officials-say) to escalate Red Sea shipping attacks, according to European officials cited by Bloomberg.

• Arab economies face potential losses of [$200 billion](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-03-31/arab-nations-may-lose-200-billion-from-iran-war-un-study-finds) from the Iran conflict, per a UN Development Programme study reported by Bloomberg.

**Saudi Arabia**

Saudi Arabia condemned Iran's [repeated attacks](https://twitter.com/KSAMOFA) against the Kingdom, with Prince Khalid bin Salman discussing Iranian aggression in calls with British Defense Minister John Healey and Hungarian Defense Minister Kristóf Szalay-Bobrovniczky. The Kingdom's Civil Defense reported [debris injuries](https://twitter.com/SaudiDCD) in Al-Kharj from intercepted Iranian drones. Saudi Foreign Minister Prince Faisal bin Farhan chaired an international conference in Paris on Palestinian statehood, securing France's commitment to [recognize Palestine](https://twitter.com/FaisalbinFarhan), according to his Twitter account.

**UAE**

The UAE bore the brunt of Iranian attacks, with defense forces intercepting [8 ballistic](https://twitter.com/modgovae) missiles, 4 cruise missiles, and 36 drones on March 31, the Defense Ministry reported. Four foreign nationals sustained [minor injuries](https://twitter.com/moiuae) from falling debris in southern Dubai, according to the Interior Ministry. President Sheikh Mohammed bin Zayed praised the country's [defensive capabilities](https://twitter.com/HHShkMohd) during a Cabinet session while approving new international partnerships in technology and space sectors.

**Qatar**

Qatar positioned itself as a potential mediator, with officials saying the country is ["ready to help"](https://www.aljazeera.com/video/newsfeed/2026/3/31/qatar-says-ready-to-help-with-us-iran-negotiations) with US-Iran negotiations, Al Jazeera reports. Prime Minister Sheikh Mohammed bin Abdulrahman Al Thani held coordination meetings with both UAE and Saudi leadership on [regional security](https://twitter.com/MBA_AlThani_).

**Bahrain / Kuwait / Oman**

Bahrain condemned Iranian [attacks on Kuwait](https://twitter.com/bahdiplomatic), with the Foreign Ministry participating in GCC coordination calls with Russia and Jordan. Oman's Foreign Minister Badr Albusaidi warned of potential [humanitarian crises](https://twitter.com/badralbusaidi) from the expanding conflict and advocated for Strait of Hormuz safe passage arrangements.

**Yemen**

Yemen's Iranian-backed Houthis continued Red Sea operations, with spokesperson Mohammed Abdulsalam claiming US forces opened fire [hysterically](https://twitter.com/abdusalamsalah) at Yemeni naval reconnaissance aircraft. Saudi Yemen envoy Mohammed Al Jaber announced plans to [visit Sana'a](https://twitter.com/mohdsalj) with an Omani delegation to consolidate ceasefire efforts and support prisoner exchanges. A Yemeni lawmaker filed suit against US military veterans, [accusing them](https://apnews.com/article/yemeni-lawmaker-assassination-attempt-uae-spear-operations-c627ad64f9a90a76349fcadfdfe7a17a) of attempting to kill him, AP reports.

**Official Signals**

Gulf allies are privately urging Trump to [continue fighting](https://apnews.com/article/trump-iran-saudi-arabia-mbs-gulf-war-uae-89f690b952fe28d3140c537b70fa5051) until Iran is "decisively defeated," AP reports, signaling coordinated regional pressure on Washington. Saudi Foreign Ministry issued a statement reaffirming that the Kingdom's position on [Palestinian statehood](https://twitter.com/rbalsaud) "is firm and unwavering," following Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman's recent comments.

**Coverage Flags**

International outlets are ahead on the economic impact story, with detailed UN and European assessments of war costs while regional Arabic press focuses primarily on attack details and diplomatic meetings.
