**Gulf News Briefing - June 2, 2026**

The US launched [airstrikes on](https://apnews.com/article/iran-us-irael-war-kuwait-strikes-88daa9f90b48baaa7beb18e35515c59d) Iranian military sites following Iranian missile attacks on American forces stationed in Kuwait, marking a dramatic escalation as the Iran conflict enters its fourth month. The strikes targeted Iranian radar installations as tensions surge across the region. Goldman Sachs expects [elevated refined](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/goldman-sachs-expects-elevated-refined-fuel-margins-through-2026-hormuz-2026-06-02/) fuel margins through 2026 due to the Strait of Hormuz disruption, while Asia's imports of US crude have surged but cannot offset the strategic waterway losses.

**Saudi Foreign Minister** Prince Faisal bin Farhan [appreciates Trump's](https://twitter.com/faisalbinfarhan) decision to give diplomacy a chance to restore security and freedom of maritime navigation in the Strait of Hormuz to its pre-February 28 state. The Kingdom continues positioning itself as a diplomatic mediator as regional tensions mount. Bahrain has [banned citizens](https://arabic.cnn.com/middle-east/article/2026/06/02/bahrains-interior-ministry-announces-travel-ban-on-citizens-to-iran-iraq) from traveling to Iran and Iraq due to ongoing security tensions, per the Interior Ministry.
