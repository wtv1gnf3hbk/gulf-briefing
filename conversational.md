**Top News**

Iranian forces launched missile and drone attacks on Kuwait early this morning, marking a dangerous escalation in the regional conflict that has Washington and Gulf allies scrambling for responses. [Kuwait's military](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/kuwaiti-army-says-air-defences-intercepting-hostile-missile-drone-attacks-2026-05-28/) confirmed its air defenses intercepted the hostile attacks, while Reuters reports the [US conducted](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/us-carries-out-new-strikes-iran-against-military-site-official-says-2026-05-27/) retaliatory strikes against Iranian military sites and drone facilities. The attacks come as the fragile ceasefire between Iran and the US faces mounting pressure, with Trump dismissing reports of any Strait of Hormuz deal.

The assault on Kuwait represents Iran's boldest move yet against a GCC state, prompting swift condemnation from across the Gulf. The [UAE strongly](https://www.france24.com/ar/%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A3%D8%AE%D8%A8%D8%A7%D8%B1%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%B3%D8%AA%D9%85%D8%B1%D8%A9/20260528-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A5%D9%85%D8%A7%D8%B1%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%AA%D9%8F%D8%AF%D9%8A%D9%86-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%87%D8%AC%D9%85%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AC%D8%AF%D9%8A%D8%AF%D8%A9-%D8%B9%D9%84%D9%89-%D8%AF%D9%88%D9%84%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%83%D9%88%D9%8A%D8%AA) condemned the new attacks according to France 24, while Bahrain's foreign ministry [issued condemnations](https://bit.ly/4dAMr6j) of the Iranian strikes. Al Jazeera reports Gulf countries are [rallying around](https://www.aljazeera.net/news/2026/5/28/%D9%87%D8%AC%D9%85%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%A8%D8%B5%D9%88%D8%A7%D8%B1%D9%8A%D8%AE-%D9%88%D9%85%D8%B3%D9%8A%D8%B1%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%AA%D8%B3%D8%AA%D9%87%D8%AF%D9%81-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%83%D9%88%D9%8A%D8%AA) Kuwait following the rocket and drone attacks.

**Energy & Economy**

• Global air cargo demand [rose 4%](https://timesofoman.com//article/172334-air-cargo-demand-rises-4-in-april-despite-middle-east-disruptions-iata) in April despite Middle East disruptions, according to IATA data reported by the Times of Oman.

**Country Watch**

**Saudi Arabia**
The kingdom is managing the annual Hajj pilgrimage [in sweltering](https://apnews.com/article/hajj-pilgrimage-mecca-saudi-arabia-b3bac12f3dec8e927dfd4e1a961572fa) heat as AP News reports nearly two million Muslims perform the rituals. Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman [received Eid](https://spa.gov.sa/N2601710) well-wishers at the Royal Court in Mina Palace, including GCC leaders and military commanders. Prince Khalid bin Salman [met with](https://twitter.com/kbsalsaud) UK National Security Adviser Jonathan Powell to discuss bilateral cooperation and regional de-escalation efforts. The kingdom and crown prince [offered condolences](https://twitter.com/spagov) to Yemen's government following the death of former president Abd Rabbuh Mansour Hadi.

**UAE**
Sheikh Mohammed bin Rashid [reviewed achievements](https://twitter.com/UAEmediaoffice) of the UAE's tourism sector for 2025, according to the government media office. The UAE [announced restoration](https://twitter.com/ABZayed) of the Umayyad Mosque in Damascus with support from Sheikha Fatima bint Mubarak, per Foreign Minister Abdullah bin Zayed. Tahnoon bin Zayed [chaired MGX's](https://twitter.com/hhtbzayed) second board meeting for 2026, reviewing new investments and growth priorities for the sovereign wealth vehicle.

**Qatar**
Mohammed bin Abdulrahman Al Thani was [honored by](https://twitter.com/MBA_AlThani_) the Emir's trust in appointing him Prime Minister, while Majed Al Ansari was [appointed as](https://twitter.com/majedalansari) advisor to the Prime Minister and official Foreign Ministry spokesman. Qatar News Agency [strongly denied](https://twitter.com/majedalansari) reports suggesting Qatar offered $12 billion to Iran to secure a deal.

**Kuwait**
Beyond the Iranian attacks, [Kuwait's Amir](https://kuna.net.kw/ArticleDetails.aspx?id=3305606), Crown Prince, and Prime Minister all [offered condolences](https://kuna.net.kw/ArticleDetails.aspx?id=3305608) to Yemen's leadership over Hadi's death, per KUNA.

**Yemen**
