Iran [escalated attacks](https://apnews.com/article/iran-us-irael-war-kuwait-strikes-88daa9f90b48baaa7beb18e35515c59d) on Kuwait with missiles and drones following US strikes on Iranian military sites, according to [AP reports](https://apnews.com/article/iran-us-irael-war-kuwait-strikes-88daa9f90b48baaa7beb18e35515c59d). The [BBC reports](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/crlpy8n7pr6o?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss) that US forces in Kuwait were targeted as Tehran responded to American attacks on Iranian radar installations. [Oil prices rose](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/oil-prices-rise-more-than-2-israel-moves-further-into-lebanon-2026-05-31/) more than 2% as tensions between Washington and Tehran reached new heights following the exchange of strikes.

Gulf states moved quickly to condemn the Iranian attacks on Kuwait. Saudi Foreign Minister Prince Faisal bin Farhan [posted that](https://twitter.com/FaisalbinFarhan) the Kingdom "highly appreciates the US President Donald Trump's decision to give diplomacy a chance" and called for ending the war while restoring maritime navigation in the Strait of Hormuz. Multiple Arab states including Egypt, Qatar, and Oman issued statements denouncing Iran's strikes on Kuwait, with regional outlets reporting widespread diplomatic condemnation of Tehran's actions.

**Country Watch**

**Saudi Arabia**
Saudi Arabia condemned both Israeli aggression in Lebanon and Iranian attacks on Kuwait in separate Foreign Ministry statements. Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman received a phone call from French President Emmanuel Macron discussing bilateral cooperation and regional security developments. The Kingdom issued [327 promotions](https://twitter.com/spagov) for Public Prosecution members at various judicial ranks through a royal decree.

**UAE** 
The UAE launched the [National Textile Recycling Initiative](https://twitter.com/UAEmediaoffice) "Naseej" as part of transitioning the textile sector to a circular economic system. Dubai announced plans for AI assistants in procurement, taxes, and customer service as part of a broader government AI transformation program. Sheikh Abdullah bin Zayed highlighted that the UAE [leads globally](https://twitter.com/ABZayed) in AI talent concentration, growing 121% between 2019 and 2025.

**Qatar**
Qatar's Deputy PM and Foreign Minister Mohammed bin Abdulrahman Al Thani was [appointed as PM](https://twitter.com/MBA_AlThani_) by Emir Sheikh Tamim bin Hamad Al Thani. Qatar strongly condemned Iran's repeated attacks on Kuwait, calling them a violation of sovereignty and international law, per [QNA reports](https://twitter.com/QNAEnglish).

**Bahrain / Kuwait / Oman**
Bahrain's Civil Defense [rescued five children](https://twitter.com/moi_bahrain) from a house fire in Aali without casualties. Kuwait's Foreign Minister met with the IAEA Director General Rafael Grossi, according to [KUNA reports](https://twitter.com/kuna_en). Oman's Foreign Minister stressed with his Dutch counterpart the necessity to intensify diplomatic efforts for regional de-escalation, per [QNA reports](https://twitter.com/QNAEnglish).

**Yemen**
Former IRG President Abd Rabbuh Mansour Hadi died, prompting condolences from Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman who [called Hadi's son](https://twitter.com/spagov). The IRG government condemned UAE airstrikes on National Army forces in Aden and Abyan, according to [Saba News Agency](https://twitter.com/sabanew_). Houthi official Mohammed Abdulsalam announced a text agreement canceling recent banking decisions and resuming Yemenia Airlines flights between Sanaa and Jordan.

**Official Signals**

• Saudi Ambassador to Yemen Mohammed AlJabir [tweeted](https://twitter.com/mohdsalj) "#Al-Hudaydah is liberated.....#Yemen is breathing" and announced plans to visit Sanaa with an Omani delegation to consolidate the truce and support prisoner exchanges.

• Oman's Foreign Minister Badr Albusaidi met with Iran's Deputy FM, [stating](https://twitter.com/badralbusaidi) "we reaffirmed the importance of diplomatic engagement on all fronts" and that Oman will continue supporting regional de-escalation efforts.

**Coverage Flags**

International outlets are leading coverage of the Iran-Kuwait strikes story, with Western media providing more comprehensive reporting on the US-Iran exchange than regional Gulf press. The death of former Yemeni President Hadi received extensive Arabic-language coverage but limited English reporting, suggesting potential for deeper NYT analysis of his legacy and impact on Yemen's ongoing conflict.
