Good afternoon from the Gulf. A dramatic escalation has gripped the region as US forces struck Iranian military positions and Iran retaliated by targeting American troops stationed in Kuwait. [The BBC reports](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/crlpy8n7pr6o) that US operations hit Iranian radar sites after Iran launched attacks damaging 20 US military installations across the region since the conflict began. [Reuters indicates](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/opec-likely-raise-july-oil-output-target-despite-hormuz-disruption-sources-say-2026-06-01/) OPEC+ is likely to proceed with planned July oil output increases despite ongoing disruptions in the Strait of Hormuz, as energy markets face mounting pressure from the regional tensions.

The conflict has drawn sharp condemnation across the Gulf. Saudi Foreign Minister Prince Faisal bin Farhan [expressed appreciation](https://twitter.com/FaisalbinFarhan/status) for President Trump's decision to "give diplomacy a chance" while calling for restoration of maritime security in the Strait of Hormuz to pre-February 28 conditions. The Saudi MFA [strongly condemned](https://twitter.com/KSAMOFA/status) both Israeli incursions into Lebanon and what it termed "sinful and repeated Iranian attacks" on Kuwait. Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman received calls from French President Macron and Chad's president, discussing regional diplomatic efforts and security concerns.
