Former Yemeni President Abdrabbuh Mansour Hadi [died at 80](https://apnews.com/article/yemen-expresident-abdrabbuh-mansour-hadi-died-b3afd7b3679af14d335578e9560c7cd8), marking the end of a tumultuous political career that saw him govern mostly from exile following the Houthi takeover of Sanaa in 2014. The internationally recognized leader spent his final years in Saudi Arabia, where he had sought refuge after being ousted from power. His death comes as Yemen remains divided between the Iran-backed Houthis controlling the north and the Saudi-backed internationally recognized government (IRG) based in Aden.

Trump administration officials continue signaling movement on Iran negotiations, with Vice President Vance stating the US is ["very close"](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c87qng40wz9o) to a deal but ["not there yet"](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c74dy9jw1q9o). Saudi Foreign Minister Prince Faisal bin Farhan [praised Trump's](https://twitter.com/FaisalbinFarhan) decision to "give diplomacy a chance" to restore security and freedom of navigation in the Strait of Hormuz. Meanwhile, European [Union condemns](https://www.kuna.net.kw/ow.ly/gJLc30sW7VU) the latest Iranian attack on Kuwait, according to KUNA.
