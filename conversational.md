**US-Iran tensions dominate Gulf attention as Trump extends diplomatic deadline**

President Trump has given diplomacy [additional time](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c74dy9jw1q9o?at_medium=RSS&amp;at_campaign=rss) to reach an Iran deal after threatening military action, following reports that US and Iran [reached preliminary](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/us-iran-reach-deal-need-trumps-final-approval-axios-reports-2026-05-28/) agreement terms. The diplomatic opening comes as [Iran responded](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c98r2qy5809o?at_medium=RSS&amp;at_campaign=rss) to fresh US strikes with its own military action. Saudi Foreign Minister Prince Faisal bin Farhan [welcomed Trump's decision](https://twitter.com/FaisalbinFarhan) to "give diplomacy a chance to reach an acceptable agreement to end the war" and restore Strait of Hormuz navigation freedom.

Former Yemen President Abdrabbuh Mansour Hadi [died at 80](https://apnews.com/article/yemen-expresident-abdrabbuh-mansour-hadi-died-b3afd7b3679af14d335578e9560c7cd8) following a prolonged illness, according to AP News reports. The IRG-aligned leader governed mostly from exile during Yemen's civil war. Saudi Yemen envoy Mohammed AlJabir is planning to [visit Sana'a](https://twitter.com/mohdsalj) with an Omani delegation to consolidate the truce and discuss prisoner exchanges.

**Energy & Economy**

• **Saudi Arabia** may [lower July](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/saudi-arabia-may-lower-july-oil-prices-asia-demand-weakens-2026-05-29/) oil prices to Asia as demand weakens, Reuters reports, following broader market concerns about energy security.

• **Japan's oil imports** hit their [lowest since 1962](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/japans-april-oil-imports-fall-nearly-66-yy-iran-war-disrupts-supply-2026-05-29/) in April, falling 66% year-on-year as Iran conflict disrupts supply chains, according to Reuters.

• **Kuwait oil prices** jumped [$4.56 to $118.35](https://kuna.net.kw/ArticleDetails.aspx?id=3305705) per barrel, KUNA reports, reflecting regional supply concerns.

• **UAE leads globally** in AI talent concentration, with [121% growth](https://twitter.com/ABZayed) between 2019-2025, Sheikh Abdullah bin Zayed notes.

**Saudi Arabia**

Prince Saud bin Mishal [announced the success](https://spa.gov.sa/N2602703) of the 1447 AH Hajj season, with millions of pilgrims completing rituals safely according to the Saudi Press Agency. Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman [received Chad's president](https://spa.gov.sa/N2602420) at Mina Palace during the Hajj, discussing bilateral relations and regional cooperation. Defense Minister Prince Khalid bin Salman [met with UK](https://twitter.com/kbsalsaud) National Security Adviser Jonathan Powell to review Saudi-British cooperation and regional de-escalation efforts.

**UAE**

Sheikh Mohammed bin Rashid [reviewed 2025](https://twitter.com/UAEmediaoffice) tourism sector achievements and launched AI assistants for procurement, taxation, and customer service during a federal government retreat. Dubai Media Office announced Emirates Airlines will unveil a [special Arsenal](https://twitter.com/DXBMediaOffice) A380 livery celebrating the club's Premier League victory. Abu Dhabi's Department of Government Empowerment expanded [employment opportunities](https://twitter.com/ADMediaOffice) for Emirati talent through the Mawaheb Talent Hub in Q1 2026.

**Qatar**

Prime Minister Mohammed bin Abdulrahman Al Thani [expressed gratitude](https://twitter.com/MBA_AlThani_) for his appointment by Emir Tamim, pledging to work with fellow ministers in serving the nation. Foreign Ministry spokesman Majed Al Ansari [denied reports](https://twitter.com/majedalansari) that Qatar offered $12 billion to Iran for a deal, calling the claims false and designed to sabotage diplomatic efforts.

**Bahrain / Kuwait / Oman**

Bahrain [strongly condemned](https://bit.ly/4dAMr6j) Iran's attack on Kuwait, according to the Foreign Ministry. Kuwait's PM [congratulated Saudi](https://kuna.net.kw/ArticleDetails.aspx?id=3305698) Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman on the successful Hajj season, KUNA reports. Sultan Haitham of Oman [performed Eid prayers](http://n9.cl/adorv) at Sultan Qaboos Mosque in Sohar, while Foreign Minister Badr Albusaidi [held discussions](https://twitter.com/badralbusaidi) with Iran's Deputy Foreign Minister emphasizing diplomatic engagement.

**Yemen**

The IRG Leadership Council [called for unity](https://twitter.com/sabanew_) and directing energies toward restoring state institutions. Ahmed Hamid Lamlas was [appointed governor](http://sabanew.net) of Aden Governorate, according to Saba News Agency. Houthi negotiator Mohammed Abdulsalam [announced an agreement](https://twitter.com/abdusalamsalah) with Saudi Arabia to cancel banking restrictions, resume Yemenia flights to Jordan, and increase flight frequency to three daily.

**Official Signals**

Prince Faisal bin Farhan's statement [appreciating Trump's](https://twitter.com/FaisalbinFarhan) diplomatic extension signals Saudi support for negotiations over military escalation with Iran. Qatar's denial of the $12 billion payment claim indicates [sensitivity about](https://twitter.com/majedalansari) its mediation role being mischaracterized during critical talks.

**Coverage Flags**

International outlets are ahead on Trump's Iran diplomatic timeline and the death of Yemen's former president. The gap between Arabic and English coverage on Houthi-Saudi banking agreements deserves NYT attention.
