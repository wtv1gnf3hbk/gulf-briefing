**Gulf News Briefing - Saturday, April 18, 2026**

The broader Iran war continues reshaping Gulf diplomatic calculations, with Saudi Foreign Minister Prince Faisal bin Farhan leading a quadrilateral meeting in Turkey's Antalya focused on Iranian developments. The [Saudi Foreign Ministry](https://www.alarabiya.net/amp/saudi-today/2026/04/18/%D8%A7%D8%AC%D8%AA%D9%85%D8%A7%D8%B9-%D9%84%D9%88%D8%B2%D8%B1%D8%A7%D8%A1-%D8%AE%D8%A7%D8%B1%D8%AC%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B3%D8%B9%D9%88%D8%AF%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D9%88%D8%AA%D8%B1%D9%83%D9%8A%D8%A7-%D9%88%D8%A8%D8%A7%D9%83%D8%B3%D8%AA%D8%A7%D9%86-%D9%88%D9%85%D8%B5%D8%B1-%D9%81%D9%8A-%D8%A7%D9%86%D8%B7%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%8A%D8%A7-) confirmed discussions on "supporting Pakistani mediation efforts" for a permanent ceasefire between Iran and the United States, while Prince Khalid bin Salman continued his defense outreach to European allies, condemning Iran's "continued flagrant attacks on the Kingdom" in meetings with Greek and British ministers.

Meanwhile, shipping tensions persist as Bloomberg reports [empty LNG tankers](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-04-17/empty-lng-tankers-attempt-hormuz-crossing-into-persian-gulf) attempting Hormuz crossings into the Persian Gulf, reflecting continued uncertainty over energy transit routes. Trump's announcement thanking Gulf states for their "tremendous" support, according to [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/video/newsfeed/2026/4/18/trump-thanks-gulf-states-for-their-tremendous-support?traffic_source=rss), signals continued regional alignment as U.S.-Iran talks are set to resume in Pakistan on Monday, per [Times of Oman](https://timesofoman.com//article/170748-next-round-of-us-iran-talks-likely-to-be-held-in-pakistan-on-monday-iranian-sources).

## Energy & Economy

• The International Energy Agency warns that [Middle East energy output](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/loss-energy-output-mideast-will-take-about-two-years-recover-iea-says-2026-04-17/) losses will require about two years to recover, according to Reuters, as 50 days of Iran war has cost [$50 billion](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/how-50-days-iran-war-led-loss-50-billion-worth-oil-2026-04-17/) worth of oil production.

• AP reports [Persian Gulf tensions](https://apnews.com/article/iran-war-gasoline-prices-strait-hormuz-dbd3d413017078988cacac046169d651) could prevent gasoline prices from dropping quickly, while Bloomberg notes [oil tankers](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-04-17/oil-tankers-make-dash-toward-hormuz-as-iran-says-strait-is-open) are making dashes toward Hormuz as Iran says the strait remains open.

• Gold markets show mixed signals, with Times of Oman reporting [resilient demand](https://timesofoman.com//article/170749-gold-demand-set-to-remain-resilient-ahead-of-akshaya-tritiya) ahead of Akshaya Tritiya, while Youm7 notes UAE [gold markets](https://www.youm7.com/story/2026/4/18/%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B0%D9%87%D8%A8-%D9%81%D9%89-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A5%D9%85%D8%A7%D8%B1%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D9%8A%D8%AA%D8%AD%D8%B1%D9%83-%D8%B9%D8%B1%D8%B6%D9%8A%D9%8B%D8%A7-%D9%85%D8%B9-%D8%B6%D8%BA%D9%88%D8%B7-%D8%AA%D9%87%D8%AF%D8%A6%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A3%D8%B3%D9%88%D8%A7%D9%82/7380701) moving sideways due to calming market pressures.

## Country Watch

**Saudi Arabia**
Defense Minister Prince Khalid bin Salman continues European outreach, meeting with Greek Defense Minister Nikos Dendias and British Defense Minister John Healey to discuss Iran's "continued attacks on the Kingdom." The Saudi [Interior Ministry](https://twitter.com/MOISaudiArabia) highlighted the eighth year of the Makkah Road Initiative, with the first Pakistani pilgrims arriving in Medina carrying 155 pilgrims, according to Al Ekhbariya TV correspondent Muhammad Sheikh.

**UAE** 
Anwar Gargash condemned Turkish Defense Minister statements as representing "a new downfall for his country's diplomacy," stating that "colonial illusions" have no place in modern diplomacy. Sheikh Mohammed bin Rashid celebrated the UAE entering the [World Trade Organization's](https://twitter.com/HHShkMohd) top ten merchandise exporters for the first time, advancing from 17th to ninth place globally in just five years.

**Qatar**
Prime Minister Sheikh Mohammed bin Abdulrahman Al Thani participated in the Antalya Diplomatic Forum, emphasizing Qatar's "established experience in mediation" and conviction that crises are resolved "at the dialogue table, not in the fields of confrontation." The PM also held talks with Omani Sultan Haitham bin Tariq on regional coordination.

**Yemen**
Saudi Ambassador Mohammed AlJabir announced plans to visit Sanaa with an Omani delegation "with the aim of consolidating the truce and ceasefire" and supporting prisoner exchanges. Houthi spokesman Mohammed Abdulsalam detailed a new [agreement text](https://twitter.com/abdusalamsalah) including bank decision cancellations and resumed Yemenia Airlines flights to Jordan and Cairo. The IRG's Mohammed Al-Eryani welcomed Lebanon's ceasefire announcement while continuing to condemn Houthi "intellectual deviation."

## Official Signals

Saudi Foreign Minister Prince Faisal bin Farhan confirmed the Kingdom's position on Palestinian statehood as "firm and unwavering," stating that Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman "clearly and unequivocally reaffirmed this stance." Qatar's new PM Sheikh Mohammed bin Abdulrahman Al Thani emphasized Qatar brings its "established experience in mediation" to current regional challenges, while UAE's Anwar Gargash bluntly told Turkey to "stop its interference in Arab affairs."
