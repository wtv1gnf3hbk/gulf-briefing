The most consequential development in the Gulf today concerns former Yemen President Abdrabbuh Mansour Hadi, who [died at](https://apnews.com/article/yemen-expresident-abdrabbuh-mansour-hadi-died-b3afd7b3679af14d335578e9560c7cd8) age 80, according to AP News. The internationally-recognized leader, who governed mostly from exile during Yemen's civil war, passed away following years of political upheaval that saw the Iran-backed Houthis seize control of much of northern Yemen, including the capital Sanaa. His death marks the end of an era for the internationally-recognized government (IRG) faction that has been fighting the Houthis since 2014.
