**Gulf News Briefing**

Trump's apparent threat to "blow up" Oman over Iran talks has escalated tensions across the region as diplomatic efforts to reach a nuclear agreement continue. The [BBC reports](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c0r2d40r91qo?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss) the US and Iran are "very close" to a deal but "not there yet," according to Vice President Vance, following President Trump's meeting to make a "final determination" on Iran. [DW reports](https://www.dw.com/ar/%D8%AA%D8%B1%D8%A7%D9%85%D8%A8-%D9%8A%D9%87%D8%AF%D8%AF-%D8%A8-%D9%86%D8%B3%D9%81-%D8%B9%D9%8F%D9%85%D8%A7%D9%86-%D9%84%D9%85%D8%A7%D8%B0%D8%A7-%D9%8A%D8%B5%D8%B9%D9%91%D8%AF-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A2%D9%86/video-77338045) the escalation comes as Oman continues its traditional mediator role. [CNN Arabic warns](https://arabic.cnn.com/middle-east/article/2026/05/30/oman-warns-suspected-mine-seen-in-strait-of-hormuz) of a suspected mine in the Strait of Hormuz, raising critical questions about navigation security.

Meanwhile, the annual Hajj pilgrimage concluded successfully with [AP reporting](https://apnews.com/video/muslim-pilgrims-conclude-annual-hajj-pilgrimage-with-final-circling-around-kaaba-11cdb58d450a4063850b90251f25afa8) Muslim pilgrims completing their final circumambulation around the Kaaba, while Gulf leaders congratulated Saudi Arabia on another successful pilgrimage season.

## **Energy & Economy**

• [Times of Oman reports](https://timesofoman.com//article/172382-hormuz-disruption-remains-most-consequential-variable-for-indias-external-price-outlook-finance-ministry-review) Hormuz disruption remains the "most consequential variable" for India's external price outlook according to a Finance Ministry review, highlighting the strait's global economic importance.

• The UAE economy expanded 6.2% with GDP rising to [AED 1.9 trillion](https://timesofoman.com//article/172380-oman-hosts-a-strategic-project-for-production-of-lithium-ion-battery-materials) in 2025, per the UAE Government Media Office.

• [Times of Oman reveals](https://timesofoman.com//article/172380-oman-hosts-a-strategic-project-for-production-of-lithium-ion-battery-materials) Oman is hosting a strategic project for lithium-ion battery materials production as the sultanate diversifies its economy.

## **Country Watch**

### **Saudi Arabia**
Saudi security forces conducted a major immigration sweep, with [Interior Ministry announcing](https://twitter.com/MOISaudiArabia) 8,090 arrests for residency, work, and border security violations within a week. Prince Abdulaziz bin Saud praised Hajj security forces following the successful completion of the pilgrimage season. The kingdom continues preparations for the 2026 World Cup, with the Saudi national team beginning training ahead of their opening match in 20 days.

### **UAE** 
[WAM reports](https://wam.ae/a/b1n6twr) UAE leaders congratulated the President of El Salvador on re-election, while Emirates Airlines unveiled a special Arsenal-themed A380 livery celebrating the club's Premier League triumph. The UAE advised citizens against travel to three African countries, according to Sky News Arabia.

### **Qatar**
Qatar News Agency [reports](https://ow.ly/qmiV50Z5RQk) Gaza's death toll has risen to 72,938 with 172,919 injured since the Israeli offensive began. The UAE Football Association parted ways with national team coach Cosmin Olaroiu, per [QNA](https://ow.ly/ekHP50Z5RCB).

### **Bahrain / Kuwait / Oman**
[Bahrain Civil Defense](https://policemc.gov.bh/en/news/78966/) rescued five children from a house fire in Aali without casualties. Sultan Haitham performed Eid prayers in Sohar, while Prince William received Omani Culture Minister Sayyid Theyazin at Windsor Castle to launch the "Oman: The Jewel of Arabia" expedition project.

### **Yemen**
The Presidential Leadership Council (IRG) appointed Ahmed Hamid Lamlas as governor of Aden Governorate, according to [Saba News Agency](https://twitter.com/sabanew_). Former President Abdrabbuh Mansour Hadi's funeral drew large crowds in Aden, with leadership council members carrying his body to its final resting place. Southern Transitional Council leader Aidarous al-Zubaidi performed Umrah rituals, while tensions continue between different Yemeni factions over governance.

## **Official Signals**

Saudi Foreign Minister Prince Faisal bin Farhan [posted](https://twitter.com/FaisalbinFarhan) that "The Kingdom of Saudi Arabia highly appreciates the US President Donald Trump's decision to give diplomacy a chance to reach an acceptable agreement to end the war, restore the security and freedom of maritime navigation in the Strait of Hormuz to its state prior to February 28th."

UAE's Anwar Gargash [criticized](https://twitter.com/AnwarGargash) Turkey's Defense Minister statement as representing "a new downfall for his country's diplomacy," saying "Relations are not managed by threats and intimidation, and there is no place for colonial illusions in this time."

## **Coverage Flags**

International outlets are ahead on Iran-Oman tensions, with Arabic coverage focusing more on local diplomatic responses than the broader strategic implications of Trump's escalatory language toward Oman.
