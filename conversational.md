The most significant story breaking in the Gulf today is the Trump administration's unprecedented warning to Oman over potential Strait of Hormuz toll facilitation. [Reuters reports](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/us-warns-oman-not-engage-facilitating-tolls-strait-hormuz-2026-05-28/) that the US has warned Oman not to engage in facilitating tolls for the critical waterway, marking a sharp escalation in tensions with a traditionally neutral Gulf ally. This comes as [AP News confirms](https://apnews.com/article/iran-us-war-oil-may-28-2026-8f5ed2813ba63df7ae9ccbe991688d29) US and Iranian negotiators have reached a tentative deal to extend the ceasefire and start new nuclear talks, though [the BBC reports](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c74dy9jw1q9o?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss) Trump says the US is "not satisfied" with the Iran deal yet.

The Yemen conflict has also taken a tragic turn with [AP News reporting](https://apnews.com/article/yemen-expresident-abdrabbuh-mansour-hadi-died-b3afd7b3679af14d335578e9560c7cd8) the death of former Yemen president Abdrabbuh Mansour Hadi at age 80. The IRG-backed government mourned the loss of the internationally recognized leader who led Yemen during critical years of the conflict. This development adds another layer of complexity to ongoing peace efforts in the war-torn nation.

• [Times of Oman notes](https://timesofoman.com//article/172348-temperature-crosses-475-degrees-in-oman) temperatures crossed 47.5 degrees in Oman, highlighting ongoing climate challenges affecting the energy sector.

## Country Watch

**Saudi Arabia**
The Kingdom is hosting major diplomatic meetings during the Hajj season. Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman met with Chad's president at Mina Palace to [discuss bilateral relations](https://spa.gov.sa/N2602420), according to the Saudi Press Agency. Prince Khalid bin Salman [met with UK National Security Adviser](https://twitter.com/kbsalsaud) Jonathan Powell to review Saudi-British cooperation and regional security coordination. Foreign Minister Prince Faisal bin Farhan [praised France's resolve](https://twitter.com/FaisalbinFarhan) to recognize Palestine and [appreciated Trump's decision](https://twitter.com/FaisalbinFarhan) to give diplomacy a chance to restore Strait of Hormuz security.

**UAE**
Sheikh Mohammed bin Rashid [reviewed UAE tourism sector](https://twitter.com/UAEmediaoffice) achievements for 2025, while the country announced it [leads globally](https://twitter.com/ABZayed) in artificial intelligence talent concentration, growing 121% between 2019-2025. The UAE [strongly condemned](https://wam.ae/en/details/1395302843663) Iranian attacks on Kuwait. Sheikh Tahnoon bin Zayed [met with US Representative](https://twitter.com/hhtbzayed) Josh Gottheimer to discuss UAE-US strategic relations.

**Qatar**
Prime Minister Sheikh Mohammed bin Abdulrahman Al Thani [expressed gratitude](https://twitter.com/MBA_AlThani_) for his appointment by Emir Sheikh Tamim. Qatar's government communications office [extended Eid greetings](https://twitter.com/GCOQatar), emphasizing unity and peace. [Majed Al Ansari denied reports](https://twitter.com/majedalansari) that Qatar offered $12 billion to Iran for a deal, calling them "simply not true" and attempts to sabotage diplomatic efforts.

**Bahrain / Kuwait / Oman**
Bahrain [strongly condemned](https://bit.ly/4dAMr6j) Iranian attacks on Kuwait, according to the Foreign Ministry. [KUNA reports](http://ow.ly/nRv730sW6tK) Iraq also condemned the drone and missile attacks targeting Kuwait. Oman's Sultan Haitham [offered Eid prayers](http://n9.cl/adorv) at Sultan Qaboos Mosque in Sohar. Foreign Minister Badr Albusaidi [held discussions](https://twitter.com/badralbusaidi) with Iran's Deputy FM, reflecting Oman's diplomatic engagement efforts.

**Yemen**
The IRG government mourned [former president Hadi's death](https://apnews.com/article/yemen-expresident-abdrabbuh-mansour-hadi-died-b3afd7b3679af14d335578e9560c7cd8), with Information Minister Al-Eryani calling him a "great fighter." Saudi Ambassador Mohammed AlJabir [announced plans](https://twitter.com/mohdsalj) to visit Sanaa with an Omani delegation to consolidate the truce and support prisoner exchanges. Meanwhile, Houthi spokesman Mohammed Abdulsalam [criticized](https://twitter.com/abdusalamsalah) US maritime actions, saying American warships opened fire "in a hysterical manner" during reconnaissance operations.

## Official Signals

Prince Faisal bin Farhan's statement appreciating Trump's decision to give diplomacy a chance for Strait of Hormuz security signals Saudi Arabia's strong support for peaceful resolution. His praise for France's Palestine recognition also shows the Kingdom's continued backing for Palestinian statehood. Meanwhile, Majed Al Ansari's firm denial of Qatar's alleged financial offer to Iran demonstrates Doha's sensitivity about its mediation role being mischaracterized.

## Coverage Flags

International outlets are significantly ahead of regional press on the Oman-US tensions over Strait of Hormuz tolls, with Reuters breaking the story while local Omani media remains silent. The death of former Yemen president Hadi is being covered more extensively by international wire services than some regional outlets, potentially indicating sensitivity around the topic in certain Gulf capitals.
