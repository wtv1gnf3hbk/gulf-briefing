The death of former Yemen President Abdrabbuh Mansour Hadi at age 80 marks a pivotal moment in Yemeni politics, as the internationally recognized government (IRG) mourns a leader who shepherded the country through its initial transition following the Arab Spring. [AP reports](https://apnews.com/article/yemen-expresident-abdrabbuh-mansour-hadi-died-b3afd7b3679af14d335578e9560c7cd8) that Hadi, who led Yemen from 2012 until being forced from power by the Houthis in 2015, died in exile. The loss comes as multiple US and Iranian officials confirm that Washington and Tehran have reached a tentative nuclear deal that could reshape regional dynamics, though [the BBC reports](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c87qng40wz9o) the agreement still requires Trump administration approval.

The Gulf continues managing multiple diplomatic tensions as the US warns Oman against facilitating tolls for the Strait of Hormuz, according to [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/us-warns-oman-not-engage-facilitating-tolls-strait-hormuz-2026-05-28/). This warning comes during a period of heightened regional uncertainty as various powers navigate the emerging US-Iran agreement framework. Meanwhile, Muslims worldwide have begun the annual Hajj pilgrimage in Saudi Arabia, with [AP News noting](https://apnews.com/article/hajj-pilgrimage-mecca-saudi-arabia-b3bac12f3dec8e927dfd4e1a961572fa) the ceremony is proceeding during sweltering heat and against a backdrop of regional war concerns.

**Technology Leadership**: Sheikh Abdulla bin Zayed's emphasis on the UAE's AI talent growth reflects the federation's strategy to position itself as a regional tech hub, with his [post](https://twitter.com/ABZayed) noting this isn't just technical progress but embodies leadership vision for building human capital.

**Yemen Leadership Transition**: International outlets like AP are providing more comprehensive coverage of Hadi's death and its implications than some regional Arabic-language sources, which focus more on protocol aspects.
