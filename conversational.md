Good afternoon from the Gulf. The region faces another day of escalating regional tensions as Iran targets American forces in Kuwait while major diplomatic initiatives compete for attention in Riyadh.

US forces in Kuwait came under direct Iranian missile attack following American strikes on Iranian radar sites, according to [BBC reports](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/crlpy8n7pr6o). The attack represents a significant escalation in the broader regional conflict, with [AP News confirming](https://apnews.com/article/iran-us-irael-war-kuwait-strikes-88daa9f90b48baaa7beb18e35515c59d) that the US subsequently bombed Iranian military sites in retaliation. Saudi Arabia's Foreign Ministry issued a [strong condemnation](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/crlpy8n7pr6o) of the Iranian attacks on Kuwait, calling them "sinful and repeated" violations of Kuwaiti sovereignty.

The escalation occurs as Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman received calls from both French President Macron and met with Chad's president, while Foreign Minister Prince Faisal bin Farhan [expressed appreciation](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/crlpy8n7pr6o) for President Trump's diplomatic approach to ending regional conflicts. The Kingdom is also dealing with internal security issues, with the Interior Ministry reporting the [arrest of 18 environmental law violators](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/crlpy8n7pr6o) and over 8,000 immigration violators in the past week.

## Energy & Economy

• **Oman's largest fertilizer producer** is approaching an IPO on the Muscat Stock Exchange, according to [Al Sharq with Bloomberg](https://asharqbusiness.com/financial-markets/134867/%D8%A3%D9%83%D8%A8%D8%B1-%D9%85%D9%86%D8%AA%D8%AC-%D8%A3%D8%B3%D9%85%D8%AF%D8%A9-%D9%81%D9%8A-%D8%B9%D9%85%D8%A7%D9%86-%D9%8A%D9%82%D8%AA%D8%B1%D8%A8-%D9%85%D9%86-%D8%B7%D8%B1%D8%AD-%D8%B9%D8%A7%D9%85-%D8%A3%D9%88%D9%84%D9%8A-%D9%81%D9%8A-%D8%A8%D9%88%D8%B1%D8%B5%D8%A9-%D9%85%D8%B3%D9%82%D8%B7/).

• **UAE economic growth** is projected at 6.2% with GDP reaching 1.9 trillion dirhams in 2025, per government media reports.

## Country Watch

### Saudi Arabia
Prince Khalid bin Salman conveyed royal condolences for the death of former Yemeni President Abd Rabbuh Mansour Hadi, while the [Saudi Press Agency](https://spa.gov.sa/N2604388) reported that King Salman promoted 327 Public Prosecution members across various judicial ranks. The first recorded birth of an onager (wild ass) in over 100 years was celebrated at the Prince Mohammed bin Salman Royal Reserve, according to [CNN Arabic](https://arabic.cnn.com/travel/article/2026/06/02/saudi-first-onager-birth-in-over-a-century).

### UAE 
Sheikh Mohammed bin Rashid and Sheikh Mansour bin Zayed attended a government retreat in Abu Dhabi to develop AI-powered government services, while the [UAE Defense Ministry](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/opec-likely-raise-july-oil-output-target-despite-hormuz-disruption-sources-say-2026-06-01/) announced that Major General Muslim Al Rashidi received the US Special Operations Command Medal. Abu Dhabi Gaming partnered with Aspire UAE to advance AI-driven game development, [WAM reports](https://wam.ae/a/bj53fiy).

### Qatar
Prime Minister Mohammed bin Abdulrahman Al Thani was officially appointed by Emir Tamim, with [Qatar News Agency](https://bit.ly/4o28NBb) covering his first statements. The country mourns the death of Abdullah bin Hamad Al Attiyah, described as one of Qatar's loyal contributors to national development.

### Yemen
Saudi Ambassador Mohammed AlJabir announced plans to visit Sanaa with an Omani delegation to consolidate the ceasefire and support prisoner exchanges. The IRG government condemned a UAE airstrike on National Army forces in Aden and Abyan, while [Saba News (IRG)](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/opec-likely-raise-july-oil-output-target-despite-hormuz-disruption-sources-say-2026-06-01/) reported the appointment of Ahmed Hamid Lamlas as Aden governor. Houthi spokesman Mohammed Abdulsalam outlined terms for recent banking and aviation agreements, while STC President Aidarous al-Zubaidi performed Umrah rituals.

## Official Signals

**Saudi Foreign Minister Prince Faisal bin Farhan** posted appreciation for Trump's diplomatic approach: "The Kingdom of Saudi Arabia highly appreciates the US President Donald Trump's decision to give diplomacy a chance to reach an acceptable agreement to end the war, restore the security and freedom of maritime navigation in the Strait of Hormuz."

**UAE National Security Advisor Anwar Gargash** criticized Turkey's defense minister: "The provocative statement of the Turkish Defense Minister represents a new downfall for his country's diplomacy. The logic of the Sublime Porte, the Supreme State, and its decrees has its place in the historical archive."
