Iran launched missile and drone attacks against Kuwait in an escalating regional confrontation, according to [Reuters reports](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/kuwaiti-army-says-air-defences-intercepting-hostile-missile-drone-attacks-2026-05-28/). Kuwait's army said its air defenses were intercepting the hostile attacks, prompting widespread Arab condemnation and expressions of solidarity. The strikes come as US and Iranian negotiators have reportedly reached a tentative ceasefire deal following recent tit-for-tat exchanges, though President Trump expressed skepticism about the arrangement. Former Yemen president Abdrabbuh Mansour Hadi [died at 80](https://apnews.com/article/yemen-expresident-abdrabbuh-mansour-hadi-died-b3afd7b3679af14d335578e9560c7cd8), marking the end of an era for the war-torn country's internationally recognized government (IRG).

The attacks against Kuwait represent a significant escalation in regional tensions following the broader Iran-US conflict. [Al Jazeera reports](https://www.aljazeera.net/news/2026/5/28/%D8%AA%D8%B1%D9%85%D8%A8-%D9%8A%D9%87%D8%AF%D8%AF-%D8%A8%D9%80%D9%86%D8%B3%D9%81-%D8%B9%D9%85%D8%A7%D9%86-%D8%A5%D8%B0%D8%A7-%D8%AF%D8%B9%D9%85%D8%AA-%D8%A5%D9%8A%D8%B1%D8%A7%D9%86) that Trump has threatened to expel Oman if it supports Iran, while the [BBC notes](https://www.bbc.com/arabic/articles/c5y7wd8d0zwo) concerns about Trump's threats toward Oman regarding the Strait of Hormuz. Three oil and LNG tankers [exited Hormuz](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/three-oil-lng-tankers-exit-hormuz-with-transponders-off-2026-05-28/) with transponders turned off, Reuters reports, indicating continued shipping disruptions in the critical waterway.
