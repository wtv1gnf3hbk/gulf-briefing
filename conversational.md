Good afternoon from the Gulf. Tensions are escalating following [US airstrikes on Iranian military sites](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/crlpy8n7pr6o) following Iran's missile attacks on American troops stationed in Kuwait. The BBC reports that the [US struck Iranian radar sites](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/crlpy8n7pr6o) after Iran targeted American forces, marking a dangerous escalation in the Gulf. Simultaneously, [OPEC+ appears likely to raise July oil output targets](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/opec-likely-raise-july-oil-output-target-despite-hormuz-disruption-sources-say-2026-06-01/) despite ongoing disruptions to shipping in the Strait of Hormuz, according to Reuters, as the cartel balances supply concerns against geopolitical risks.

Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman received calls from both French President and Chad's president, according to [Saudi Press Agency reports](https://x.com/spagov). Meanwhile, Qatar is playing an active mediation role, with [Prime Minister Al Thani discussing efforts](https://www.youm7.com/story/2026/6/2/) between Washington and Tehran by telephone with Iran's foreign minister, per Youm7. The kingdom also condemned both Israeli operations in Lebanon and Iranian attacks on Kuwait, with the Foreign Ministry issuing [strong statements](https://x.com/KSAMOFA) against both actions.
