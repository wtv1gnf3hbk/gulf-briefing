The escalation comes as [BBC reports](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/crlpy8n7pr6o) sirens sounded over Kuwait City following the US attacks. Saudi Arabia issued strong statements condemning both Iran's attacks on Kuwait and Israel's incursion into Lebanon, calling for immediate cessation of both conflicts.

The Strait of Hormuz situation remains tense despite diplomatic efforts. President Trump announced that Israel and Hezbollah have agreed to halt mutual attacks, though implementation remains uncertain.
