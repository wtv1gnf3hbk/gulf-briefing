The US and Iran appear close to reaching a tentative agreement that could reshape the Gulf region's security landscape. President Trump indicated yesterday that negotiations are nearing completion but [cautioned that the US](https://apnews.com/article/iran-us-war-oil-may-28-2026-8f5ed2813ba63df7ae9ccbe991688d29) is "not there yet" on finalizing a deal, according to AP News. The [BBC reports that](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c87qng40wz9o?at_medium=RSS&amp;at_campaign=rss) Vice President Vance similarly described the US as "very close" to an agreement. A key component involves restoring freedom of navigation in the Strait of Hormuz to pre-February 2026 levels, as [Qatar News Agency reports](https://ow.ly/u5ns50Z5Mpx) Trump called for free passage through the critical waterway.

The potential Iran deal comes as Saudi Arabia marks the successful conclusion of its 2026 Hajj season. Sultan Haitham of Oman and Emir Sheikh Mishal of Kuwait both [congratulated King Salman](https://spa.gov.sa/N2602764) and Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman on the pilgrimage's organization, per the Saudi Press Agency. Prince Khalid bin Salman emphasized the kingdom's pride in serving the Two Holy Mosques, while [Foreign Minister Prince Faisal](https://twitter.com/FaisalbinFarhan) praised Trump's decision to give diplomacy another chance to resolve regional tensions.

**Energy & Economy**

• Saudi Arabia [may reduce](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/saudi-arabia-may-lower-july-oil-prices-asia-demand-weakens-2026-05-29/) July oil prices to Asia as demand softens, Reuters reports, signaling potential shifts in the kingdom's pricing strategy during a period of regional uncertainty.

• Japan's oil imports [plummeted nearly 66%](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/japans-april-oil-imports-fall-nearly-66-yy-iran-war-disrupts-supply-2026-05-29/) year-over-year in April due to Iran war disruptions, according to Reuters, highlighting the conflict's impact on global energy flows.

• The EU [strongly condemned](https://ow.ly/gJLc30sW7VU) Iran's latest attack on Kuwait, KUNA reports, as regional powers continue to grapple with Tehran's expanding military operations.

• Kuwait's Interior Ministry [arrested suspects](https://ow.ly/eMgs30sW7VH) attempting to smuggle 12 kilograms of methamphetamine, per KUNA, reflecting ongoing security challenges in the region.

**Saudi Arabia**

Saudi Ambassador to Yemen Mohammed al-Jabir announced plans to visit Sana'a with an Omani delegation to advance ceasefire talks and prisoner exchanges. Al-Jabir noted that nearly [2 million Yemenis](https://twitter.com/mohdsalj) work in the kingdom, transferring over $4 billion annually to support 15 million family members back home. The kingdom also celebrated its successful Hajj organization, with Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman [hosting an annual reception](https://spa.gov.sa/N2602369) at the Royal Court in Mina Palace.

**UAE**

Sheikh Mohammed bin Rashid [reviewed the UAE's tourism](https://twitter.com/UAEmediaoffice) sector achievements for 2025, while the country launched the first batch of AI assistants for procurement, taxes, and customer service. Sheikh Abdullah bin Zayed highlighted the UAE's [leading position](https://twitter.com/ABZayed) in AI talent concentration globally, with 121% growth between 2019 and 2025. Emirates Airlines also unveiled a [celebratory A380 livery](https://twitter.com/DXBMediaOffice) dedicated to Arsenal FC following their Premier League victory.

**Oman**

Oman and Iran [reaffirmed their commitment](https://timesofoman.com//article/172358-oman-and-iran-reaffirm-commitment-to-strait-of-hormuz-security) to Strait of Hormuz security, according to Times of Oman. Foreign Minister Badr Albusaidi held [constructive discussions](https://twitter.com/badralbusaidi) with Iran's Deputy FM, emphasizing diplomatic engagement and peaceful regional coexistence. Sultan Haitham performed [Eid prayers](http://n9.cl/adorv) at Sultan Qaboos Mosque in Sohar.

**Yemen**

The internationally recognized government continues to face internal challenges. Presidential Leadership Council member Abu Zaraa [criticized individual decisions](https://twitter.com/AbuZar3a) that have caused divisions within the council, arguing that unilateral decision-making undermines institutional work. IRG Information Minister Mohammed al-Eryani [mourned the death](https://twitter.com/ERYANIM) of former President Abd Rabbuh Mansour Hadi, while Southern Transitional Council's Amr al-Bidh emphasized that political agreements [should not obstruct](https://twitter.com/AmrAlBidh) citizens' lives and livelihoods.

**Official Signals**

Foreign Minister Prince Faisal bin Farhan's statement appreciating Trump's diplomatic approach signals Saudi support for the emerging Iran deal framework. His [emphasis on restoring](https://twitter.com/FaisalbinFarhan) Strait of Hormuz security to pre-February 2026 conditions suggests the kingdom views freedom of navigation as a key benchmark for any agreement's success.

Qatar's Prime Minister Mohammed bin Abdulrahman Al Thani's appointment [announcement demonstrates](https://twitter.com/MBA_AlThani_) continuity in Doha's foreign policy approach, while his Eid greetings reflect regional hopes for renewed stability and prosperity.

**Coverage Flags**

International outlets are leading on the US-Iran negotiations, while regional press focuses heavily on Hajj celebrations and Eid greetings. The gap between English and Arabic coverage is particularly notable regarding the Iran deal's implications for Gulf security arrangements.
