A tentative US-Iran nuclear deal has emerged, with former Yemeni President Abdrabbuh Mansour Hadi's death marking another significant shift in the region's political landscape. Multiple outlets report [initial progress](https://apnews.com/article/iran-us-war-oil-may-28-2026-8f5ed2813ba63df7ae9ccbe991688d29) toward a 60-day ceasefire framework, while [AP News confirms](https://apnews.com/article/yemen-expresident-abdrabbuh-mansour-hadi-died-b3afd7b3679af14d335578e9560c7cd8) Hadi died at 80 following a prolonged struggle with the Houthis and years in Saudi exile. The developments come as Trump administration officials signal cautious optimism about diplomatic momentum, though the president himself says the US is "not satisfied" with current terms.

The Iran negotiations appear to center on restoring Strait of Hormuz navigation security to pre-February 28 levels, according to [Times of Oman reporting](https://timesofoman.com//article/172342-us-iran-reach-tentative-60-day-deal-to-ease-tensions-trumps-approval-pending-axios-report). However, competing claims emerge about implementation details, with US military officials [denying Iranian assertions](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/irans-state-tv-says-us-aircraft-destroyed-irans-bushehr-no-confirmation-us-2026-05-28/) about downing American aircraft near Bushehr. The tentative framework requires Trump's final approval and faces skepticism from regional partners who question Iran's commitment to maritime security guarantees.

• Mohammed bin Rashid reviewed UAE tourism sector achievements for 2025, showing continued economic diversification progress, [according to WAM](https://x.com/UAEmediaoffice).

**Yemen**

• Former President Abdrabbuh Mansour Hadi's death removes a key figure from Yemen's complex political landscape. The IRG government mourned the "great fighter" who led during the early war years against Houthi advances.

• IRG Presidential Leadership Council member Abu Zaraa criticized unilateral decision-making within the council, [stating](https://x.com/AbuZar3a) that individual choices have been "a major cause of division" in institutional work.

• Houthi spokesperson Mohammed Abdulsalam claimed Yemeni naval forces conducted reconnaissance operations in the Red Sea when American warships "opened fire in a hysterical manner," [according to his account](https://x.com/abdusalamsalah).

• Southern Transitional Council President Aidarous Al-Zubaidi performed Umrah rituals in Saudi Arabia, [STC reported](http://stcaden.com/news/10114), while the council declared continued support for southern security operations.

**Official Signals**

Saudi Foreign Minister Prince Faisal bin Farhan's public endorsement of Trump's diplomatic approach signals Riyadh's preference for negotiated solutions over military escalation. His [Twitter statement](https://x.com/FaisalbinFarhan) praising the US decision to "grant negotiations an additional opportunity" suggests Saudi coordination with Washington on Iran policy.

Qatar's firm denial of financial incentives for Iran negotiations through Spokesperson Al Ansari indicates Doha's concern about being portrayed as bankrolling regional deals, particularly given ongoing diplomatic sensitivities with Gulf neighbors.

**Coverage Flags**

International outlets are significantly ahead of regional press on the Iran-US negotiations story, with Reuters and AP providing more detailed sourcing than local agencies. The death of former Yemen President Hadi has received limited English-language coverage compared to its political significance for Yemen's future governance structure.
