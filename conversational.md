## Gulf Briefing - May 30, 2026

**Saudi Arabia** [announced the successful](https://timesofoman.com//article/172355-hm-congratulates-saudi-leadership-for-successful-hajj-season-1447-ah) conclusion of the 1447 AH Hajj season, with [Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman](https://www.spa.gov.sa/N2602369) declaring that Saudi Arabia has been honored to serve the Two Holy Mosques and the pilgrims. The achievement comes during active diplomatic maneuvering, as [Foreign Minister Prince Faisal bin Farhan](https://www.faisalbinfarhan.com) announced Saudi appreciation for President Trump's decision to [give diplomacy](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c74dy9jw1q9o) a chance to reach an acceptable agreement to end the Iran war and restore security in the Strait of Hormuz.

**Oman and Iran** [reaffirmed their commitment](https://timesofoman.com//article/172358-oman-and-iran-reaffirm-commitment-to-strait-of-hormuz-security) to Strait of Hormuz security following high-level diplomatic consultations between Foreign Minister Badr Albusaidi and Iranian Deputy Foreign Minister Gharibabadi. The talks emphasize Oman's continued role as a regional diplomatic bridge as negotiations with Iran reportedly [reach a critical juncture](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c87qng40wz9o), with US Vice President Vance stating the two sides are "very close" to a deal but "not there yet."

- **Japan's oil imports** hit [their lowest level since 1962](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/japans-april-oil-imports-fall-nearly-66-yy-iran-war-disrupts-supply-2026-05-29/) in April, falling nearly 66% year-over-year as the Iran conflict disrupts global supply chains.
- **Kuwait** [arrested suspects attempting to smuggle](http://ow.ly/eMgs30sW7VH) 12 kilograms of methamphetamine, according to KUNA reports.
- **UAE leads globally** in [artificial intelligence talent concentration](https://twitter.com/ABZayed/status/1843035816370802812), with 121% growth between 2019 and 2025, reflecting the Emirates' strategic focus on technology development.
