Looking at the data, I see significant developments unfolding across the region, particularly involving Iran-US diplomatic negotiations and regional responses. Here's today's briefing:

The United States and Iran appear close to reaching a tentative agreement that could pause current hostilities and restart nuclear talks, according to [multiple reports](https://apnews.com/article/iran-us-war-oil-may-28-2026-8f5ed2813ba63df7ae9ccbe991688d29). The [BBC reports](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c87qng40wz9o?at_medium=RSS&amp;at_campaign=rss) Vice President Vance saying the two sides are "very close" to a deal, though Trump has indicated the US is "not satisfied" with the current terms. The potential agreement reportedly includes provisions to restore maritime security in the Strait of Hormuz to pre-February 2026 conditions, though details remain limited.

Regional reactions have been swift and telling. Saudi Foreign Minister Prince Faisal bin Farhan posted that the Kingdom "highly appreciates the US President Donald Trump's decision to give diplomacy a chance to reach an acceptable agreement to end the war, restore the security and freedom of maritime navigation in the Strait of Hormuz." Meanwhile, Oman and Iran have reaffirmed their commitment to Strait of Hormuz security through diplomatic channels, with Omani Foreign Minister Badr Albusaidi meeting with Iran's Deputy Foreign Minister to discuss regional stability.
