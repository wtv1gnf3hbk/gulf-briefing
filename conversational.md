# Gulf News Daily Briefing — Friday, May 29, 2026

Oman and Iran reaffirmed their commitment to Strait of Hormuz security during high-level talks between their foreign ministers, as [Times of Oman reports](https://timesofoman.com//article/172358-oman-and-iran-reaffirm-commitment-to-strait-of-hormuz-security), following President Trump's recent threat to lift a naval blockade of Iranian ports. The diplomatic contact comes after [Reuters reports](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/iran-could-open-strait-hormuz-within-month-if-terms-agreed-state-tv-says-2026-05-27/) US strikes against Iran continued following Trump's denial of an agreement on the strategic waterway. According to [the BBC](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c87qng40wz9o?at_medium=RSS&amp;at_campaign=rss), Vice President Vance said the US and Iran are "very close" to a deal but "not there yet."

Former Yemeni President Abdrabbuh Mansour Hadi, who governed mostly from exile during the country's civil war, [died at age 80](https://apnews.com/article/yemen-expresident-abdrabbuh-mansour-hadi-died-b3afd7b3679af14d335578e9560c7cd8), per AP News. His passing marks the end of a controversial presidency that began in 2012 and was disrupted by Houthi rebels capturing the capital in 2014. Saudi Ambassador to Yemen Mohammed AlJabir announced on Twitter that [Hudaydah is liberated](https://twitter.com/mohdsalj) and Yemen is "breathing," while also confirming plans to visit Sanaa with an Omani delegation to consolidate the truce and ceasefire.
