## Country Watch

**Saudi Arabia**
The kingdom is celebrating a successful Hajj season with congratulations flowing from across the Gulf. Prince Khalid bin Salman expressed gratitude for "serving the Two Holy Mosques and caring for the pilgrims" and thanked security forces and government sectors. Saudi Ambassador to Yemen Mohammed AlJabir announced plans to visit Sanaa with an Omani delegation to "consolidate the truce and ceasefire" and support prisoner exchanges, per his [Twitter post](https://twitter.com/mohdsalj). **UAE**
Emirates unveiled a celebratory A380 livery honoring Arsenal FC following their Premier League victory, according to [Dubai Media Office](https://twitter.com/DXBMediaOffice). **Qatar**
Qatar's leadership sent congratulations to Saudi Arabia on the successful Hajj season, with the Amir, Deputy Amir, and Prime Minister all [sending messages](https://twitter.com/QNAEnglish) to the Custodian of the Two Holy Mosques and Crown Prince. Bahrain strongly condemns an Iranian attack on Kuwait, per the [Foreign Ministry](https://twitter.com/bahdiplomatic). Houthis announced a text agreement to cancel recent banking decisions, resume Yemenia flights, and hold meetings. Abu Zaraa criticized individual decisions within the Presidential Leadership Council, stating they were "a major cause of division."

## Official Signals

Prince Faisal bin Farhan's statement appreciating Trump's decision to "give diplomacy a chance" represents Saudi Arabia's clearest signal yet of support for the emerging Iran deal. His emphasis on restoring Strait of Hormuz navigation "to its state prior to February 28th" provides specific benchmarks for success.

Mohammed AlJabir's announcement of an upcoming Sanaa visit with Omani mediation signals Saudi Arabia's continued commitment to Yemen peace efforts, even as other regional tensions escalate. His mention of "consolidating the truce and ceasefire" suggests existing agreements remain in place despite broader regional instability.
