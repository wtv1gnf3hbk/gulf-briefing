**Top News**

Saudi Arabia is celebrating the successful completion of the 2026 Hajj season as millions of pilgrims completed their rituals. The Saudi Press Agency reports regional leaders have [congratulated King](https://spa.gov.sa/N2603169) Salman and Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman on what the kingdom calls an exceptionally well-organized pilgrimage. Interior Minister Prince Abdulaziz bin Saud met with security sector leaders and Hajj forces to review operations as [Medina received](https://alekhbariyatv) the first pilgrims arriving from Mecca. According to CNN Arabic, [Iranian and Omani](https://arabic.cnn.com/middle-east/article/2026/05/29/first-contact-between-foreign-ministers-of-iran-and-oman-trumps-threat) foreign ministers held their first contact following Trump's recent threats, with Oman's Foreign Minister Badr Albusaidi meeting Iran's Deputy Foreign Minister following what sources describe as mounting regional tensions.

Multiple international outlets report the US and Iran remain in talks over a potential ceasefire deal that could end the current conflict. The BBC reports Trump [ended a](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c0r2d40r91qo?at_medium=RSS&amp;at_campaign=rss) Situation Room meeting without announcing an agreement, while the White House says any deal must "satisfy red lines." According to the AP, [negotiators reached](https://apnews.com/article/iran-us-war-oil-may-28-2026-8f5ed2813ba63df7ae9ccbe991688d29) a tentative deal to extend a ceasefire and start new nuclear talks, though Trump has not yet decided whether to move forward with the arrangement.
