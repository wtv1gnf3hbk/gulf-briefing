# Gulf News Briefing - May 30, 2026

Saudi Arabia's unprecedented disclosure of Hajj-season security statistics reveals the kingdom's effort to demonstrate operational success as it continues positioning itself as guardian of Islam's holy sites. The [Interior Ministry reported](https://spa.gov.sa/N2603513) that over 8,000 residency and border violations were processed in just one week, as more than 1.9 million pilgrims completed this year's Hajj. This transparency appears aimed at showcasing Saudi capabilities while former Yemeni President Abd Rabbuh Mansour Hadi's death marks the end of an era in Yemen's complex political landscape, with [Prince Khalid bin Salman](https://twitter.com/kbsalsaud) conveying condolences from the Saudi leadership.

Meanwhile, Trump's contentious remarks about "neutral" Oman have drawn sharp rebuke from [Times of Oman](https://timesofoman.com/article/172388), calling his statement against the sultanate "not a passing mistake — it is a historical sin." The criticism underscores Oman's sensitivity about its carefully cultivated neutrality in regional conflicts, particularly as it continues diplomatic engagement with Iran.
