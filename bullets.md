**Top News**

- Kuwait said its air defenses intercepted multiple Iranian drone and missile attacks targeting the country, Reuters [reported](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/kuwaiti-army-says-air-defences-intercepting-hostile-missile-drone-attacks-2026-05-28/).
- The US carried out fresh strikes against Iranian military sites and drones, escalating tensions after Iran targeted American bases, according to [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/us-carries-out-new-strikes-iran-against-military-site-official-says-2026-05-27/).
- Iran targeted US bases in response to American strikes, the BBC [reported](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c98r2qy5809o?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss), as President Trump dismissed reports of a Hormuz deal.
- Former Yemeni President Abd Rabbuh Mansour Hadi died, with Saudi Arabia's King Salman and Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman offering [condolences](https://twitter.com/spagov) to the IRG government and Yemeni people.
- Saudi Arabia expressed appreciation for Trump's decision to give diplomacy another chance to end the Iran war and restore Strait of Hormuz security, Foreign Minister Prince Faisal bin Farhan [said](https://twitter.com/FaisalbinFarhan).
- Hajj pilgrims performed rituals in Mecca during soaring heat as Eid al-Adha celebrations began, AP News [reported](https://apnews.com/article/islam-hajj-mina-saudi-arabia-muslim-908f0c90c0f2308496ab90997f78488a).

**Energy & Economy**

- Three oil and LNG tankers exited the Strait of Hormuz with their transponders switched off, Reuters [reported](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/three-oil-lng-tankers-exit-hormuz-with-transponders-off-2026-05-28/).
- Air cargo demand rose 4% in April despite Middle East disruptions, IATA said, according to Times of [Oman](https://timesofoman.com//article/172334-air-cargo-demand-rises-4-in-april-despite-middle-east-disruptions-iata).
