**Top News**

- Iran struck a Kuwaiti power station, killing at least one worker, according to [CNN Arabic](https://arabic.cnn.com/middle-east/article/2026/03/30/worker-killed-in-attack-on-kuwait-power-plant-news-agency-reports).
- Saudi Arabia intercepted five Iranian ballistic missiles targeting the eastern region, the Defense Ministry confirmed on [social media](https://x.com/alekhbariyatv).
- UAE air defenses engaged 16 ballistic missiles and 42 drones from Iran on March 29, bringing total intercepts since the war began to 414 ballistic missiles and 15 cruise missiles, the Defense Ministry [reported](https://x.com/modgovae).
- Six people were injured in Abu Dhabi after a missile was intercepted near the KEZAD economic zone, Reuters [confirmed](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/five-injured-abu-dhabi-after-missile-intercepted-near-kezad-authorities-say-2026-03-28/).
- Houthis launched a missile attack on Israel, stoking fears of renewed Red Sea shipping strikes, AP News [reported](https://apnews.com/article/iran-israel-us-houthis-yemen-dba2e2e2309f08547a3cbfdc2c367897).
- Pakistan hosted regional talks on Iran with Saudi Arabia, Egypt, and Turkey expressing support for US-Iran peace negotiations, Bloomberg [said](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-03-29/pakistan-says-saudi-egypt-turkey-support-us-iran-peace-talks).

**Energy & Economy**

- Gulf stock markets fell on fears of broader Iran conflict escalation, Reuters [noted](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/most-gulf-markets-ease-fears-broader-iran-conflict-2026-03-29/).
- Crude oil and LNG supplies face "worst-possible scenario" risks from the ongoing Middle East conflict, Reuters energy analysts [warned](https://www.reuters.com/markets/commodities/crude-oil-lng-supply-are-risk-worst-possible-scenario-2026-03-30/).
- Saudi crude shipped to Pakistan in a rare transit move through regional shipping lanes, Bloomberg's Hormuz [tracker showed](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-03-29/hormuz-tracker-saudi-crude-heads-to-pakistan-in-rare-transit).
- Qatar Central Bank implemented protective measures for the financial system during the regional crisis, according to [Al Arabiya](https://www.alarabiya.net/aswaq/economy/2026/03/30/%D9%85%D8%B5%D8%B1%D9%81-%D9%82%D8%B7%D8%B1-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%B1%D9%83%D8%B2%D9%8A-%D9%8A%D8%AA%D8%AE%D8%B0-%D9%85%D8%AC%D9%85%D9%88%D8%B9%D8%A9-%D9%85%D9%86-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D8%AF%D8%A7%D8%A8%D9%8A%D8%B1-%D9%84%D8%AD%D9%85%D8%A7%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%86%D8%B8%D8%A7%D9%85-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%8A).

**Country Watch**

**Saudi Arabia**
- Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman received calls from the King of Spain, Uzbekistan's President, and Mauritania's President to discuss regional military escalation, the Foreign Ministry [posted](https://x.com/KSAMOFA).
- Defense Minister Prince Khalid bin Salman met with French Armed Forces Minister Catherine Vautrin and condemned repeated Iranian attacks on the Kingdom, he [announced](https://x.com/kbsalsaud).

**UAE**
- Federal authorities in Dubai confirmed that loud sounds across the city resulted from successful air defense interceptions, the Interior Ministry [stated](https://x.com/moiuae).
- Deputy Prime Minister Sheikh Mansour bin Zayed chaired a Cabinet meeting praising the defensive spirit of armed forces during the Iranian attacks, Sheikh Mohammed bin Rashid [said](https://x.com/HHShkMohd).

**Qatar**
- Prime Minister Sheikh Mohammed bin Abdulrahman Al Thani condemned Iranian attacks on Qatar as "rejected and unjustified under any pretext," he [wrote](https://x.com/MBA_AlThani_).
- Schools resumed full in-person classes with enhanced security guidelines following the regional crisis, the Government Communications Office [confirmed](https://x.com/GCOQatar).

**Kuwait**
- National Guard forces shot down one drone and four additional drones during Iranian attacks, Al Ekhbariya TV [reported](https://x.com/alekhbariyatv).

**Bahrain**
- The Foreign Ministry condemned Iranian attacks on Kuwait in an official statement, the diplomatic service [said](https://x.com/bahdiplomatic).
- Air raid sirens sounded across the country with authorities urging citizens to seek shelter, the Interior Ministry [announced](https://x.com/moi_bahrain).

**Oman**
- Emergency supplies were delivered to isolated villages in Wadi Bani Khalid following recent weather disruptions, Times of Oman [reported](https://timesofoman.com//article/170042-emergency-supplies-delivered-to-isolated-villages-in-wadi-bani-khalid).
- Foreign Minister Badr Albusaidi told the EU Special Representative that the US and Iran have "strong shared interest" in ending the war, he [posted](https://x.com/badralbusaidi).

**Yemen**
- Houthis joined Iran's war with attacks against Israel, raising concerns about Red Sea shipping disruption, the BBC [said](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cd6l5n8jv4yo?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss).
- Saudi envoy Mohammed Al-Jadaan announced plans to visit Sanaa with an Omani delegation to consolidate the truce and discuss prisoner exchanges, he [stated](https://x.com/mohdsalj).

**Official Signals**

- UAE Minister of State Anwar Gargash criticized Turkey's Defense Minister for "provocative statements" representing "a new downfall for his country's diplomacy," he [tweeted](https://x.com/AnwarGargash).
- Houthi spokesperson Mohammed Abdulsalam confirmed Yemen's naval operations aim to support Palestinians and warned that expanding the conflict would bring consequences, he [said](https://x.com/abdusalamsalah).
