**Top News**
- Iranian forces launched a coordinated drone strike on Kuwait International Airport, targeting fuel depots and causing fires, Kuwait Civil Aviation [reported](https://timesofoman.com//article/170131-kuwait-civil-aviation-iranian-drones-target-kuwait-international-airports-fuel-depots). Iranian attacks also hit a Kuwaiti oil tanker in Dubai port area, Bloomberg [reported](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-03-30/iran-strikes-fully-laden-kuwait-oil-tanker-in-dubai-port-area).
- Bahrain Civil Defence extinguished a fire at a company facility caused by Iranian aggression, the Interior Ministry [confirmed](https://twitter.com/moi_bahrain), while sirens sounded warning citizens to seek shelter.
- Saudi air defenses intercepted 12 drones and 8 missiles Tuesday, with debris from intercepted drones causing minor injuries and property damage in Al-Kharj Governorate, Civil Defense [said](https://twitter.com/SaudiDCD). UAE air defenses engaged 8 ballistic missiles, 4 cruise missiles and 36 UAVs on March 31, the Defense Ministry [reported](https://twitter.com/modgovae).
- The US warned Americans in Saudi Arabia to shelter in place after unspecified threats, Reuters [reported](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/us-warns-americans-saudi-arabia-shelter-place-after-threats-2026-03-31/).
- OPEC oil output plunged in March as war forced export cuts, a Reuters [survey found](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/opec-oil-output-plunges-march-war-forces-export-cuts-reuters-survey-finds-2026-03-31/).
- Arab nations face potential losses of $200 billion from the Iran war, according to a UN [study](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-03-31/arab-nations-may-lose-200-billion-from-iran-war-un-study-finds) cited by Bloomberg.

**Energy & Economy**
- The Aramco-Dow joint venture Sadara Chemical halted production due to regional turmoil, Reuters [reported](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/aramco-dow-joint-venture-sadara-chemical-halts-production-over-middle-east-2026-03-31/), with Bloomberg describing the [shutdown](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-03-31/saudi-chemical-plant-shuts-down-as-war-disrupts-supply-chains) as disrupting supply chains.
- Abu Dhabi's 2PointZero made a $2.25 billion investment in a US gas deal, Bloomberg [reported](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-03-31/abu-dhabi-s-2pointzero-splashes-out-2-25-billion-in-us-gas-deal).
- Gulf stocks showed mixed results on reports that Trump is weighing an end to the Iran war, Reuters [reported](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/gulf-stocks-gain-report-trump-weighing-end-iran-war-2026-03-31/).

**Country Watch**

**Saudi Arabia**
- Saudi Foreign Minister Prince Faisal bin Farhan co-chaired an international conference in partnership with France on Palestinian statehood and the two-state solution, his office [announced](https://twitter.com/FaisalbinFarhan). The Foreign Ministry issued a [statement](https://twitter.com/rbalsaud) reaffirming that Saudi Arabia's position on establishing a Palestinian state "is firm and unwavering."
- Prince Khalid bin Salman met with British Defense Minister John Healey to discuss the strategic defense partnership and condemned Iran's continued attacks on the Kingdom, he [said](https://twitter.com/kbsalsaud).

**UAE**
- UAE officials praised the defensive spirit of armed forces and patriotic spirit of citizens during regional tensions, with Sheikh Mohammed bin Rashid noting efficient institutional work under Mohammed bin Zayed's leadership, he [posted](https://twitter.com/HHShkMohd).
- Sheikh Abdulla bin Zayed called for international efforts to end the Iran war and hold those responsible accountable, marking one month since the conflict began, he [wrote](https://twitter.com/ABZayed).

**Qatar**
- Qatar's Prime Minister discussed regional developments with Saudi Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman and UAE President Mohammed bin Zayed, stressing the need for coordination to protect regional security, he [said](https://twitter.com/MBA_AlThani_).
- Qatar's Foreign Ministry spokesman called for reducing escalation and rejected steps threatening regional security and stability, Dr. Majed Al-Ansari [confirmed](https://twitter.com/majedalansari).

**Bahrain**
- Bahrain condemned Iranian attacks on Kuwait in the strongest terms, the Foreign Ministry [stated](https://twitter.com/bahdiplomatic).

**Oman**
- Oman's Foreign Minister participated in a GCC-Russia-Jordan ministerial meeting, expressing concerns about war's humanitarian impact, Badr Al-Busaidi [said](https://twitter.com/badralbusaidi). He argued the war is "not of Iran's making" and emphasized Oman's efforts for Strait of Hormuz safe passage arrangements.

**Yemen (IRG)**
- Yemen's Ambassador to Saudi Arabia Mohammed Al-Jabir announced Al-Hudaydah had been liberated and declared "Yemen is breathing," he [posted](https://twitter.com/mohdsalj). He also announced plans to visit Sana'a with an Omani delegation to consolidate the truce and discuss prisoner exchanges.
- Presidential Council member Abu Zaraa criticized unilateral decision-making within the council as causing division, he [wrote](https://twitter.com/AbuZar3a).

**Yemen (Houthis)**
- Houthi spokesman Mohammed Abdulsalam defended Yemen's naval operations as supporting Palestinians, not challenging anyone, and warned that those seeking to expand the conflict must bear consequences, he [stated](https://twitter.com/abdusalamsalah).

**Yemen (STC)**
- STC President Aidarous Al-Zubaidi performed Umrah rituals, his office [announced](https://twitter.com/AidrosAlzubidi), while Deputy President Amr Al-Bidh addressed European diplomats about southern Yemen's situation.

**Official Signals**
- The UAE Cabinet approved over 120 international agreements in energy, investment, and diplomatic fields, expanding the country's global partnerships, Sheikh Mohammed bin Rashid [announced](https://twitter.com/HHShkMohd).
- Saudi Arabia's Foreign Ministry welcomed the UN Human Rights Council's unanimous adoption of a resolution on Iranian attacks against the Kingdom and Bahrain, Princess Reema [posted](https://twitter.com/rbalsaud).

**Coverage Flags**
- Multiple Gulf states activated air defense systems and issued shelter-in-place warnings, indicating coordinated Iranian assault across the region requiring urgent diplomatic attention.
