Gulf Bureau Briefing

Iranian drone attacks struck Kuwait International Airport's fuel depots overnight, igniting fires and marking a dangerous escalation in the regional conflict. Kuwait Civil Aviation confirmed the targeting of fuel tanks, according to the Times of Oman. Multiple Arabic outlets including Al Arabiya reported the airport fuel tank fires resulted from Iranian aggression.

The regional conflict is also forcing major industrial shutdowns. The Aramco-Dow joint venture Sadara Chemical halted production as war disrupts supply chains, Reuters reports. Bloomberg separately reported that Iran struck a fully laden Kuwait oil tanker in Dubai's port area. The US has warned Americans in Saudi Arabia to shelter in place following threats, per Reuters.

Energy & Economy

• OPEC oil output plunged in March as war forces export cuts, according to a Reuters survey.

• Abu Dhabi's 2PointZero splashed out $2.25 billion in a US gas deal, Bloomberg reports.

• Arab nations may lose $200 billion from the Iran war, a UN study finds, per Bloomberg.

• Gulf stocks were mixed on reports Trump is weighing ending the Iran war, Reuters reports.

Saudi Arabia

Saudi air defenses continue intercepting Iranian attacks. The Civil Defense reported shrapnel damage from intercepted drones in Al-Kharj Governorate caused minor injuries and property damage. Al Ekhbariya TV reported Saudi defenses intercepted 12 drones and 8 missiles on Tuesday alone.

UAE

The UAE Defense Ministry said air defenses engaged 8 ballistic missiles, 4 cruise missiles and 36 UAVs on March 31. Iran also attacked oil tankers after Trump appeared to waver on war escalation, Bloomberg reports.

Kuwait

Beyond the airport attack, Bahrain's Foreign Ministry condemned the Iranian attacks targeting Kuwait. The strikes represent a significant escalation against GCC infrastructure.

Bahrain

Bahrain's Civil Defense extinguished fires at company facilities resulting from Iranian aggression. Air raid sirens sounded, with authorities urging residents to seek shelter.

Yemen

The internationally recognized government's Foreign Minister Al-Eryani declared that Hodeidah is liberated, with Saudi Yemen envoy Mohammed AlJabir celebrating the development. The IRG continues operations against Houthi forces while the Ansar Allah movement maintains its maritime campaign in support of Gaza.

Official Signals

Prince Khalid bin Salman met with British Defense Minister John Healey, condemning Iran's continued attacks on the Kingdom and discussing regional security implications. UAE Foreign Minister Anwar Gargash called for intensified coordination to protect regional security following escalating Iranian attacks. Qatar's Prime Minister emphasized the need for peaceful solutions to contain regional escalation during meetings with Saudi and UAE leadership.

Coverage Flags

International outlets are ahead on economic impact stories, particularly Bloomberg's comprehensive coverage of industrial shutdowns and financial losses from the conflict.
