# Gulf Bureau Daily Briefing

May 31, 2026

Saudi Arabia welcomed the completion of this year's Hajj pilgrimage as nearly two million pilgrims departed Mecca following the farewell circumambulation of the Kaaba. The kingdom's extensive security and logistical preparations for the 1447 AH season concluded successfully, with Prince Khalid bin Salman expressing gratitude for the opportunity to serve the Two Holy Mosques and care for pilgrims. The Saudi Interior Ministry reported arresting 8,090 violators of residency, work, and border security regulations over the past week, demonstrating continued enforcement efforts during the pilgrimage period.

Regional diplomatic activity intensified as former Yemeni President Abd Rabbuh Mansour Hadi passed away, prompting condolences from across the Gulf. Saudi Defense Minister Prince Khalid bin Salman conveyed leadership condolences to Hadi's family, describing him as a wise leader committed to Yemen's security and prosperity. The death of the internationally-recognized former president comes during ongoing diplomatic efforts to resolve the Yemen crisis.

• The UAE economy showed strong growth with the government announcing 6.2% expansion and GDP rising to AED 1.9 trillion in 2025, reflecting continued economic diversification efforts.

• Qatar's stock exchange resumed official trading today after the Eid al-Adha holiday, according to Mubasher Info.

• Saudi Arabia is reportedly looking to raise 3 billion riyals from offering 30% of Mutlaq Al-Ghuwairi on the stock exchange, per Al-Sharq Economy with Bloomberg.

• Dubai Electricity and Water Authority announced commissioning 1,994 new 11kV substations in 2025, a 30% increase from the previous year.

Saudi Arabia

Prince Faisal bin Farhan led Saudi participation in an international conference for the peaceful settlement of the Palestinian issue in partnership with France. The foreign minister commended France's decision to recognize Palestine, saying it "bolsters the path to a just and enduring peace." Separately, Saudi Arabia's ambassador to Yemen Mohammed Al-Jabir announced plans to visit Sanaa with an Omani delegation to consolidate the truce and support prisoner exchanges as part of comprehensive political dialogue.

UAE

Dubai launched new artificial intelligence initiatives during a government retreat, with Sheikh Mohammed bin Rashid announcing the first batch of AI assistants for procurement, taxes, customer happiness, and technical support. The ruler emphasized the goal of transforming half of government services and operations through artificial intelligence. Meanwhile, Tahnoon bin Zayed met with US Congressman Josh Gottheimer to discuss strengthening UAE-US cooperation in various fields.

Qatar

Qatar saw significant political developments as Mohammed bin Abdulrahman Al Thani expressed honor at being appointed Prime Minister by Emir Sheikh Tamim bin Hamad Al Thani. The new PM said he looks forward to working with fellow ministers in serving the nation under the Emir's wise leadership. Additionally, Majed Al Ansari was appointed as advisor to the Deputy Prime Minister and official spokesman for the Ministry of Foreign Affairs.

Bahrain / Kuwait / Oman

Bahrain's civil defense successfully rescued five children from a house fire in the Aali area without injuries, according to the Interior Ministry. In Kuwait, the first groups of Hajj pilgrims returned to a joyful atmosphere at Kuwait Airways' T4 terminal. Oman's Foreign Minister Badr Albusaidi conducted good discussions with Iran's Deputy Foreign Minister, reflecting the constructive nature of Oman-Iran dialogue and reaffirming diplomatic engagement efforts.

Yemen

The IRG condemned a UAE air strike on National Army forces in Aden and Abyan, with the government calling it interference in Yemeni affairs. Ahmed Hamid Lamlas was appointed governor of Aden Governorate according to Saba News Agency. In Houthi-controlled areas, Mohammed Abdulsalam announced text of an agreement to cancel recent decisions against banks on both sides and resume Yemenia Airlines flights between Sanaa and Jordan, with plans to increase to three daily flights.
